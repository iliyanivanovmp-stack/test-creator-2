What the Sources Show

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Bundle-led traffic promises The AHA Peel and The Toner with 14% korting, but the destination opens on a single-unit Toner buy box and places the matching bundle lower on the page.
  • The strongest message match sends collagen and Matrixyl claims to The Rich Cream, whose benefit list repeats those terms.
  • Anti-aging discovery traffic reaches a quiz with five answer buttons and 20+ years of clinical experience, but no product, price, reviews, or repeated claims.

Google Ads

  • Search creative uses doctor authority, prestige, and older-skin framing instead of the discount and bundle language used in paid-social creative.
  • A doctor-recommendation angle is paired with a 4-star and 168-review badge.
  • No visible discount or bundle offer appears across the reviewed Google units.

Reviews and UGC

  • Customers describe visible skin changes within three days and one week.
  • Repeat-purchase language spans more than two years, with named praise for The Rich Cream and The Toner.
  • Price is the recurring complaint, and multiple reviewers ask for more frequent promotions.
  • LABAREAU directs customers to the newsletter because promotions are announced there first.

PageSpeed and Core Web Vitals

  • The homepage measured 52/100 on mobile, with 5.2s LCP, 480ms TBT, and 3.6s FCP.
  • The Rich Cream PDP measured 38/100, with 19.7s LCP, 790ms TBT, and 4.6s FCP.
  • The PDP LCP was roughly four times Google's 2.5-second poor threshold.

Business Context

  • Site conversion held at 2.5%, matching pre-giveaway performance, while AOV was slightly higher.
  • CTR fell 36% and CPC rose 43% after the giveaway period, producing a 27% revenue-per-click decline from €14.49 to €10.56.
  • Discount share of gross revenue rose from 12% to 20.5% while volume halved.

Site Screenshots

  • The captured homepage uses two stacked banners before navigation and repeats doctor, Trustpilot, press, before-and-after, and testimonial proof through the page.
  • The captured collection showed 24 products with visual stars but no printed counts. A September 11 live check found printed ratings and counts, so that gap now requires measurement rather than a new variation.
  • The captured cart drawer contains two line items, a free-sample module, checkout, and payment icons, with no guarantee, returns, or shipping-time copy.

Social and Community Research

  • A giveaway post reached 61,208 views and 459 likes, the highest engagement in the 30-day research window.
  • The live homepage still carried the giveaway site-wide on September 11.
  • Trustpilot and Instagram reel content could not be collected and remain research gaps.

Competitor Research

  • Labareau's The Rich Cream was listed at €119, versus about €265 for Augustinus Bader's product with the same name.
  • Augustinus Bader and Dr. Barbara Sturm use doctor-created clinical positioning at two to three times Labareau's price point.
  • The pricing gap leaves room to make clinical authority more specific without relying only on promotions.

Below the grid: a "No promises. Just proof." section header with subcopy "Your best skin ever," leading into a before/after image carousel (4 split-image tiles showing forehead/face comparisons).
**Fold 3:** Continuation of before/after content into four written testimonial cards with day-count labels (Day 1/Day 30, Day 1/Day 60) and headlines like "A Transformation I Can See," "I love the LABAREAU Glow," "Beyond Expectations," "Finally, Skincare That Delivers," each with a short Dutch-language customer quote. Below that, a full-width dark science/ingredient section: "The most powerful native collagen in the market," subcopy "Patented SAXZ12 technology, 3 moleculair hyaluronic acid, peptides," with a "How Labareau works in the skin" text link, set against a molecular/lab-glassware background image. The fold ends at the start of a "Meet The Founder" section with a model/founder portrait.
**CTA behavior:** Primary hero CTA ("SHOP NOW") is a static, non-sticky button embedded in the hero — no sticky header or persistent CTA bar observed across the three captured folds.
**Trust signals:** Trustpilot rating displayed above the fold on the hero ("Uitstekend 4.7 uit 5 ★ Trustpilot") alongside a "Recommended by doctors" checkmark line. Individual product tiles in fold 2 show star ratings (visual stars, no review count printed on the tiles themselves). Press/media logos appear twice in the captured folds (fold 2 strip). Before/after imagery and written testimonials serve as social proof in folds 2-3. No guarantee or shipping-promise copy visible in the three captured folds.
**Layout notes:** A floating "Beloningen" (Rewards) pill button is pinned to the bottom-left corner across all three folds. A "Hulp nodig?" (Need help?) chat widget is pinned bottom-right across all three folds. The top of the page carries two stacked banners (rotating announcement + static gift-threshold bar) before the main nav, adding vertical space before the hero is reached.

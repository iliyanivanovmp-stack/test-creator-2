# Meta Ads Visual Summary

Note on collection: Ad #2 lands directly on the PDP for The Rich Cream — the "landing page" folds for Ad #2 below are the actual PDP, so no separate PDP screenshots were captured. Ad #3 lands on a landing page with only one fold (no fold 2/3 exists).

## Ad 1
**Creative:** Static product image, Dutch copy. Headline offer: "Nieuwe actie: Bestel de AHA Peel & The Toner en ontvang 14% korting!" (New promo: order the AHA Peel & The Toner and get 14% off). Bullet list: "Zichtbaar vernieuwde huidstructuur," "Porie-verfijnend effect," "Verhoogde opname van skincare," "Directe glow…". Product image shows a "Toner & Collagen Duo" bundle with a red/gold price tag graphic. Footer copy: "Eén stap. Zichtbaar verschil." / "New Product" tag. CTA button: "Shop Now."
**Landing Page Fold 1:** Hero shows The Toner (200ml) product shot on white background. Above it, a top announcement bar rotates ("Proven results for healthier skin") and a persistent red banner reads "THE TONER 100ML CADEAU · BIJ €100+" (gift threshold). Nav nor breadcrumbs show "PRODUCTEN" as active. Price: €49,00 (single unit), star rating with "1141 Reviews," a social-proof line ("Carice van Houten, Rose Bertram en 100k+ anderen gebruiken LABAREAU"), and a "Doe de test" (take the quiz) link circled/highlighted in the screenshot. Below: 4 numbered benefit bullets and a "ZICHTBARE RESULTATEN" (visible results) checklist. Bundle/bespaar (bundle & save) options begin below the fold with a single-unit radio selected.
**Landing Page Fold 2:** Continuation of bundle options ("The Toner & The AHA Peel" -14%, product-tile layout showing per-item pricing). Primary CTA "IN WINKELWAGEN - €49,00" (add to cart) in a full-width red bar. Below CTA: delivery-cutoff reassurance line, skin-type suitability line, a loyalty-points callout ("Verdien 49 punten…"), and a row of payment-method icons (iDEAL, Amex, Apple Pay, Bancontact, Mastercard, PayPal, Shop, Visa, Klarna). Below that: accordion sections (Skin Benefits, Application, Description, Ingredients) all collapsed, followed by a "Loved by 100.000+ clients" UGC video carousel.
**Landing Page Fold 3:** Continuation of the UGC video row, then a press-logo strip (Vogue, Elegance, Grazia, The Times, Talkies, LINDA), then an editorial-style section titled "The Toner" with a model close-up image and descriptive copy about lactic acid/salicylic acid exfoliation, ending in a sticky bottom bar showing the product, price (€49,00), a size selector (200ml), and an "IN WINKELWAGEN" button.
**LP CTA:** "IN WINKELWAGEN" (add to cart), red full-width button, appears both inline (fold 2) and as a persistent sticky bottom bar (fold 3) on mobile.
**LP trust signals:** Review count (1141 Reviews) and star rating directly under the price, above the fold. Celebrity/social-proof line ("100k+ anderen gebruiken LABAREAU") also above the fold. "Vrij van parabenen," "Dierproefvrij," "FSC-gecertificeerde verpakking" badges below the product image. Press logos (Vogue, Elegance, etc.) present lower on the page. No explicit guarantee/returns copy visible in the captured folds.
**Message Match:** The ad promotes a specific bundle offer ("AHA Peel & The Toner, 14% korting"), but the landing page opens on a single-unit Toner PDP with a different bundle discount structure (Toner & AHA Peel shown at -14% further down, not immediately visible). The exact "14% korting" language from the ad is not echoed verbatim above the fold on the landing page.

## Ad 2
**Creative:** Testimonial-style copy: "Geen zin meer in crèmes die niks doen?" (Tired of creams that do nothing?). Bullet claims: "Bevat premium collageen en Matrixyl® die dieper gaan dan oppervlakkige hydratatie," "Pakt zichtbaar de lijnen aan rond je ogen en mond," "Herstelt elasticiteit, zodat je huid weer als jóuw huid voelt…". Image shows a before/after-style face closeup with "Echte huid, Echte rimpels, Echte resultaten" text overlay next to product shots. Footer: "LABAREAU.NL / Geliefd door duizenden, klinisch bewezen effectief" (Loved by thousands, clinically proven effective). CTA: "Shop Now."
**Landing Page Fold 1 (= PDP):** This ad lands directly on the PDP for "The Rich Cream." Same top banner/gift-threshold bar as Ad 1. Price €119,00, "68 VERKOCHT IN HET AFGELOPEN UUR" (68 sold in the last hour) urgency badge, star rating with "2382 Reviews," same celebrity social-proof line, "Doe de test" link circled. Three award badges shown next to the product image (Womanshome Skin Awards 2025 Winner, Dutch Beauty Awards Nominee, Beauty Awards Finalist). Benefit checklist covers anti-aging claims (diepe rimpels, hydratatie, Matrixyl Morphomics, collageen, multifunctionele verzorging) and a "ZICHTBARE RESULTATEN" 6-week results list.
**Landing Page Fold 2:** Bundle/bespaar options (Rich Cream + Toner, Rich Cream + Rich Cream bundle-style tiles), sticky-style "IN WINKELWAGEN - €119,00" CTA bar, gift-threshold reminder, delivery/skin-suitability reassurance lines, loyalty points, payment icons, collapsed accordions (Skin Benefits, Application, Ingredients, Description), and start of "Loved by 100.000+ clients" UGC video carousel.
**Landing Page Fold 3:** Continuation of the UGC carousel, four icon-led benefit tiles (Matrixyl Morphomics, Native collagen, Deep Hydration, Clinically Proven — shown in English, inconsistent with the rest of the Dutch-language page), a press-logo strip (partially different set than Ad 1's: Vogue, Elegance, Grazia, de Bijenkorf, Talkies, SKINS Cosmetics), a "No promises. Just proof." section header, and the start of a before/after image gallery.
**LP CTA:** "IN WINKELWAGEN" red button, appears inline and as part of the bundle module; no distinct sticky bar was visible in the captured folds for this PDP (unlike Ad 1's fold 3).
**LP trust signals:** Review count (2382 Reviews) and rating above the fold, celebrity social-proof line, three award badges next to the product image, "Doe de test" skin-quiz link, and the same three certification badges (paraben-free, cruelty-free, FSC packaging) below the image. Before/after gallery and UGC testimonial videos appear further down.
**Message Match:** The ad's core claim — premium collagen, Matrixyl®, targeting lines around eyes/mouth, elasticity — matches the PDP's benefit list closely (Matrixyl Morphomics, Huididentiek Collageen, anti-aging crème copy). This is the strongest message match of the three ads, since the ad literally sends traffic to the exact product it describes.

## Ad 3
**Creative:** Testimonial-quote format: "'Ik ben gestopt met zoeken naar het 'perfecte' anti-aging product. Dit is 'm.' — Claire D." Bullet claims: "Geavanceerde technologie voor zichtbare resultaten," "Pakt fijne lijntjes bij de kern aan," "Hydrateert intens en versterkt de huidbarrière," "Herstelt een jeugdige glow…". Image shows a product lineup (cream, serum-style bottles) with an overlay reading "Waarom werkt je skincare niet?" (Why isn't your skincare working?). Footer: "LABAREAU.COM / Zichtbaar minder fijne lijntjes." CTA: "Learn More."
**Landing Page Fold 1 (only fold — single-fold page):** A quiz-style interstitial page, not a standard product landing page. Headline: "In minder dan 2 minuten ontdek je welke anti-aging skincare het beste werkt voor jouw huid:" (In under 2 minutes discover which anti-aging skincare works best for your skin). Sub-question: "Wat frustreert je het meest aan skincare die je tot nu toe hebt geprobeerd?" (What frustrates you most about skincare you've tried so far?) with 5 selectable answer options (e.g. "Ik zie geen écht verschil," "Alles voelt oppervlakkig, geen dieptewerking"). Below the quiz card, a dark maroon panel states "Ontdek in max 2 minuten welke routine het beste past voor jouw huid. Gebaseerd op 20+ jaar klinische ervaring" with a "Shop LABAREAU" link. No product imagery, pricing, or reviews are visible on this page.
**Landing Page Fold 2:** Not collected — page has only one fold.
**Landing Page Fold 3:** Not collected — page has only one fold.
**LP CTA:** No add-to-cart or "Shop Now" button visible in the captured fold; the only interactive elements are the 5 quiz-answer buttons and a "Shop LABAREAU" text link at the bottom of the maroon panel.
**LP trust signals:** Only trust signal present is the "20+ jaar klinische ervaring" (20+ years clinical experience) line in the bottom panel. No review count, star rating, guarantee, or shipping promise visible.
**Message Match:** The ad promises visible results and a "perfect anti-aging product" discovery narrative; the landing page delivers a skin-quiz funnel rather than a product page or the specific claims from the ad (Matrixyl, huidbarrière, etc. are not mentioned on this single fold). Whether the quiz eventually routes to a matching product recommendation cannot be assessed from a single-fold capture.

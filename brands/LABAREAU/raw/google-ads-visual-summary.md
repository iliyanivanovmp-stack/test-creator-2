# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png

**Ads visible:** At least 11 distinct ad units across both screenshots, spanning multiple formats — Shopping/product listing ads (image + price tiles, e.g. "Labareau | The Rich Cream," "Labareau | The B[B] Filter | Luxury...," "Labareau | The Glow Getter | 10..."), Display/video ads (a dark video-style creative and a product-pair image ad), Responsive Search Ads (headline + description text blocks, e.g. "Labareau Glow Getter," "Labareau," "Labareau - Aangeraden Door Artsen"), a branded "Open" app/site-link-style ad, and two Discovery/Performance Max-style content cards with bullet-list callouts ("Gladde en strakke huid," "Krachtige Serums," etc.). All units are labeled "LABAREAU B.V. / Verified" beneath them, and most show a "Gesponsord" (Sponsored) tag.

**Headline themes:** Recurrent angles are: (1) luxury/prestige skincare positioning ("Labareau | The B[B] Filter | Luxury..."), (2) results-and-glow language ("Ontdek de luxe huidverzorgingsproducten van Labareau," "Labareau Glow Getter — Een intensieve glow-boost"), (3) authority/medical endorsement — one search ad headline reads "Labareau - Aangeraden Door Artsen" (Recommended by Doctors) with body copy "Wereldwijd aanbevolen door artsen, dermatologen en chirurgen. Nu met gratis verzending. Klinisch geteste en award-winning skincare. Probeer het nu! Natuurlijke Ingrediënten," tagged with sitelinks "Gezichtsverzorging," "Anti-Aging Oplossingen," (4) age-specific anti-aging framing — one ad headline: "dagcreme voor oudere huid - Liftend huidverjongend effect" (day cream for older skin — lifting/rejuvenating effect), and (5) a plain branded/navigational ad: "LABAREAU | Officiële website | Online Winkelen ... meer dan 20 jaar klinische ervaring ... klinische formules, mogelijk gemaakt door SAXZ-12."

**Offers and CTAs:** "[Prijs]" (price) placeholders appear on multiple Shopping tiles rather than a fixed number, suggesting dynamic pricing rendering. No explicit discount/promo code is visible in either screenshot (unlike the Meta ads, which show an active 14% bundle discount and a free-gift-at-€100 threshold). CTAs are largely implicit (clicking the ad title/image); one unit has an explicit "Open" button.

**Visual approach:** Shopping tiles use clean product-on-white photography consistent with the site's red/gold packaging. The video/display ad uses a darker, moodier black background with a single product highlighted — a different visual register than the bright, white-background Shopping tiles and the site itself. Two Discovery-style cards use a light beige/cream background with a bullet checklist format (partially cut off in the screenshot, but visible items include "Gladde en strakke huid," "Krachtige Serums," "Gezichtsverzorging," "Anti-Aging Oplossingen," "97% ziet huidverbetering," "Liftend huidverjongend effect," "Over Ons," "Krachtige Serums"). One search ad shows a star rating: "Beoordeling voor labareau.com ★★★★☆ (168)" under the "dagcreme voor oudere huid" headline.

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Meta ads lean on first-person testimonial copy ("Ik ben gestopt met zoeken...", "Claire D.") and a specific, time-bound bundle discount (14% off Toner + AHA Peel, gift at €100+). Google ads instead lean on third-party authority claims ("Aangeraden Door Artsen," a 4-star/168-review rating badge) and broad brand/prestige positioning, with no matching discount or bundle messaging visible in either screenshot. The "recommended by doctors" and clinical-authority framing on Google has no direct equivalent among the three Meta ad creatives collected. The age-specific "oudere huid" (older skin) angle on Google also isn't reflected in the Meta ad set.

Testing Roadmap

Eight focused ideas, moving from the product page performance fix into earlier trust, clearer pricing, consistent labels, and stronger cart reassurance.

Slot 1Immediate Fix

PDP Page Speed

One Shot Miner PRO PDP

The product page records a 22.0-second mobile LCP and a 22.1-second Time to Interactive, compared with 4.5 and 16.8 seconds on the homepage. The product image, the near-buy-box message that stock is running extremely low and shoppers should secure 2026 miners, bundle tiers, and Add to Cart action may all remain unavailable until late in the load. The fixed experience prioritizes those buying elements before secondary page content.

* The essential product and purchase controls load before secondary content. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2A/B Test

Homepage Trust Signal

Homepage

The homepage opens with “BANKRUPT SALE” and the 3.125 BTC reward promise, but no rating or guarantee appears in the first three captured folds. The product page already shows “★★★★★ (1420+ reviews)” above its buy box. The variation places that same review signal beneath the homepage headline, before the Choose Your Miner action.

* The existing 1420+ review signal moves into the homepage hero before the primary action. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3A/B Test

PDP Social Proof Placement

One Shot Miner PRO PDP

The PDP lists “One-In-A-Million Solo CKPool Win (~$373K)” under Recent Solo Mining Success Stories, dated 20-26 Feb 2025, in fold 3. The section notes that its examples may not use OSM devices, so this is external solo-mining evidence rather than an OSM customer result. The variation brings a condensed, qualified callout below the review line and above the bundle choices, while keeping the full story in place.

* The Recent Solo Mining Success Stories example moves nearer the buy box with its CKPool label, date range, and non-OSM caveat. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4A/B Test

Bundle Price Alignment

One Shot Miner PRO PDP

The selected Buy 3 & Get 1 Free tier is labeled $179.97 on the PDP, but Add to Cart creates four One Shot PRO units at $45 each, a free eBook, and a $180 cart subtotal. The Best Value choice therefore changes its displayed math between the buy box and cart. The variation states four miners at $45 each, the included eBook, and a $180 total consistently across both surfaces.

* The selected bundle uses the same four-unit, $45-each, $180-total story from PDP to cart, with the free eBook retained. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

Collection Discount Logic

All Miners Collection

Collection badges show 40%, 70%, 65%, 67%, and 72% savings across similar hardware tiers without a visible reason for the spread. Public reviews repeatedly describe the pricing and refund experience as confusing or manipulative, which raises the cost of unexplained markdowns. The variation ties every badge to one visible quantity rule so shoppers can understand why the saving changes.

* Product badges follow one visible quantity rule instead of unexplained card-by-card markdowns. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6Immediate Fix

Version Label Consistency

Collection Footer and Product Detail Page

The collection footer links to “One Shot Miner PRO (2025 Edition),” while the homepage and product page both call the same product “(2026 Edition).” The mismatch creates a visible question about which model the link opens. The fixed footer uses the current 2026 product name everywhere.

* The footer link now matches the 2026 product title used on the homepage and PDP. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7A/B Test

Cart Payment and Return Reassurance

Cart Drawer

The cart drawer places “24/7 Support - Lifetime Warranty” above the item and “Last units left” below checkout, but it shows no payment icons or return-policy link. The product page already uses payment icons directly under Add to Cart. The variation brings the same payment reassurance and a Return policy link directly above checkout, while keeping the existing cart content intact.

* Payment methods and the return policy appear immediately before checkout in the existing cart drawer. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8A/B Test

Homepage Card Headlines

Homepage

All six cards in the live featured grid repeat “Join the race of solo mining 3.125 BTC by yourself” at the top of their image artwork. Each card already has a model-specific orange banner, including the 24/7 operation, 1.2 TH/s, and hydro-cooled messages. The variation removes only the repeated top headline and preserves those existing model-specific banners.

* The repeated 3.125 BTC headline leaves the image artwork, while the existing orange model-specific banners stay unchanged. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  • The product page records a 22.0-second mobile LCP and 22.1-second Time to Interactive. The page that contains the buy box is the slowest measured surface.
  • The homepage carries the strongest urgency and scarcity language, but no review, rating, or guarantee appears in the first three captured folds.
  • Pricing and product labels contradict one another. Collection markdowns range from 40% to 72%, the Best Value PDP label shows $179.97 before the cart shows four $45 units and a $180 subtotal, and the footer names a 2025 model that the store sells as the 2026 Edition.
  • Public reviews split sharply between smooth delivery with working products and failures around delivery, refunds, cancellations, and support. Small credibility gaps on-site land in an already sensitive trust context.

What the Data Shows

Site Screenshots

  • The homepage opens with “BANKRUPT SALE | ENDS WHEN STOCK RUNS OUT” and shows no star rating, review count, or guarantee in the first three captured folds.
  • The PDP adds a near-buy-box message that stock is running extremely low and urges shoppers to secure 2026 miners.
  • All six cards in the live featured grid repeat the same 3.125 BTC headline in their image artwork, while their orange bottom banners are already model-specific.
  • The collection shows 40%, 70%, 65%, 67%, and 72% savings badges, while its footer calls the flagship product the 2025 Edition and the homepage and PDP call it the 2026 Edition.
  • The default Best Value cart contains four One Shot PRO units at $45 each, a free eBook, and a $180 subtotal, rather than the PDP’s $179.97 bundle label.
  • The cart drawer includes lifetime warranty and low-stock messages, three upsells, subtotal, and checkout, but no visible payment icons or return-policy link.

Live Homepage Content

  • The live homepage content includes Free Shipping, Secure Payments, Moneyback Guarantee, Customer Support, and a 1000+ five-star reviews section.
  • Those trust elements do not appear in the first three captured homepage folds, placing them later than the opening urgency message.

PageSpeed Insights

  • The homepage scores 79/100 on mobile, with 4.5s LCP, 0 CLS, 150ms TBT, and 16.8s Time to Interactive.
  • The product page scores 63/100 on mobile, with 22.0s LCP, 0 CLS, 240ms TBT, and 22.1s Time to Interactive.
  • The product page has the worst measured performance and the longest LCP of the collected pages.

Reviews and UGC

  • Positive reviews repeatedly praise simple setup, plug-and-play use, the miner’s display value, low power draw, and educational appeal.
  • Negative reviews repeatedly describe non-delivery, failed refunds, ignored cancellations, broken units, and repetitive support replies.
  • Several reviewers cite the company’s one-hour cancellation policy as not being honored, and more than three negative reviews name the same support representative.
  • The company replies to negative reviews with personalized fixes, but the audit found that public escalation often appears necessary before resolution.

Panafrica pairs a distinctive product and strong customer love for comfort and color with a storefront that delays the buying path and sends inconsistent offer signals. This roadmap removes technical and messaging breakpoints, then brings products, trust, fit guidance and recommendations closer to the decision.

Slot 1Immediate Fix

Homepage Load Experience

Page: Homepage

The homepage hero takes 16.6 seconds to become the largest visible element on mobile, while the page takes 23.4 seconds to become interactive. The delay keeps the collection and its shop path from appearing promptly. The proposed experience makes the existing campaign hero and controls visible quickly, with mobile LCP targeted below 4 seconds.

Homepage First Fold

Page: Homepage

The opening fold shows lifestyle imagery and one “DÉCOUVRIR” CTA, while product prices and trust remain absent across the first three folds. Shoppers must scroll before they can see what is for sale or the proof already used on PDPs. The variation keeps the campaign image and adds a compact featured-product strip with prices and a review line beside the CTA.

Featured sneakers, prices and customer proof enter the first fold without replacing the campaign image. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3Immediate Fix

Offer Message Match

Pages: WhatsApp Club offer and onsite bubble

The WhatsApp Club message offers 15€, while an onsite bubble presents -10€. The shopper sees two different values within the same onsite journey. The proposed experience uses one current offer and repeats the same wording at every touchpoint.

The 15€ WhatsApp Club message and -10€ onsite bubble become one current promise repeated across the journey. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4Immediate Fix

Paid Creative Feed

Surface: Paid search creative

Paid-search tiles display unfilled variables such as <Prix> and <Rating (Reviews)>, while another tile shows a raw 500 error. These are visible broken states inside active creative variants. The fix replaces every placeholder and error state with complete product, price, and destination content.

Broken variables and error tiles are replaced by complete product creative with real destinations. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

PDP Size Guidance

Page: Sahara-Safran product page

Sizing runs large is the most repeated complaint across more than 90 Sahara reviews, despite the existing fit note on the page. The advice sits below the selector, but it does not clearly lead the size decision. The variation places “Ce modèle taille grand” directly under the size buttons and recommends taking one size down.

The recurring sizing warning moves directly beneath the size buttons, before the purchase decision. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  • The homepage combines a 16.6s mobile LCP with no product price or trust signal in its first three folds.
  • Sizing confusion is the dominant repeated complaint across more than 90 Sahara reviews.

What We Found

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Offer-focused traffic can move from no discount promise to a landing page with a 15€ WhatsApp Club message and a separate -10€ bubble.
  • Every reviewed paid-social landing page used the same Arusha PDP structure, with a 5-star trust block and the same two-tier purchase buttons.
  • Live review counts and availability states no longer match the collected screenshots, confirming a timing gap between capture and audit.

Google Ads

  • Search creative leads with ethical, vegan, eco-responsible and unique wax design messages, while paid-social creative leads with color, style and discount.
  • Several creative tiles show unfilled dynamic variables, and another shows a raw 500 error.
  • No 5-star or review signal appears in the reviewed search creative.

Reviews and UGC

  • Comfort is repeated across nearly every reviewed Sahara comment.
  • Design originality, color combinations and perceived quality are recurring strengths.
  • Sizing runs large is the most repeated complaint, with multiple customers recommending one size down and some recommending two sizes down.
  • At least two reviewers say the physical colors are paler or duller than the product photography.

Page Speed Insights

  • The homepage records a 0.49 performance score, 16.6s LCP, 5.6s FCP and 23.4s Time to Interactive.
  • The Sahara-Safran PDP records a 0.64 performance score, 4.2s LCP, 3.2s FCP and 23.3s Time to Interactive.
  • Both pages have near-identical Time to Interactive despite very different LCP results, suggesting a shared render-blocking or third-party script cost.

Site Screenshots

  • The homepage shows no priced product or trust signal until its third fold.
  • On the New In collection, hovered cards expose sizes 36 to 47 and 33 models without an Add to cart action, while neighboring cards remain image, name and price.
  • The 5-star review block appears high on PDPs but is absent from the reviewed homepage and collection cards.
  • Arusha and Sahara-Safran both show the recommendation module in the same relative fold after delivery and returns.

Competitor Analysis

  • No direct wax-print sneaker rival with comparable positioning surfaced in the research.
  • Veja is the closest broader ethical French sneaker comparison, with a publicly observed price range of about €125 to €215.
  • Panafrica's competitive pressure appears to come from the broader ethical and sustainable sneaker set rather than a like-for-like rival.

# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png (Google Ads Transparency Center grid view, "H&V Verified" tiles)

**Ads visible:** A large grid of ad units across multiple formats — Performance Max / Display image ads, Shopping product listings, YouTube video ads, Search text ads, and Sponsored local/map listings. Roughly 25+ individual tiles visible across the two screenshots, several repeating the same creative.

**Headline themes:**
- "Réveillez votre style" / "Pourquoi choisir entre style et valeurs ? Portez des sneakers qui ont du sens et du style" — style-meets-values positioning
- "Baskets éthiques et colorées - La Marque de Baskets éthiques" — ethical + colorful positioning
- "Baskets vegan" — vegan material callout
- "Sneakers 100% uniques - Sneakers Panafrica" / "Sneakers 100% uniques - Sneakers Solaires & Éthiques" — uniqueness + "solaires" (sun/solar) framing
- "Panafrica Baskets éthiques & mode éco-responsable" / "A drop of summer" — eco-responsible fashion, seasonal drop framing
- "Design Wax Unique" — African wax-print design pattern callout
- "NOUVELLE COLLECTION — Mode Responsable & Colorée. Des sneakers uniques pour ceux qui osent. Retours gratuits" — new collection, responsible/colorful, free returns
- Video ad: "Collection Automne-Été. Le confort d'une basket premium, l'audace d'un design unique. Trouvez votre paire" — comfort + bold design, mixed-season naming ("Automne-Été" = Autumn-Summer, an odd pairing)

**Offers and CTAs:** "Retours gratuits" (free returns) appears in at least 2 variants. One display ad references "jusqu'à -40% sur une sélection de paires, jusqu'au 28 juillet" (up to 40% off a selection, until July 28 — a dated/expired promo still showing in the ad account). CTAs seen: "Ouvrir" (Open), "Open", "Website", "Call", "Directions" (on local/map ad units). No CTA button reading "Shop" or "-10€" offer appears anywhere in the Google Ads set, unlike Meta.

**Visual approach:** Mix of clean product-on-background shoe photography (single shoe, pair, or stacked pairs), lifestyle/behind-the-scenes video content (a woman at a textile/wax-print workshop), a few placeholder/broken tiles ("500. That's an error. There was an error. Please try again later.", "<Rating (Reviews)>", "<Category>", "<Prix>", "<Note (Avis)>", "<distance>" — several tiles show unfilled dynamic-ad template variables instead of real values), and local/map-pack style listings citing "7 Rue Joseph Dijon 75018" (Paris store address).

**Gaps vs. Meta:**
- Google Ads messaging leads with "ethical", "vegan", "eco-responsible", "unique wax design", and "style meets values" — none of the three Meta ads reviewed use this ethical/vegan/eco angle; Meta ads lead with color/style and a "-10€ first order" discount instead.
- Google Ads shows a "-40% sale, until July 28" promo and "Retours gratuits" (free returns) as the primary offers; Meta ads show a "-10€" floating offer on-page instead. The two channels are not running matching offers.
- Several Google ad units are rendering broken/unfilled dynamic placeholders (`<Rating (Reviews)>`, `<Prix>`, `<Category>`, a raw 500 error tile) — these are live in the transparency center grid, meaning some active ad variants may be serving with template variables unrendered.
- No 5-star/100-review trust signal appears in any Google ad creative, unlike the consistent 5-star/100-review block shown on every Meta landing page.

Trust, pricing, and offer promises change across the storefront, while the talisman page adds a severe mobile performance barrier. This roadmap first restores a stable buying experience, then makes the offer and reassurance consistent from first click to checkout.

Testing Roadmap

Eight focused opportunities, sequenced from foundation to growth.

Slot 1Immediate Fix

Talisman Page Performance

Magnetic Energy Talisman PDP

The talisman page scores 0.13 for mobile performance, with a 13.3-second LCP, 0.897 CLS, and 48.3-second TTI. The product, price, and buy controls visibly move and remain unavailable during the load window. The proposed experience keeps the gallery and purchase area stable and usable as the page appears.

* The product gallery and purchase area stay fixed and usable while the page loads. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2A/B Test

Automatic First-Box Price

Mindful Subscription Box

The customer-facing promise says the discount is automatic, but the landing page shows a $39.97 CTA and asks shoppers to enter MYBOX35 for $25.99. The main action contradicts the promise that earned the click. The variation applies the offer in the primary price and removes the manual code step.

* The promised first-box price moves into the primary CTA and the manual code step disappears. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3A/B Test

Site-Wide Trust Number

Homepage

The first three homepage folds show 12k+ reviews, 2M+ boxes delivered, and 500k+ happy customers with equal visual weight. The collection page adds 3k+ Trustpilot reviews and 11,388 happy customers. The variation repeats one verified trust treatment across the hero, reassurance strip, and category section.

* One verified trust treatment appears in every high-visibility homepage placement. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4A/B Test

Homepage Discount Message

Homepage

LABOR35 appears in the announcement bar, headline, and supporting copy within the first homepage fold, while other offers use different discount mechanics. Repetition makes the hero work harder without adding new information. The variation keeps the code once in the announcement bar and lets the hero focus on the product and savings.

* The discount code appears once in the announcement bar while the hero carries the product promise. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

Cart Guarantee

Cart drawer

The cart drawer shows a discount-code note before checkout but no guarantee or returns reassurance. Elsewhere, Mindful Souls prominently offers a 30-day money-back guarantee. The variation brings that same reassurance directly above the secure checkout button.

* The 30-day money-back guarantee appears immediately before secure checkout. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6A/B Test

Talisman Trust Signals

Magnetic Energy Talisman PDP

The talisman page shows one customer review and a Trustpilot badge, but no review count or guarantee copy near the CTA in the captured folds. Subscription landing pages provide a fuller reassurance stack. After the performance fix, the variation adds the documented review count and a compact shipping, returns, guarantee, and checkout strip beside the action.

* The documented review count and four reassurance cues move beside the talisman CTA. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7A/B Test

Collection Review Weight

Best Sellers collection

A product with 12 reviews and one with 886 reviews use the same full-size five-star treatment on the collection grid. That presentation gives very different sample sizes equal visual authority. The variation keeps counts beside the stars and gives lower-sample ratings a quieter scale.

* Review counts stay visible, while lower-sample ratings use a quieter star treatment. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8Custom Landing Pages

Product Catalog Landing Pages

Product-specific paid traffic

Paid traffic promotes rings, phone cases, necklaces, water bottles, pendulums, incense, and other individual products, but the matching destination details were not verified in this audit. That leaves product, price, and offer continuity unconfirmed. The project creates or verifies focused landing experiences that repeat each customer-facing claim from click through to purchase.

* Each product promise leads to a page with the same product, price, and offer. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  • Trust figures do not reconcile across the homepage, collection, landing pages, product page, and paid-search snippet. Each number receives similar visual authority.
  • LABOR35, SPRING35, MYBOX35, an unlabeled 35% offer, and a 50% off plus free gift offer are live across acquisition surfaces.
  • The Magnetic Energy Talisman page is the severe performance outlier: 0.13 mobile Performance, 13.3-second LCP, 0.897 CLS, and 48.3-second TTI.

**Descriptions visible:**
- "Blue Razz flavor meets clean focus. No caffeine or nicotine. Get smooth energy and mental clarity without the crash."

**Visual approach:**
- Display ads use product photography (cans on white/light background)
- Video ads show a man at what appears to be a podcast or event setting holding Ultra cans
- Dark video variant labeled "Sharp Mind Daily" visible
- Shopping cards show individual flavors: Tropical, Blue Razz (multiple listings)

**Product line visible:**
- Focus Pouches (Cool Mint, Wintergreen, Tropical, Blue Razz)
- Sleep Pouches (Honey Lemon) — a separate product line appearing in shopping ads

**Gaps vs. Meta:**
- Google search ads use no discounts; Meta ads show 35-50% off prominently
- Google leans on "clean energy, no crash" and "mental clarity/sharpness" — closer to functional claims than Meta's lifestyle/identity angle ("Flow state," "NO MORE SOFT D*CK")
- Podcast-style video creative on Google matches Ad 2 on Meta (man with cans), suggesting asset reuse
- One shopping ad has a broken "[Price]" placeholder — potential revenue loss from non-rendering product price

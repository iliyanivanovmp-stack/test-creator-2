For a product that's marketed as an energy/focus pouch, that's pretty disappointing and not what I was expecting. The marketing makes it sound like you'll feel a clean, noticeable boost, but that just wasn't my experience at all.

Flavor-wise they're…meh, but nothing special. The pouches are a bit bitter, and it feels like they're trying to cover that up with a ton of menthol. On the mango flavor I could barely taste any mango at all—just menthol dominating everything. It's not terrible, but it's definitely not something I look forward to using. Between the lack of effect and the so-so taste, there just isn't much reason for me to keep using them.

Overall, I won't be ordering these again. It feels like it's mostly clever branding and marketing, and they did manage to get me to try them once, but that's about it. If you're looking for a noticeable energy boost, I'd recommend looking elsewhere.

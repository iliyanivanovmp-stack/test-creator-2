**Offers and CTAs:** "Save up to 30% on Essentia" and HSA/FSA savings repeated across multiple units (consistent with the Meta HSA/FSA ad). "Buy Essentia mattress today," "Handcrafted Custom Sizes," phone CTA offering a mattress expert consult. No explicit percent-off sitewide discount (e.g., the 20% off seen on-site) appears in the Google ad copy reviewed.

**Visual approach:** Display/video ad thumbnails show lifestyle imagery (woman on mattress with dog, founder/CEO talking to camera in a video-style ad), product shots (Er-Go Neck Pillow, folded mattress cross-section), and a static badge-style creative ("Patented Organic Foam Tech" over a blue background with "Save up to 22% on eco-friendly mattresses"). Several ad slots rendered blank/unavailable ("Sorry, we're not able to show you this ad at this time").

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Google Ads lead heavily with certification/authority and "Made in USA" claims not present in the three Meta ad creatives reviewed. HSA/FSA savings messaging is consistent between Google (multiple units) and Meta (Ad 2). Google ads reference a 30% savings figure while one Google display creative references 22% off — a discount-percentage inconsistency within Google's own ad set. None of the Google ad creatives reference the sitewide "REST & RECHARGE SALE — 20% OFF" banner shown on-site.

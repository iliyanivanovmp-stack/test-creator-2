# Meta Ads Visual Summary

Note: All three Meta ads land on the homepage (https://lanxshoes.com/) rather than a dedicated landing page. Site Pages homepage folds (below) serve as the landing page reference for message match on all three ads.

## Ad 1
**Creative:** Video ad. Library ID 828852699697683, started running Apr 24, 2026, active on Facebook and Instagram. Headline copy: "Step inside the LANX Whalley Warehouse Shop in Lancashire." Body: "If you're passing by, pop in and say hello. The kettle's always on, and the beers are cold." Address and hours listed (Unit 29, Mitton Rd, Whalley, Lancs, BB7 9YE; open daily 10-4). Video thumbnail shows an exterior building shot. CTA button: "Learn more." Link text: "LANXSHOES.COM — Whalley Shop — Open Daily 10-4."
**Landing Page Fold 1:** Homepage hero — full-width street photo of a man and woman in outerwear standing in a UK terraced-house alley, "AXIS" logo mark, headline "SOMETHING SOLID AT THE CENTRE," two CTA buttons "SHOP MEN" / "SHOP WOMEN." Top bar promo: "10% OFF YOUR FIRST ORDER." Nav: MEN'S, WOMEN'S, ACCESSORIES, EXPLORE, search bar, account/notification icons, RE-LANX, HELP, cart.
**Landing Page Fold 2:** Split-image section — men's boot on cobblestone (left, "MEN'S — SHOP BY STYLE" with Boots/365 Vibram/Shoes/Trainers buttons) and women's boot sole close-up (right, same style buttons for women). Below: Reviews section header, "4.79 STARS (6,612 REVIEWS)," row of quote-mark review cards begins.
**Landing Page Fold 3:** Continuation of review carousel (7 customer quote cards visible, verified-customer badges, star ratings, names). Below reviews: lifestyle photo of the physical Whalley shop interior — boot display shelves, leather sofas, LANX-branded rugby shirts on a rack.
**LP CTA:** "SHOP MEN" / "SHOP WOMEN" buttons in hero, both above the fold, not sticky.
**LP trust signals:** 4.79-star rating with 6,612 reviews visible on fold 2/3; verified-customer badges on review cards. No shipping/returns/guarantee copy visible in these three folds.
**Message Match:** Zero message match. The ad promotes a physical shop visit (Whalley Warehouse address, hours, "pop in and say hello") with no online-shopping CTA. The homepage it lands on is a standard ecommerce hero (AXIS collection, Shop Men/Women) with no mention of the Whalley shop, its address, or store visit messaging anywhere in the first three folds.

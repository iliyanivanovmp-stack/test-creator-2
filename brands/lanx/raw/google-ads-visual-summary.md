# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png (Google Ads Transparency Center results, advertiser "LANX GROUP LIMITED")

**Ads visible:** Two screenshots showing a grid of ~30+ ad units total across both images — mix of formats: Shopping/product listing ads (individual shoe images with price/rating), text search ads (blue headline + description + sitelinks), display/image ads (lifestyle photography, some video with play icon), and one "not able to show this ad" placeholder.

**Headline themes:** Recurring copy patterns across text ads:
- "Proud English company — Handmade Boots, Shoes, Brogues" / "Proper shoes, get 'em worn"
- "LANX Official Store — Handmade Boots, Shoes, Brogues and Sneakers Men's and women's footwear"
- Product-category-specific headlines: "Men's Boat Shoes | LANX Leather Casual Footwear," "Men's Chelsea Boots | Leather Slip-On Dealer Boots — LANX," "LANX Women's Hiker Boots - Women's Hiker Style Boots," "Men's Trainers & Sneakers | Casual Footwear — LANX"
- Location/heritage angle repeated: "Proudly Northern. Proudly Worn - Proud English company," "Located in the beautiful Ribble Valley, Lancashire"
- One loyalty-focused ad: "LANX Loyalty - Join LANX Loyalty today — Earn points, access to exclusive benefits, offers up to 50% off. Buy Now, Pay Later. Women's 365 Collection."

**Offers and CTAs:** "10% off Everything" and "Christmas" badge visible on one sitelink-style ad. "Buy Now, Pay Later" and "Earn points" mentioned in the loyalty ad. Sitelinks across several ads: "Women's Footwear," "Men's Footwear," "Men's Boots," "Women's Boots," "Shoe Sizing Advice," "Contact," "Wallet," "Visit Our Lancashire Factory," "Women's 365 Collection," "Sliding Advice," "Women's Popular Footwear."

**Visual approach:** Shopping ads use plain product-on-white shoe photography with price and star rating/review count under the name. Display ads use on-location lifestyle photography (a person standing outside a shop, similar in style to the Meta shop-visit ads) and one grid collage of 6 boots. Text ads are standard Google blue-link format with 2-3 sitelinks each.

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Google ads lean on heritage/provenance ("Proud English company," "Proudly Northern," "beautiful Ribble Valley") and product-category/price-led messaging (Shopping ads, sizing advice sitelink, loyalty/BNPL offer), while the three Meta ads reviewed are entirely shop-visit/brand-experience focused (Whalley Warehouse visit, Manchester shop visit, founder factory walkthrough) with no product, price, or offer messaging at all. The two channels are promoting fundamentally different things: Google drives toward purchase intent and specific product categories; Meta drives toward physical store visits and brand story content, then dumps that traffic on a generic ecommerce homepage.

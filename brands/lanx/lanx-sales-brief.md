# LANX (lanxshoes.com) - Sales Brief

Prepared 2026-08-21 via /last30days research (Reddit, HN, GitHub, Polymarket, web). For Convertibles sales - concepts + Loom outreach.

## Brand snapshot

- Handmade boots, shoes, brogues, sneakers (men's + women's), Lancashire, UK. Positioning: "Something solid at the centre" - bridges formal/outdoor, heritage/modern.
- Young brand (<10 years), Shopify store, free UK shipping and returns.
- Collections: Axis, Craftsman, Artisan, 365 (Vibram), 1985, Casual. First loafer (Finsley) just added.
- RE-LANX: repair + trade-in program for loyalty credits, refurbished at their Whalley factory.
- Collabs: WALSH (Made-in-England trainers), Bath Rugby, Leicester Tigers, Vibram.

## What's happening right now (the timely hook)

- **AXIS collection launched 13 August 2026** - eight days ago. New flagship "Lancashire Boot" positioned as the centre of the range. They are actively pushing traffic at new PDPs right now - the best possible moment for a CRO conversation.
- Annual Sample Sale weekend runs in March (up to 70% off) - heavy discount event in an otherwise premium-positioned brand.

## Proof points (theirs)

- Trustpilot 4.8 with ~2,000 reviews, 93% five-star. Strong customer-service reputation.
- Review language: "built to last", "quality of leather", "attention to detail", recrafting praised.

## Friction signals (our opening)

1. **Sizing confusion is the #1 recurring complaint.** Trustpilot 1-star tail: "weirdly sized", "buy a size smaller", "buy several pairs and return the ones that don't fit". Sizing is model-dependent (Ogden true to size, others run large). Their sizing advice lives on a separate page, not in the buy box.
   - Concept: comprehensive per-model fit guide directly above the variant selector (fashion-PDP best practice), or a 3-question fit finder. Cuts returns AND the anxiety that kills first-purchase CVR at this price point.
2. **Returns-process complaints** despite "free UK returns" promise - anxiety gap between policy and perception. Concept: returns reassurance at the point of size selection.
3. **RE-LANX is under-leveraged as a conversion asset.** Repair/trade-in is a powerful anxiety-killer ("buy once, they'll maintain it") - prime objection-section material on PDPs, not just a standalone page.
4. **New AXIS PDPs are fresh** - launched days ago, likely un-optimized and untested. Natural A/B test candidate while launch traffic is high.

## Honest coverage note

Last-30-days social listening was thin: only 5 Reddit threads touched the brand space (r/goodyearwelt, r/malefashionadvice) and none cleared relevance thresholds - LANX is not currently a topic of organic community conversation. The signal above comes from press coverage, first-party announcements, and ~2,000 Trustpilot reviews. Low social chatter + high review volume = brand grows on paid/CRM, which strengthens the CRO pitch (site conversion is where their money is made).

## Loom talking points (Clay)

1. Congratulate the AXIS launch (13 Aug) - shows we did homework, creates urgency.
2. Show their PDP: sizing advice buried on a separate page while their own reviews say sizing is the top purchase anxiety.
3. One concrete concept: per-model fit guidance above the selector + RE-LANX objection block on the PDP. Frame as "your 4.8 Trustpilot says the product is great - the site just needs to remove the one doubt stopping first-time buyers."
4. CTA: 20-minute call, we bring 2 more test concepts from the review mining.

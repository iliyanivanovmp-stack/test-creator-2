# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png (Google Ads Transparency Center results)

**Ads visible:** Approximately 30 ad units across the two screenshots — a mix of text/search-style ad cards, image ad cards, and one video ad thumbnail. All attributed to "Instant Hydration Inc. — Verified."

**Headline themes:** Recurring angles across ads include: "Real Hydration. No Junk" / "No Junk, No Filler," positioning against "sugary sports drinks" ("Every week, thousands are replacing their 'sugary sports drinks' with a healthier option"), ingredient/formula superiority ("Lab-Tested for Superior Taste, Performance, and Safety"), "Ditch Fake Salt Brands - Superior Electrolyte Formula," "Instant Hydration and Pregnancy: 6 Things Every Mom Should Know" (content/educational angle), "Hydration Without The Junk - Made With French Sea Salt," and product-specific callouts ("ICEE Blue Raspberry & Cherry Electrolyte Drink Mix").

**Offers and CTAs:** Discount offers visible include "40% Off Auto Ship Orders," "Up to 55% Off Auto Ship Orders," "-35%" and "-35%" badges on product image ads, "Free Shipping For Subscription," "Limited-Time 44% Off Sale," "Limited-Time 40% Off Sale," and "Every stick under $1.50." Several cards show star ratings with review counts, e.g. "4.6 ★★★★★ (110)," "4.6 ★★★★★ (111)," "4.6 ★★★★★ (109)," "4.4 ★★★★☆ (33)." Link text on several cards includes "View Full Ingredients," "Shop Our 30-Pack Flavors," "Try It Risk-Free For 50 Days," "Got a Question?," "Return policy."

**Visual approach:** Image-format ads show product boxes/sticks on a pink/salmon background with "-35%" discount badges in the corner. One video ad thumbnail shows a hand holding stick packs with overlay text "Your Favorite Hydration... Our Biggest Sale." Search-style ad cards use a black circular "Instant Hydration" avatar icon, blue headline link text, and a "Verified" checkmark label under the business name.

**Gaps vs. Meta:** The Meta ad creatives reviewed (raw/meta-ads-visual-summary.md) center on the ICEE collaboration and the "Try Risk Free for 50 Days + FREE GIFT" offer with "7x/6x Electrolytes, Zero Sugar" messaging. The Google Ads screenshots show a broader, more varied message set (anti-"sugary sports drinks" positioning, ingredient superiority, pregnancy/education content, French sea salt) with discount percentages (35-55% off) that differ numerically from the Meta ad's "50 Days risk-free + free gift" framing, and do not surface the ICEE branding/flavor angle at all in the sampled ads.

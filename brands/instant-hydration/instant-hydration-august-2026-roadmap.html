CRO Testing Roadmap

August 2026

The strongest opportunity is a cleaner path from promise to purchase. The roadmap aligns the offer across key surfaces, steadies the highest-value landing experience, and answers product questions before they become objections.

Testing Ideas

Eight focused opportunities across discovery, product education, landing-page performance, and cart clarity.

Slot 1Immediate Fix

Homepage Hero CTA

Homepage

The homepage hero promotes one offer, then sends shoppers to Premium Electrolyte Drink Mix while the next section introduces ICEE. The destination breaks the product story at the main click. Route the hero CTA to the product featured by the active promotion.

* The main promo click lands on the product featured in the promotion. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2A/B Test

Landing Page Offer

ICEE Landing Page

Offer-focused traffic promises a 50-day risk-free trial plus a free gift, but the combined promise is absent from the first buy-box view. The page separates the guarantee from the gift. Restate the complete offer directly above the flavor CTA.

* The full risk-free offer appears inside the first buy-box view. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3Performance Fix

Landing Page Stability

ICEE Landing Page

The ICEE landing page records a 7.0s LCP and 0.546 CLS on mobile. Product media and decision modules can visibly shift while the page loads. Reserve their space so the offer and CTA stay fixed from first paint to ready state.

* Product media and decision modules keep their position while content loads. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4A/B Test

Collection Card Pricing

Top Rated Electrolytes Collection

Six captured product cards show imagery and titles without price, compare-at price, or per-unit cost. Shoppers cannot compare offers from the collection itself. Add pricing under each title while keeping the current three-column product grid.

* Price appears directly below each product title for faster comparison. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

Serving Guidance

ICEE Landing Page

Saltiness is the most repeated complaint across the 45 reviewed ratings, including positive reviews that describe using more water. The ingredient section names French Grey Sea Salt without dilution guidance. Add a milder-taste mixing note beside that ingredient callout.

* The salt callout gains clear guidance for a milder mix. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6A/B Test

Mobile Add-to-Cart Bar

ICEE Landing Page

The mobile page repeats “SELECT FROM 14 FLAVORS” across several sections, but the CTA does not stay available while shoppers scroll. Mobile time to interactive is 35.3s. Keep the product name, price, and primary action fixed at the bottom after the first buy box.

* The product action remains reachable while shoppers explore the mobile page. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7A/B Test

Free Gift Clarity

Cart Drawer

The cart drawer adds a “3 Pack Sampler” marked “FREE” without explaining why it appears. Nothing connects the line item to the free-gift promise. Label it as the shopper’s automatically added free gift.

* The sampler is clearly connected to the promised free gift. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8A/B Test

Competitor Comparison

ICEE Landing Page

The comparison table shows 10 calories and 0g sugar against named alternatives, but it appears after the buy box, accordion, review, and lifestyle content. Its strongest differentiation arrives late. Move the existing table directly below the buy box.

* The existing comparison appears immediately after the buy box, before supporting content. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Data Insights

Cross-Source Themes

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  • The ICEE landing page carries the strongest paid-traffic intent but records the weakest mobile loading performance.
  • Saltiness appears across positive and negative reviews, while captured site surfaces provide no dilution guidance.

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Paid-social creative consistently promises “Try Risk Free for 50 Days + FREE GIFT” and sends traffic to the ICEE product page.
  • The ICEE hero matches the collaboration and flavor promise, but the combined risk-free and free-gift line is not present in the captured first buy-box view.
  • The buy box defaults to Subscribe & Save with a free first-shipment gift and free U.S. shipping, while One Time shows “NO FREE SHIPPING” and “NO FREE GIFT.”

Google Ads

  • Discount framing ranges from 35% to 55% off, alongside risk-free, ingredient, and zero-sugar messages.
  • Google’s discount-led value proposition does not align with the paid-social risk-free plus free-gift framing.

Reviews and UGC

  • Saltiness is the most repeated complaint across the 45 reviewed ratings, including reviewers who use double or triple the recommended water ratio.
  • Stevia aftertaste and sensitivity appear separately from the brand’s monk-fruit messaging.
  • Price objections recur in otherwise positive reviews, including “$37 for 20 packs” and “a little pricey.”
  • Customers praise hydration and cramp support, the clean ingredient list, zero sugar, French grey sea salt, and single-serve convenience.

PageSpeed and Core Web Vitals

  • The ICEE landing page scores 36/100 on mobile, with a 7.0s LCP, 0.546 CLS, and 35.3s time to interactive.
  • The homepage scores 60/100 on mobile, with a 4.8s LCP, 0 CLS, and 33.2s time to interactive.
  • The ICEE page has the weaker LCP and a CLS score more than double the 0.25 poor threshold.

Site Screenshots

  • The homepage hero CTA links to Premium Electrolyte Drink Mix, while the next section promotes ICEE and Shop Electrolytes.
  • None of the six captured collection cards show price, compare-at price, or per-unit pricing.
  • The cart drawer includes a free 3 Pack Sampler without copy explaining why it was added.
  • The homepage contains a strong trust stack: athlete endorsements, 4.5 out of 5, 25,000+ verified reviews, and 2 million+ customer purchases.

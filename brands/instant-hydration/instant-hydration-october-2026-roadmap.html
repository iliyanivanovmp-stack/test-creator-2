Customers talk about flavors, but choosing a first box takes work. These tests make the starting choice easier, align flavor promises, and bring value and reassurance closer to the purchase.

Slot 01A/B Test

PDP Default Flavor Selection

Premium Electrolyte PDP ↗

The flavor selector starts at zero boxes, leaving the purchase button inactive until a shopper chooses. Variety Pack reviewers describe it as a way around flavor indecision. Test a pre-selected Variety Pack with an active add-to-cart button and the freedom to swap flavors.

A selected Variety Pack turns the empty starting state into a ready-to-buy box. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 02A/B Test

PDP Flavor Match

Premium Electrolyte PDP ↗

Cotton Candy and P.O.G. offers lead to a page whose announcement bar promotes Crisp Apple. The selector starts with Energy+ Sour Green Apple, then Energy+ Tropical Crush, then Raspberry. Match the announcement, first image, and first flavor card to the flavor that brought them here.

Cotton Candy leads the page for Cotton Candy visitors; the same approach applies to P.O.G. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 03A/B Test

PDP Free Gift Clarity

Premium Electrolyte PDP ↗

The bottle is promised both on the first subscription shipment and with two or more boxes. These conditions leave the gift entitlement unclear. Test one gift explanation beside the purchase options, with the true eligibility rule confirmed before this idea is scoped.

One gift explanation replaces competing messages; eligibility remains an open question. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 04A/B Test

Cart Free Bottle Progress

Cart drawer

The checked one-box Energy+ subscription cart shows a $49.50 line and a $27.50 subtotal after the welcome discount, with no bottle-progress prompt. Resolve the displayed line price and subtotal before scoping this test. Then show how one more box unlocks the bottle, with item prices and totals that explain the applied discounts.

The populated one-box cart gains a two-box gift prompt after pricing is reconciled; shown prices reflect the checked discount combination. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 05A/B Test

Homepage Hero Trust Strip

Homepage ↗

The Crisp Apple hero leads with a limited flavor, while ratings and purchase proof sit further down the homepage. A new visitor reaches the shopping invitation before that reassurance. Bring the existing proof beneath the hero and add a direct link to all Hydration flavors.

Existing brand proof and an all-flavors path move directly beneath the hero. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 06A/B Test

PDP Per-Flavor Proof

Premium Electrolyte PDP ↗

Flavor cards offer a name, label, and quantity control, while the review sample repeatedly names favorite flavors. That customer language can help explain the choice. Put short verified-buyer quotes on Strawberry Lemon and LUIGI'S cards; favorite badges need validation against the full review set.

Real flavor-specific quotes sit beside the choice, without inventing ratings or favorite status. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 07A/B Test

Energy+ Purchase Options

Energy+ PDP ↗

Energy+ puts its one-time purchase option below the product details, away from the flavor choice. The captured buy box also lacks customer proof for the newer product. Bring the trial option, an Energy+ review, and clearly labeled brand-level reassurance together beside the purchase action.

The trial option and Energy+ review move into the buy box, with brand-level proof labeled clearly. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 08A/B Test

PDP Subscription Switch

Premium Electrolyte PDP ↗

One Time costs $1.65 per stick, compared with $0.92 on subscription. The $22.00 difference per box is not expressed as a switch beside the one-time price. Add a direct subscription comparison there while retaining the full subscriber benefit stack.

A price comparison beneath One Time makes the subscription switch explicit. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Where the evidence converges

Biggest Killers of

  • A flavor promise that changes on arrival

    Flavor-specific offers meet a Crisp Apple announcement. The audit also finds seasonal promotion language that does not match the site.

  • A heavy mobile landing page

    The September 24 mobile lab audit records a 9.8 s PDP LCP. Front-end weight is the performance issue identified in the audit.

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Flavor-focused paid-social creative promotes Cotton Candy and P.O.G., while the captured landing-page announcement leads with Crisp Apple.
  • The 50% discount applies to Subscribe & Save. One Time shows 10% off at $49.50.
  • Free-gift messaging meets bottle terms that differ between the subscription perk and the two-box promotion.

Google Ads

  • Ingredient-led messaging includes French Gray Sea Salt, zero sugar, and nothing artificial. This differs from the flavor and discount emphasis of paid social.
  • The under-$1.00-per-stick promise holds for subscription at $0.92, compared with $1.65 for One Time.
  • The captured Labor Day promotion ended September 10. Whether it still serves is unknown.

Reviews and UGC

  • In the small sample of 25 pasted reviews, Strawberry Lemon is named in 7 entries and LUIGI'S Lemon Italian Ice in 5.
  • Variety Pack feedback directly describes difficulty choosing a flavor. One reviewer also asks for a larger mixing bottle because the powders feel strong.
  • The pasted sample contains no star ratings or negative-review set. It cannot establish flavor-wide ratings or complaint frequency.

Page Speed Insights

  • Audit-sourced mobile lab results, September 24, 2026: PDP performance 45, LCP 9.8 s, and page weight 8,122 KiB. These measurements have not been rerun.
  • Homepage performance is 55 with LCP 5.4 s. The PDP has estimated image-delivery savings of 935 KiB and unused JavaScript savings of 1,051 KiB.
  • No field data or desktop performance results were collected. Energy+ was not tested.

Site Screenshots

  • The captured PDP selector starts at zero boxes. Its purchase action reads "0 BOXES SELECTED" until a flavor is chosen.
  • Energy+ places "TRY IT ONCE" below the second captured fold. No rating or review count appears in those captures.
  • The audit cart shows a $99.00 Energy+ line and a $55.00 subtotal. This is a capture-sourced discrepancy, not a verified explanation of checkout pricing.

Live Site Review

  • The audit's September 24 text checks found Cotton Candy and P.O.G. in the flavor list, although neither appeared in the captured landing folds.
  • Homepage copy includes "4.5 out of 5", "2 Million+ customer purchases", and "50-Day Money-Back Guarantee" beyond the captured top folds.

Social and Community Research

  • Directional research from August 25 to September 24, 2026 found creators recommending the Variety Pack as a starting point and discussing flavor novelty.
  • One Amazon review mentions saltiness and headaches. It is a single, unconfirmed item; broader complaint themes were not verified.

Competitor Research

  • Competitor prices came from third-party search results and were not verified against brand sites. They are not ready for a customer-facing price comparison.
  • The verified Instant Hydration comparison in the audit is internal: $0.92 per stick on subscription and $1.65 for One Time.

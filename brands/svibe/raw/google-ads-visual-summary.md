# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png (Google Ads Transparency Center results grid, advertiser "SVibe" / legal entity "AMOREO EUROPE S.L.", all marked "Verified")

**Ads visible:** Approximately 20+ distinct ad units across both screenshots, mixing text/search ad formats and Shopping/product listing ad formats. Roughly 16 search-style text ads and 4 Shopping-style product ads (with product images and "[Price]" placeholders) are visible.

**Headline themes:** Several repeating angles:
- Novelty/social-proof framing: "Home Of The Snail Vibe – Premium Couples Vibrators," "Home Of The Snail Vibe – Everyone's Talking About It," "SVibe is the newest thing in sex. Make sure you don't miss out."
- Product/technology framing: "Snail Vibe – Unroll your pleasure," "Body First Design – Explore New Sensations," "Snail Vibe Original. Revolutionary Classic Perfection of the Unique Snail Vibe Unrolling Concept."
- Couples-specific: "svibe snail jovi – dual couples' stimulator," "Snail Jovi: N°1 Couples Toy," repeated across multiple ad variants.
- Content/educational angle distinct from product ads: "squirt: myths debunked & the truth about female ejaculation – svibe," positioned as an informational landing page with a "US$10 off Sign Up to Save Now!" offer tag.
- Category/collection framing: "Snail Collection – Dual Stimulation Redefined – SVIBE," "Discover the Best Sex Toys – Vibrators, Masturbators & More."

**Offers and CTAs:** "US$10 off Sign Up to Save Now!" appears as a recurring offer tag on multiple ad units. "Save up to $30" appears as a recurring promo chip in the product-category navigation row under several ads. "2 years warranty. 30 days return policy." is repeated verbatim across nearly every text ad's body copy as a standard trust line. Shopping ads show "[Price]" and "[Shipping price]" placeholders (actual values not visible in the screenshot).

**Visual approach:** Search-style text ads use a standard blue-headline/black-body Google SERP layout with a row of sub-links (product category chips: "All Products," "Curve," "EVO," "Gizi," "Jovi," "Save up to $30"). Shopping ads display product photography — a hand holding the product against skin — consistent with the same hand-holding product shots used in the Meta ad landing pages (see meta-ads-visual-summary.md). One Shopping ad ("SVibe Snail Evo – Dual Masturbat...") shows a dark-colored device; others show a purple/navy Snail Curve-style device.

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Google Ads copy leans heavily on brand/novelty messaging ("newest thing in sex," "everyone's talking about it") and warranty/returns trust signals repeated in nearly every ad, neither of which appears in the three Meta ad creatives reviewed (meta-ads-visual-summary.md), which instead lead with personal-testimonial-style copy ("Honestly, when you first see it, you're like 'what is this thing?'"). The Google Ads "US$10 off Sign Up" and "Save up to $30" offers are not referenced in any of the three Meta ad creatives or their landing pages reviewed. The Google Ads set also includes a non-transactional educational ad ("squirt: myths debunked") with no equivalent format seen among the Meta ads collected.

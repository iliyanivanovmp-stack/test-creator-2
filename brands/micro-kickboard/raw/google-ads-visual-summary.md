# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png (Google Ads Transparency Center results)

**Ads visible:** Approximately 30+ ad units across both screenshots — a mix of text search ads, shopping/product image ads, sitelink-style ads, and video ad thumbnails. All listed under "Micro Kickboard, Inc." / "Micro Kickboard," all marked "Verified."

**Headline themes:** Several distinct headline angles appear:
- Product/category focus: "Micro Kickboard Scooters," "Micro Kickboard Accessories - Helmets, Parts & More," "Children – Micro Kickboard," "Micro Scooter Luggage"
- Authority/ranking claims: "The #1 Kids & Adult Scooter - The Micro Experience," "Micro Kickboard - The #1 Kids Scooter"
- Promotional/sale-driven: "Micro Mini® Scooter - Holiday Sale - Up to $50 Off," "Spring Sale: Up to 25% Off Scooters," "Micro Maxi Scooters - Spring Sale: Up To 25% Off"
- Seasonal/lifestyle: "Smooth Rides This Spring - Shop Award Winning...," "Years of Fun and Exercise - Shop Award Winning Scooters," "Scoot Into Spring"

**Offers and CTAs:** Multiple discount offers visible: "Up to $50 Off" (holiday sale), "Up to 25% Off" (spring sale, appears twice with slightly different copy). Sitelinks repeated across ads: "Shop For Ages 2 - 5," "Shop For Ages 5 - 12," "Shop For Ages 1 - 2," "Warehouse Deals," "Children Scooters," "Adults Scooters," "Teen/Adult Scooters." One ad shows a star rating "5.0 ★★★★★ (91)" and another "5.0 (88)" directly in the ad unit.

**Visual approach:** Product image ads show flat-lay or studio shots of scooters in multiple colorways (grid of 6 product images in one unit). Video ad thumbnails show a child riding a scooter outdoors on a sidewalk, captioned "With fun features for every adventure." One ad unit is a plain info icon placeholder with no visible creative.

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Google Ads messaging is built around scooter category/age-range navigation, awards/rankings, and time-boxed discounts (holiday, spring sale, % off). None of the reviewed Google ads use the "screens vs. real childhood" emotional hook seen across all three Meta ad creatives — the two channels run on different messaging angles. The discount offers visible in Google Ads ("Up to $50 Off," "Up to 25% Off") are not present in the Meta ad creatives or the shared landing page fold captures.

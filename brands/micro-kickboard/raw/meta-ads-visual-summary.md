# Meta Ads Visual Summary

Note: All three ads route to the same landing page — https://microkickboard.com/products/micro-mini-scooter — which is also the PDP. The same three fold screenshots (`ad1,2,3-landing-f1/f2/f3.png`) apply to all three ads below.

## Ad 1
**Creative:** Video ad. Text overlay reads "Before screens took over... there was THIS. Let your kids experience real childhood again." Video thumbnail shows a child outdoors near a scooter, with text overlay "The one thing I bought that actually gets them off the tablet without a fight." Headline below: "Turn Every Walk Into Playtime." Subtext: "Smooth-glide wheels for indoor + outdoor play." CTA button: "Shop Now."
**Landing Page Fold 1:** Product gallery (4 images) on left showing the Micro Mini Scooter in Ice Blue from multiple angles, with "Wirecutter Our Pick 2026" and "Parents Editor's Pick 2026" badges overlaid on two images. Right side: "Ages 2-5" label, product title "Micro Mini Scooter," price "$99.99," star rating (5 stars, "348 Reviews"), short description, color swatch selector (5 colors, Ice Blue selected), quantity selector, black "Add to cart" button, purple "Buy with Shop" button, "More payment options" link.
**Landing Page Fold 2:** Continuation of product gallery (video thumbnail), remainder of buy box (Features & Details, Scooter Specs, Warranty accordions; "Buy It With" cross-sell for Micro Pattern Helmets at $69.99). Below the buy box, a yellow section begins: "MINI SCOOTER / So much to love" with three lifestyle photos of children riding scooters wearing helmets.
**Landing Page Fold 3:** Continuation of yellow section with three feature callouts: "Lean-to-steer," "Super smooth glide," "Replaceable parts," each with a short description. Below, a large lifestyle photo with an overlay tag "Micro Pattern Helmets →" and a "Complete the look" cross-sell section showing two accessory products (a basket and a helmet).
**LP CTA:** "Add to cart" (black button) and "Buy with Shop" (purple button), both positioned in the right-hand buy box on fold 1. No sticky CTA bar visible in the captured folds.
**LP trust signals:** 5-star rating with "348 Reviews" count, positioned directly under the product title in the buy box. "Wirecutter Our Pick 2026" and "Parents Editor's Pick 2026" badges overlaid on product images. No guarantee or shipping-promise copy visible in the captured folds.
**Message Match:** Ad emphasizes "get kids off screens" and outdoor/indoor play messaging. Landing page headline and body do not repeat this specific screen-time angle — the page leads with product specs, awards, and price rather than the ad's emotional hook.

# Site Visual Summary

Note: No standalone PDP screenshots were collected. The Cliganic PDP is the Organic Jojoba Oil product page, and two of the three Meta ads (Ad 1, Ad 3) land directly on it — see `meta-ads-visual-summary.md` for PDP fold-by-fold detail on that page (and on the Rosemary Repair PDP that Ad 2 lands on).

## Homepage
**Fold 1:** Sticky top banner: "Free US Shipping on $40+ orders." Nav: Shop All, Build Your Own Bundle, Subscribe & Save, Learn, plus Rewards/search/account/cart icons. Hero is a full-bleed lifestyle photo (model holding Rosemary Repair Scalp & Strand Oil in her hair) with headline "Make Every Day A Great Hair Day!", subhead "A biotin-infused oil to help promote the look of fuller, thicker hair," and a white "TRY IT NOW!" button. A "NEW DROP" badge sits on the product bottle in the image. Below the hero, a scrolling marquee strip of claims: "10,000+ 5-STAR REVIEWS," "10,000+ RETAIL STORES | 40 COUNTRIES," "FORMULATED WITHOUT 2,500+ HARMFUL INGREDIENTS," "GC/MS TESTED," "500,000+ 5-STAR REVIEWS."
**Fold 2:** "Real Life Wellness" section with a row of 7 certification badges (USDA Organic, EWG Verified, Non-GMO Project Verified, Certified Vegan, Cruelty Free, Highest Quality & Purity, Affordably Priced, Sustainable As Possible). Below that, a "Best Sellers" module with category filter pills (Skin & Hair Oils, Essential Oils, Skincare, Mosquito Repellents, Supplements, Cotton) and a 3-up product carousel (Rosemary Repair Scalp & Strand Oil $10.07/$11.99, Organic Jojoba Oil $9.87/$12.99 "BEST SELLER," Organic Rosehip Oil $15.29/$17.99 "BEST SELLER"), each with star rating and an Add to Bag button showing struck-through pricing.
**Fold 3:** Four-tile category nav band (Skin & Hair Oils, Essential Oils, Clean Skincare, Mosquito Repellents) as lifestyle-image tiles. Below, a "Clean Beauty Standards" editorial block: large circular badge graphic, headline "Our Commitment to Clean Beauty," body copy about formulating without 2,500+ harmful ingredients, and a "SHOP CLEAN SKINCARE" text link with arrow.
**CTA behavior:** Hero CTA ("TRY IT NOW!") is a static in-hero button, not a sticky bar — no sticky add-to-cart or navigation CTA visible on the homepage itself (sticky behavior appears only on PDPs, per the ad landing page screenshots).
**Trust signals:** Marquee strip repeats review-count and retail-distribution claims (500,000+ 5-star reviews, 10,000+ retail stores in 40 countries) directly under the hero. Certification badge row (7 icons) appears in fold 2. No star rating or guarantee copy is visible on the homepage itself — trust signals here are brand-level claims and certifications, not review-specific.
**Layout notes:** Hero image and headline are positioned left-aligned over a wide lifestyle photo; product callout badge ("NEW DROP") overlaps the product bottle in-image rather than as a separate UI element.

## Collection Page
**Fold 1:** Header "Shop All" with an 8-tile subcategory nav row (Clean Skincare, Skin & Hair Oils, Essential Oils, Diffusers, Lip Balms, Mosquito Repellents, Cotton, Supplements), each tile a lifestyle photo with a text label underneath. Product count shown ("172 products") with Filters (0) and Sort By ("Best selling") controls to the right. Grid begins with 4 products per row: Organic Jojoba Oil, Organic Castor Oil, Organic Rosehip Oil, Organic Argan Oil — each card has a "BEST SELLER" badge and a "SAVE X%" badge in opposite corners.
**Fold 2:** Grid continues with Mosquito Repellent Bracelets, Mosquito Repellent Patches, Organic Frankincense Essential Oil, 100% Pure Vitamin E Oil, then Organic Rosemary/Lavender Essential Oils, Organic Pumpkin Seed Oil, Non-GMO Jojoba Oil. Each card repeats the same layout: badge(s) top corners, star rating + review count, product name, subcategory label, Add to Bag button with live price (struck-through compare price where discounted).
**Fold 3:** Grid continues (Mosquito Repellent Patches variants, Organic Peppermint/Lemon Essential Oils, Organic Tea Tree Essential Oil, Tinted Lip Balm Set, Organic Eucalyptus Essential Oil, 8-Piece Essential Oil Aromatherapy Set). Layout and card structure unchanged from folds 1-2; no pagination or sort control visible yet at this scroll depth.
**Price display:** Compare-at/strikethrough pricing is shown directly on the product card (not just on click-through) for most discounted items, alongside a "SAVE X%" badge in the card's top-right corner. Star rating with numeric average (not just stars) and no explicit review count on the card itself (count appears only on the PDP).
**Layout notes:** 4-column grid on the captured viewport width. "BEST SELLER" and "SAVE %" badges use consistent pill styling across the grid. No per-unit pricing shown on collection cards (per-unit/oz pricing appears only on the PDP size selector).

## Cart (Drawer)
**Layout:** Slide-out drawer ("YOUR BAG") rather than a full cart page. Top of drawer shows a 3-tier progress row (Free Shipping Over $40 / Free Gift Over $55 / Free Tumbler Cup Over $100) with a progress bar and a live message: "You are $30.13 away from FREE SHIPPING!" Single line item shown: Organic Jojoba Oil (4oz), quantity stepper, price $9.87 struck through $12.99, with a "Delivery every 2 months with 24% discount" dropdown (subscription line item) and a remove/trash icon.
**CTA:** "CHECKOUT+ • $12.34" as the primary button, full-width, static within the drawer (drawer itself is the persistent element, not a separate sticky bar). Secondary text link below: "Continue without package protection."
**AOV elements:** Multi-tier free-shipping/free-gift/free-tumbler progress bar with live dollar-amount-remaining copy is the primary AOV mechanic. "Recommended For You" cross-sell module below the line item shows 2 products (Organic Castor Oil $11.99, Organic Argan Oil $16.99) each with its own "Add" button, in a swipeable carousel (arrow controls visible). "SavedBy Package Protection" add-on line item shown at $2.47 with an info icon, appears opt-in by default (bundled into the $12.34 checkout total based on button copy).
**Trust signals:** "500,000+ 5-Star Reviews" with a 5-star icon row appears at the very bottom of the drawer. No guarantee or returns copy visible within the cart drawer itself.

# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png (Google Ads Transparency Center results for Cliganic / A to Z Beauty, LLC)

**Ads visible:** Approximately 20 distinct ad units visible across both screenshots — mix of Search text ads, Shopping-style product ads, and a couple of video/image ad thumbnails. All attributed to advertiser "A to Z Beauty, LLC" (marked "Verified"), with a small number attributed to "Collegedunia Web Pvt. Ltd." (an affiliate/reseller).

**Offers and CTAs:** Repeated offer patterns across ads: "Free US shipping on $40+ orders," "60-Day Refund Policy," "$40+ Order Free Shipping." Several ads point to Amazon listings rather than cliganic.com directly (e.g. "Shop on Amazon," "Buy Jojoba Oil on Amazon - Free Prime Delivery," "100% Pure Jojoba Oil - Shop on Amazon"). Multiple ads cite Amazon star ratings and review counts (e.g. "4.5 ★★★★★ (9,458)", "(9,409)", "(9,467)", "(9,584)", "(9,752)"). Some ads route to cliganic.com/official-site or cliganic.com directly with "Shop Cliganic™ Oils Now" framing. One ad shows an "83%" discount callout on a multi-product bottle collage image.

**Headline themes:** Two dominant themes: (1) Amazon Best Seller / social proof framing ("Amazon Best Seller," "35,000+ 5-Star Reviews," "16,000+ reviewers," "42,000+ Reviews"), and (2) product-attribute framing for jojoba, castor, and argan oil (100% Pure, Cold-Pressed, USDA Organic, Non-GMO Certified, Non-Comedogenic, Hexane-Free, Vegan, Clinically tested). A secondary theme covers mosquito repellent products (bracelets, patches, stickers) with DEET-free/kid-safe messaging, distinct from the skin/hair oil line featured in the Meta ads.
**Visual approach:** Product ads show packaging shots (jojoba oil bottle + box) on plain backgrounds, sometimes paired with a multi-bottle collage. Two thumbnails in the set are video ad placements (still frame of a person speaking to camera) rather than static product shots.
**Gaps vs. Meta:** Meta ads route to the Cliganic-owned PDP on cliganic.com. A large share of the Google ad inventory instead routes to Amazon product/listing pages rather than the site, and leans heavily on Amazon-specific social proof (review counts, "Amazon Best Seller") that doesn't appear anywhere in the Meta ad creatives or the site-side PDP screenshots reviewed. Google ads also surface the mosquito repellent product line, which has no corresponding creative or landing page in the collected Meta ad set.

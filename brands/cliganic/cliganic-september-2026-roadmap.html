CRO Testing Roadmap

September 2026

Paid traffic reaches product pages with slow mobile rendering, mismatched promises, and defaults that need clearer disclosure. This roadmap first repairs page health and message continuity, then tests trust and transparency at the moments closest to purchase.

Testing Roadmap

What we test next

Eight focused changes across product pages, paid-traffic continuity, the homepage, and the cart drawer.

Slot 1Immediate Fix

PDP Mobile Load Time

Page: Organic Jojoba Oil PDP

The Organic Jojoba Oil PDP reaches Largest Contentful Paint in 33.9 seconds and becomes interactive in 60.1 seconds on mobile. That page is a primary paid-traffic destination, so every other improvement depends on it loading first. The proposed experience keeps the same PDP visible while the main product content and controls arrive promptly.

The same product page becomes usable before the shopper loses the thread. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2A/B Test

Review-Count Continuity

Page: Organic Jojoba Oil PDP

Paid search can cite Amazon review totals from 9,000+ to 42,000+, while the PDP shows 279 Verified Reviews. The unexplained difference creates a sharp trust gap at landing. The variation keeps the site rating and adds a clearly labeled Amazon-inclusive review line, using a verified aggregate before launch.

The variation explains the two review pools instead of leaving the shopper to reconcile them. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3Immediate Fix

Rosemary Trial-Stat Match

Page: Rosemary Repair Scalp & Strand Oil PDP

Offer-focused traffic arrives after seeing 95% shinier-looking hair with smoother ends after 14 days, but the PDP presents 93% stronger and 91% fuller after 28 days. Different claims and timeframes break the story at landing. The fix puts one verified trial result in both the traffic promise and the PDP experience.

The shopper sees the same verified trial story before and after the click. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4Immediate Fix

Jojoba Price and Positioning Match

Page: Organic Jojoba Oil PDP

Offer-focused traffic promises $12.99 and Amazon best-seller positioning, then lands on a 16 oz selection at $28.89 with no matching proof. The higher default price changes the offer at the moment of decision. The fix lands that traffic on the matching size and price, while carrying only a verified best-seller claim.

The selected size and price now continue the offer shown before the click. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

Package Protection Opt-In

Page: Cart Drawer

The cart adds SavedBy Package Protection at $2.47 into the checkout total by default, while removal sits in a small text link. This can make the total feel different from the basket the shopper built. The variation uses an unchecked, explicit opt-in and leaves the checkout total item-only until selected.

Protection becomes a clear choice, and the checkout total reflects that choice. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6A/B Test

Cart Guarantee

Page: Cart Drawer

The 60-Day Money-Back Guarantee appears below Add to Bag on the PDP, then disappears in the cart drawer. The final screen before checkout retains a large review claim but drops the clearest returns reassurance. The variation carries the guarantee into the cart beside the existing review proof.

The PDP guarantee stays visible through the final step before checkout. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7A/B Test

Homepage Hero Product

Page: Homepage

The captured homepage hero leads with Rosemary Repair, while Organic Jojoba Oil appears only in the Best Sellers section below. Jojoba is also the product emphasized by the collected paid traffic and carries a Best Seller badge on the homepage. The variation keeps Cliganic's hero structure but puts Organic Jojoba Oil, clean-and-natural positioning, and a direct product CTA first.

The first homepage story shifts from Rosemary Repair to the documented jojoba best seller. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8A/B Test

Subscription Clarity

Page: Organic Jojoba Oil PDP

Subscribe & Save is pre-selected on both captured jojoba landing states without a strong visual callout that the default is recurring. Directional community research also flags unwanted recurring-charge complaints, although the first-party review set does not confirm them. The variation keeps the selected default but adds a clear Subscription tag and the full benefit stack: save 24%, free shipping, and more subscriber benefits.

The recurring default is unmistakable, with its full value explained in the selected state. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Data Insights

What the evidence says

The strongest patterns are repeated across measured performance, captured storefront states, paid-traffic promises, and customer feedback.

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  • The Organic Jojoba Oil PDP records a 33.9s mobile LCP and a 60.1s Time to Interactive, making page speed the clearest measured funnel blocker.
  • Paid-traffic promises and landing-page content diverge on price, trial results, timeframes, and review volume. The mismatch appears across both collected paid channels.
  • Subscribe & Save is pre-selected on the PDP, while $2.47 Package Protection is included in the captured cart total by default. Both require clearer disclosure at high-intent moments.

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Rosemary Repair traffic promises 95% shinier-looking hair with smoother ends after 14 days. The landing page instead shows 93% stronger and 91% fuller after 28 days.
  • Jojoba traffic shows $12.99 and Amazon best-seller positioning, while the captured landing state defaults to 16 oz at $28.89 with no Amazon reference.
  • The captured jojoba landing states pre-select Subscribe & Save, with 24% off for 4 oz and 15% off for 16 oz.

Google Ads

  • Paid search cites Amazon review counts from 9,000+ to 42,000+, while the jojoba PDP shows 279 Verified Reviews.
  • A large share of the collected inventory routes to Amazon and uses Amazon-specific social proof, while site-directed messaging repeats free shipping and a 60-day refund policy.

Reviews and UGC

  • Customers repeatedly praise jojoba oil for lightweight absorption, multi-use versatility, long-term repeat use, and value.
  • Plastic rather than glass packaging is the most repeated complaint in the collected review set.
  • Shipping damage, delivery delays, and batch-to-batch scent consistency appear as operational or product-quality concerns, not page-level test ideas.

PageSpeed Insights

  • The mobile homepage records a 0.32 performance score, 7.2s LCP, 3.9s FCP, and 1,990ms Total Blocking Time.
  • The Organic Jojoba Oil PDP records a 0.28 performance score, 33.9s LCP, 18.4s FCP, 1,650ms Total Blocking Time, and 60.1s Time to Interactive.
  • CLS is 0 on both pages. The measured problem is load and render blocking, not layout shift.

Site Screenshots

  • The captured homepage hero features Rosemary Repair. Organic Jojoba Oil appears after the hero in the Best Sellers module with a Best Seller badge.
  • The collection exposes 172 products with Filters (0), while product cards show ratings and prices but no visible review count.
  • The cart uses a three-tier free-shipping and gift progress bar, yet the 60-Day Money-Back Guarantee shown on PDPs is absent from the drawer.

Social and Community Research

  • Third-party sources directionally surface recurring-charge and customer-service concerns, but the collected first-party reviews do not confirm explicit subscription-billing complaints.
  • Third-party retailer reviews repeat the plastic-packaging complaint found independently in the first-party review set.

CRO Testing Roadmap

August 2026

1620 Workwear has strong product conviction, but sale signals, product availability, and the purchase path are not working as one system. This roadmap focuses on rebuilding continuity from the first promise through the final buying decision.

What the Data Shows

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Paid testimonial traffic reaches a sold-out Size 38 / Meteorite variant with a PREORDER CTA and March, April, and June 2026 production milestones that had all passed by July 25.
  • The in-stock Size 40 / Meteorite variant presents ADD TO CART, the BUY 3 & GET 1 FREE offer, and trust badges in fold one, creating the strongest observed message match.
  • Durability and loyalty-focused creative leads to a $132 CLOSEOUT - FINAL SALE product with several sizes crossed out and no trust badges visible in fold one.

Google Ads

  • Paid search promotes "Labor Day Sale, 20% Off" while the homepage promotes "Retirement Sale, Up to 50% Off."
  • The Full Tech Work Hoodie shopping listing renders "[PRICE]" instead of the actual product price.
  • Search listings show a 4.8 advertiser rating, while observed PDP ratings are 4.5. The former is a seller aggregate, but the distinction is not visible in the journey.

PageSpeed Insights

  • The Double Knee Utility Pant 2.0 PDP scores 41/100 on mobile, with a 42.9s LCP, 43.4s TTI, 710ms TBT, and 7,986 KiB page size.
  • The homepage scores 62/100 on mobile, with an 11.7s LCP, 21.9s TTI, 130ms TBT, and 5,012 KiB page size.
  • The PDP records 20 long tasks compared with 9 on the homepage, making the direct paid destination the more urgent performance problem.

Site Screenshots

  • The homepage hero and announcement bar promise up to 50% off, while the adjacent product grid shows regular prices with no compare-at pricing on the visible cards.
  • Six collection products are labeled CLOSEOUT - FINAL SALE. Five of those six show no visual price differentiation, while the Full Tech Sweatpant is the only visible compare-at exception.
  • The cart shows a $228 Double Knee Utility Pant 2.0, guarantee strip, checkout button, Shop Pay, and PayPal. It includes no upsell, cross-sell, bundle reminder, or threshold prompt.

Live Site Review

  • The Double Knee Utility Pant 2.0 carries a 4.5 rating across 419 reviews, with 75% five-star ratings and recent praise for durability, pocket design, and construction use.
  • The Single Knee Utility Pant 2.0 carries a 4.5 rating across 311 reviews, with 84% five-star ratings and a customer note that sizing required moving up.
  • The observed product experience combines strong review proof with inconsistent availability, trust placement, and purchase access across variants.

Context Notes

  • The stale preorder schedule gives no indication whether the June production batch shipped, was delayed, or was cancelled.
  • No standalone review source, competitor research, email campaigns, inspiration sites, or standalone PDP screenshots were collected for this audit.

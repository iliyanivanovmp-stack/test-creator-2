Press section: "FEATURED IN:" with logos — Men's Journal, National Fisherman, Pro Tool Reviews, Outside, ABC, GearJunkie.

**CTA behavior:** Red "SHOP NOW" button in fold 1 hero. No sticky header or CTA bar. No visible persistent CTA on scroll.

**Trust signals:** "100,000+ PANTS SOLD", "GUARANTEED FOR LIFE", "MADE IN THE USA" — all three visible in fold 1 below the hero. Press logos visible in fold 3. No star ratings or review count on homepage outside of product cards in fold 2+.

**Layout notes:** Hero takes full viewport. The retirement sale messaging is dominant but the product grid immediately below shows no sale prices, creating a disconnect between the promotional hero and the browsing experience. "Pant Comparison Guide" banner in fold 3 suggests the lineup complexity requires active navigation support.

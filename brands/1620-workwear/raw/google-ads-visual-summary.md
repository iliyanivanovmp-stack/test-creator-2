# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads.png
**Ads visible:** Multiple formats — search text ads, display image ads, shopping/product listing ads

**Headline themes:**
- "Guaranteed for Life - Pants Last Up to 5x Longer"
- "1620 Workwear - The Best Workwear. Period."
- "American Made Workwear and Gear"
- "Rated Best Overall Work Pants - Labor Day Sale - 20% Off"

**Offers and CTAs:**
- "Labor Day Sale - 20% Off" visible in one search ad (conflicts with homepage "Retirement Sale up to 50% off" — two different sale names and discount levels running simultaneously across channels)
- No specific dollar amounts or free-shipping offers visible in text ads
- Shopping ads show [Price] placeholder on one product listing (Full Tech Work Hoodie, Hi Vis) — price is not rendering in the ad unit, shown as "[Price] / By Google"

**Visual approach:**
- Shopping/display ads show clean product-on-white imagery: pants in multiple colorways, a neon green/black hoodie
- One display ad shows a lifestyle image of a person in work pants seated outdoors
- One display ad is a multi-product grid showing pants in various colors with the 1620 logo
- One display ad shows a text-heavy layout listing product categories (Men's Work Pants, Workwear, Tops, Trousers, Utility Workwear Pants)

**Rating signals:**
- "4.8 advertiser rating" cited in two search ads (with 554 reviews in one instance)
- This differs from the 4.5-star rating shown on product PDPs — the Google rating appears to be a brand/seller aggregate, not a product-level rating

**Gaps vs. Meta:**
- Meta ads use long-form testimonial copy as the primary hook; Google search ads use feature-benefit headlines ("Guaranteed for Life", "5x Longer", "Best Workwear. Period.")
- Meta ads do not mention a sale; Google ads feature "Labor Day Sale - 20% Off" — creating inconsistent promotional messaging across channels
- Meta LP for Ad 1 shows a preorder/backorder state; Google shopping ads show these same products as available, which may mislead clicks
- The broken price rendering ("[Price]" placeholder) on the hoodie shopping ad means that product is showing without a price, which typically suppresses click-through on Shopping placements

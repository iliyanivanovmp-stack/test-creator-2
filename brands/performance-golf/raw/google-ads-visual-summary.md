# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png (Google Ads Transparency Center grid view)

**Ads visible:** Approximately 20+ ad units visible across the two screenshots, mixing responsive search ads, display/image ads, and shopping-style product cards. Several tiles are blank/collapsed placeholders (labeled "Collapsed ad on mobile / Collapsed ad on desktop / Expanded ad"). All ads attributed to advertiser "Blackfish Media, LLC" (Verified), running under the Performance Golf brand.

**Headline themes:** Recurring angles include: slice-fix / no swing changes needed ("New Slice-Fix Driver - No Swing Changes Needed," "Fix your slice forever (guaranteed)"), distance for seniors ("First Ever Senior Super 7 Wood," "Slower Swing, More Distance," "Over age 50? Do this one thing..."), putter accuracy ("The World's Straightest Putter - Make More Putts, Guaranteed," "Not Another Zero Torque Putter," "Built For The Weekend Golfer"), and quick-fix drills ("5 Min Drill Adds 15-25 Yards," "Simple Pendulum Technique," "Genius Trick To Hit Straight," "Try this weird 'Finger Fix' to make perfect contact today").

**Offers and CTAs:** Visible CTAs include "Learn More," "Visit Site," "Open," and product price tiles ("[Price] Performance Golf"). One display ad shows "SALE 20% OFF" styling on a fairway hybrid creative ("THE NEW FAIRWAY HYBRID REPLACES YOUR 3, 5 & 7 WOODS," "3 Wood Distance. 7 Wood Swing," "Fastest Way To Shave 6 Strokes Without Changing Your Swing (New Tri-Fusion Technology)"). Another shows "SUMMER SALE 20% OFF - Only 3 Days" styling (partially visible, text cut off).
**Visual approach:** Display/image ads use dark backgrounds with red/orange accent colors and bold white headline text overlaid on product renders or lifestyle golf photography (e.g., person swinging club on a course). Product-card style ads show a plain product image with price and "By Google" / advertiser label. Search-style ads are plain text with blue headline link, advertiser name/domain, and body description.

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Google Ads surfaces additional angles not seen in the three collected Meta ad creatives — putter messaging ("World's Straightest Putter," "SQ Putter" family), swing-drill/instructional hooks ("5 Min Drill," "Pendulum Technique," "Finger Fix"), and senior-specific fairway wood messaging in text-ad form (Meta Ad 1 covers the senior fairway wood angle via image/LP but Google runs multiple text-ad variants of it). The SF2 slice-fix driver messaging (Meta Ads 2 & 3) is echoed in Google's "New Slice-Fix Driver" text ad and the "3 Wood Distance. 7 Wood Swing" display ad extends the Tri-Fusion technology claim to a different product framing (fairway hybrid replacing 3/5/7 woods) not present in the collected Meta ads.

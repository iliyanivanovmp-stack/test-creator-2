Mobile performance breaks across home and product journeys while high-value products lack consistent proof, offer framing, and cross-sell support. This roadmap stabilizes the shopping experience first, then closes the message and merchandising gaps around the products carrying the business.

Testing roadmap

Eight focused opportunities across mobile performance, product proof, offer clarity, and merchandising.

Slot 1Immediate Fix

Club PDP Mobile Stability

Page: Product detail pages, all club templates

The 357 product page scored 21/100 on mobile, with CLS at 0.637 and time to interactive at 43.4 seconds. The buy box and sticky purchase bar sit inside this unstable load experience. The corrected page reserves their space from first paint so selectors and the Add to Cart action stay fixed while content loads.

* The product buy box holds its shape while every purchase control loads. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2Immediate Fix

Homepage Mobile Stability

Page: Homepage

The homepage scored 20/100 on mobile, with CLS at 0.638 and time to interactive at 46.2 seconds. Its hero, trust row, and technology sections load into the same unstable environment. The corrected experience keeps the hero and primary action locked in place from first paint.

* The hero, primary action, and proof row appear in one stable load. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3A/B Test

Cart Drawer Cross-Sell

Page: Cart drawer, site-wide

The cart drawer shows “YOU MAY ALSO LIKE” with no products beneath it, while only 6.0% of club orders contain more than one club. Multi-club orders average $556.69 compared with $346.03 for single-club orders. The variation turns the empty area into a compact complementary-club recommendation without changing the existing cart.

* The empty recommendation area becomes a compact complementary-club offer. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4A/B Test

357 Landing Page Message Match

Page: 357 Fairway and Hybrid Landing Page

Offer-focused traffic arrives with the promise that this club is built for golfers over 55, but the first three landing-page folds lead only with Tri-Fusion technology. The age-specific reason to believe disappears at the handoff. The variation restores “Built for Golfers Over 55” beside the existing technology headline and video.

* The hero keeps its Tri-Fusion story and restores the “Golfers Over 55” promise above the fold. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

RS1 and SF2 Review Proof

Page: RS1 Putter and SF2 Driver product detail pages

RS1 and SF2 produce 70.0% of gross revenue, yet neither captured page shows an on-site review widget. The lower-revenue 357 page already places a 4.8 rating and review count inside its buy box. The variation brings the same proof pattern below each flagship title once verified on-site ratings and counts are available.

* Both flagship buy boxes gain a visible rating row beside the purchase decision. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6A/B Test

SF2 $100 Off Message Match

Page: SF2 Driver Landing Page

Offer-focused paid traffic promises $100 off, while the captured landing-page folds show $299 reduced from $399 without stating the discount amount. The math is present, but the promise is not repeated. The variation places a clear “$100 OFF” callout beside the existing price without changing the offer.

* The existing $299 and $399 price treatment gains the exact “$100 OFF” promise. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7A/B Test

RS1 Collection Discount Badge

Page: Golf Clubs Collection

The 357 and SF2 collection cards carry savings badges, while the RS1 card shows only “From $399.00.” That leaves the highest-revenue product as the visual exception in the same grid. The variation gives RS1 the same top-left savings treatment already used by neighboring clubs, based on the live discount applied at checkout.

* RS1 gains the same top-left savings treatment already used by adjacent club cards. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8A/B Test

Homepage Hero Offer Framing

Page: Homepage

The homepage hero leads with the RS1 technology story but shows no price, discount, or urgency, while every captured offer-focused landing page leads with those signals. Returning visitors can move from a direct-response offer to a colder homepage experience. The variation adds a concise sale line beneath the existing headline while preserving the putter image and “SEE HOW IT WORKS” action.

* The existing RS1 hero gains a concise price and urgency line between the headline and CTA. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

What the data says

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Senior-focused creative promises a club for golfers over 55, but the captured 357 landing-page folds replace that framing with Tri-Fusion technology.
  • Offer-focused creative promises $100 off the SF2. The shared landing page shows $299 reduced from $399 but no explicit “$100 OFF” callout in the captured folds.
  • Paid landing pages use direct-response structures, bonus stacks, and guarantees that differ sharply from the organic homepage experience.

Google Ads

  • Search creative repeats senior distance, slice-fix, and no-swing-change angles across the club range.
  • Google also introduces putter and instructional angles that were not present in the paid-social sample.
  • One display treatment positions the fairway hybrid as replacing a 3, 5, and 7 wood.

Reviews and UGC

  • 357 reviewers repeatedly praise bag simplification, easier contact, and forgiveness for seniors and higher handicappers.
  • Distance underperformance against the advertising promise is the most repeated complaint theme.
  • Shaft durability and shipping damage appear unprompted in the review sample.
  • No review data was collected for RS1 or SF2, despite the two products producing 70.0% of gross revenue.

PageSpeed Insights

  • The homepage scored 20/100 on mobile, with 5.6s LCP, 0.638 CLS, and 46.2s time to interactive.
  • The 357 PDP scored 21/100 on mobile, with 4.6s LCP, 0.637 CLS, and 43.4s time to interactive.
  • Total blocking time measured 1,100ms on the homepage and 1,780ms on the PDP.

Site Screenshots

  • The homepage delays its 4.8 rating, 1M+ subscribers, and 800K clubs sold proof until the third fold.
  • The cart drawer renders the “YOU MAY ALSO LIKE” heading with no products beneath it.
  • The RS1 collection card lacks the savings badge shown on the 357, SF2, and ONE.1 cards.
  • The 357 buy box includes visible rating proof, selectors, a full-width Add to Cart action, and a sticky purchase bar deeper on the page.

Shopify Business Summary

  • Orders rose 30% week over week while AOV fell from $282.09 to $196.60 and weekly revenue stayed between $555K and $637K.
  • RS1 produces 49.4% of gross revenue. RS1 and SF2 together produce 70.0%.
  • Only 6.0% of club orders contain more than one club. Those orders average $556.69 compared with $346.03 for single-club orders.
  • Checkouts from $300 to $500 represent 46.9% of abandonments but 28.2% of completed orders.

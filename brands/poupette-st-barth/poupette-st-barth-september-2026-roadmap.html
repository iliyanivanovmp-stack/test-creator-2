Poupette St Barth pairs a strong visual identity with a shopping journey that hides trust, value, and purchase actions at key moments. This roadmap brings those signals closer to the decision while improving the speed and continuity of the experience.

Testing Roadmap

Eight focused experiments across product detail pages, paid landing experiences, collection browsing, and the homepage.

Slot 1A/B Test

PDP Review Rating

Product Detail Page

The captured buy box shows no review rating or count across the product page. Visitors reach the price and purchase action without customer proof. The variation adds a live star rating and review count beneath the product title.

A live rating and review count appear between the product title and price. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2A/B Test

PDP Purchase Reassurance

Product Detail Page

Free shipping appears below the purchase action, while secured payment and simple returns sit in the footer. The strongest reassurance arrives after the buying decision. The variation places all three existing trust messages directly beneath Add to Cart.

Free shipping, secured payment, and simple returns move directly beneath Add to Cart. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3A/B Test

Paid Landing Page Message Match

Paid Social Search Results Landing Page

Resort-wear paid traffic arrives on a search page for "sasha" with filters and 22 results, but no headline that continues the promise. The first screen feels disconnected from the reason for the visit. The variation opens with "Bring a little Saint Barth wherever you are" and a campaign image before the existing search results.

The paid promise becomes the first message, while the existing product results remain below. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4A/B Test

PDP Load Experience

Product Detail Page

The audited product page records an 18.1-second LCP and 23.3-second Time to Interactive on mobile. The buying experience appears too late. The variation shows the primary product image and buy box first, with lower content following afterward.

The primary dress image and buy box become visible before lower-page content. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

Homepage Load Experience

Homepage

The homepage records a 13.7-second LCP and 17.1-second Time to Interactive on mobile. The first campaign moment takes too long to arrive. The variation keeps the same hero, headline, and Shop link, but makes that first screen visible sooner.

The existing campaign hero becomes the first complete visual moment. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6A/B Test

Collection Offer Visibility

New Collection

The captured New Collection page shows 103 products with single prices and no offer badges, while the announcement bar promotes 10% off a first order. The value message disappears during product scanning. The variation adds "10% OFF FIRST ORDER" to each product tile.

The existing first-order offer becomes visible on every collection tile. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7A/B Test

Sticky Add to Cart

Product Detail Page

Add to Cart scrolls out of view before shoppers reach shipping, returns, and FAQ content. Buying then requires a return trip to the buy box. The variation keeps the product, selected size, price, and purchase action visible in a compact sticky bar.

The purchase action remains available while shoppers read lower-page details. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8A/B Test

Homepage Hero Offer

Homepage

The captured hero shows "THE SEASON'S ESSENTIALS" and a Shop link, but no product, price, or first-order offer. The visual is strong, yet the buying reason stays generic. The variation adds a featured style, its price, and the existing 10% first-order offer beneath the headline.

A featured style, price, and first-order offer turn the hero into a specific shopping invitation. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  1. 01

    Trust arrives too late

    Visible captures across the homepage, paid landing page, collection, PDP, and cart show no review count, star rating, or guarantee near the decision. The trust signals found sit in the footer or lower FAQ content.

  2. 02

    Mobile pages load too slowly

    The homepage and PDP both miss the good LCP threshold by a wide margin. The PDP is the more severe experience, with an 18.1-second LCP and 23.3-second Time to Interactive.

  3. 03

    The paid promise breaks at arrival

    General resort-wear creative leads into a raw search-results page for "sasha." The destination opens with search controls and filters instead of the message that earned the visit.

What the Data Shows

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Paid-social creative uses hand-made, limited-run, general resort-wear, and Sasha best-seller stories.
  • General resort-wear traffic lands on a search-results page for "sasha" with 22 results, filters, and no matching headline in the captured first screen.
  • Other paid-social landing pages were not collected, so their message match remains unconfirmed.

Google Ads

  • Creative centers on "Iconic Prints, Timeless Cuts," seasonal collections, and a girls' clothing line.
  • Offers include free worldwide shipping, easy returns, and 10% off a first order.
  • The hand-made and best-seller framing used in paid social does not appear in the reviewed Google copy.

PageSpeed Insights

  • The homepage scored 64/100 on mobile, with a 13.7-second LCP and 17.1-second Time to Interactive.
  • The PDP scored 62/100 on mobile, with an 18.1-second LCP, 23.3-second Time to Interactive, and 8.8-second Speed Index.
  • Layout shift was not an issue. CLS measured 0.002 on the homepage and 0 on the PDP.

Site Screenshots

  • No review count, star rating, or guarantee badge appears in the captured homepage, collection, PDP, paid landing page, or cart views.
  • The New Collection page shows 103 products with single straight pricing and no sale or first-order offer badge.
  • The PDP Add to Cart button scrolls out of view before the shipping, returns, and FAQ content.
  • The captured cart shows no upsell, cross-sell, bundle, shipping-progress, or trust treatment.

## Reviews Note

Reviews were collected by the user but are not included in this project. The brand currently has very little review volume, so the collected reviews were judged not useful for audit purposes and were deliberately excluded to avoid polluting the research context. The audit should note reviews were collected but not used, rather than treating reviews as a missing data source.

## Ad 3 Note

Ad 3 (Core Cut walk-behind gas saw) links directly to the homepage rather than a dedicated landing page. No separate Ad 3 landing page screenshots were collected — the homepage screenshots in this folder serve as Ad 3's landing destination. This is confirmed intentional, not a data gap.

Conversion testing roadmap

Concrete Tools Direct September 2026

The store has strong contractor-focused offers and meaningful trust advantages, but slow mobile pages and mismatched landing experiences keep those strengths from reaching shoppers at the right moment. This roadmap starts with speed, then brings product relevance, price confidence, trust, and buying actions closer to the decision.

Testing sequence

Where we start

Eight focused changes across speed, paid-traffic relevance, PDP confidence, collection discovery, and checkout entry.

Slot 1Immediate Fix

Homepage and PDP Page Speed

Pages: Homepage and product detail pages

The homepage reaches LCP at 28.0 seconds and the sampled PDP at 12.8 seconds on mobile. That delay blocks the product and purchase experience before the store's strongest offers can work. The proposed experience prioritizes the homepage hero and PDP gallery so each core surface aims for Google's good LCP range.

The homepage hero and PDP gallery become the first meaningful content to load. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2A/B Test

Walk-Behind Saw Landing Destination

Journey: Homepage to walk-behind concrete saws collection

Offer-focused traffic for the Core Cut 11.7HP walk-behind saw currently lands on a homepage whose first three folds show no Core Cut product or brand. The landing experience does not continue the product promise that earned the click. The variation sends that traffic directly to the verified Walk-Behind Concrete Saws collection.

Saw-focused traffic lands where walk-behind saws are immediately visible. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3A/B Test

Best Price Guarantee on Mixer PDPs

Pages: Imer Minuteman II and Imer Workman II 250

Price-guarantee-focused traffic reaches two mixer PDPs that show stock, shipping, tax, and pricing information but omit the Best Price Guarantee badge used on product cards. The page drops the exact promise that introduced the products. The variation places the existing guarantee treatment beside each PDP price.

The existing Best Price Guarantee treatment sits beside the price on both mixer PDPs. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4A/B Test

Sticky Add To Cart on PDP

Page: MudMixer Evolution Ultimate Bundle

The orange Add To Cart button sits below the five-item bundle list and scrolls out of view when a shopper reaches Product Details. The purchase action disappears while the shopper continues reading. The variation keeps a compact Add To Cart bar visible after the original button leaves the viewport.

A compact purchase bar stays visible while shoppers read below the original CTA. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

Trust Signals Beside the CTA

Page: MudMixer Evolution Ultimate Bundle

Authorized Dealer, Google Top Quality Store, BBB Accredited, secure checkout, and 90-day returns all sit below the purchase button in the next fold. Those trust signals arrive after the main decision. The variation moves the badge row and 90-day return line directly beneath Add To Cart while leaving the testimonial and phone CTA below.

Dealer badges, secure checkout, and 90-day returns move directly beneath Add To Cart. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6A/B Test

Homepage Star Rating

Page: Homepage

The live site holds a 4.90-star average across 61 verified reviews, but the captured header and hero show no rating or review count. The available proof appears only in a floating card on the next fold. The variation adds a static 4.90-star, 61-review signal between the hero headline and Shop Equipment button.

The verified rating becomes a static proof point between the homepage headline and CTA. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7A/B Test

Equipment Collection Default Sort

Page: Equipment collection

The 641-product Equipment collection defaults to Price, high to low, placing $4,095, $3,995, and $3,695 bundles first. The opening product row is shaped by maximum price rather than product popularity. The variation keeps the existing controls and changes only the default to Best Selling.

The collection opens on Best Selling while the current filters and navigation remain unchanged. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8A/B Test

Checkout Entry Reassurance and Cross-Sell

Page: Order summary and Express Checkout entry

The captured $4,095 checkout entry shows express-pay buttons and three trust icons, but no cross-sell or free-shipping message. It leaves no visible reason to add to the order before the Contact form begins. The variation adds one related-accessory row and restates the 90-day return guarantee beside the existing trust area. The exact accessory needs catalog confirmation.

One catalog-confirmed accessory and the 90-day return guarantee appear before Contact and Delivery. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Cross-source pattern

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  • Mobile page speed is the strongest quantified issue. Homepage LCP is 28.0s and PDP LCP is 12.8s, with Performance scores of 32 and 34 out of 100.
  • Paid-social price and product promises break at the landing page. Mixer PDPs omit the Best Price Guarantee, while saw-focused traffic reaches a homepage with no matching Core Cut product in the first three folds.
  • The PDP purchase action disappears during deeper reading, while dealer badges, secure checkout, and 90-day returns sit below the CTA. The captured checkout entry also has no cross-sell or free-shipping message.

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Paid-social creative leads with a Best Price Guarantee, but the linked Imer Minuteman II and Imer Workman II 250 buy boxes do not display that guarantee.
  • Saw-focused traffic lands on the homepage, where no Core Cut product or brand appears in the first three folds.
  • The Imer Workman II 250 PDP supports value with $3,649 to $3,199 pricing and Save $450, but still omits the explicit guarantee.

Google Ads

  • The captured Shopping creative uses specification-led catalog titles with no visible lifestyle or value-proposition copy.
  • Actual prices rendered as placeholders in the captures, so price-message consistency with the site and paid-social creative cannot be confirmed.
  • A repeating tile for 1,200 lbs of free Quikrete shows that the on-site bundle mechanic carries into at least one feed title.

Reviews and UGC

  • The live site shows a 4.90-star average across 61 verified reviews.
  • Review volume was judged insufficient for reliable theme extraction, so no customer-love or frustration themes are reported.

PageSpeed Insights

  • Mobile homepage Performance is 32/100, with 28.0s LCP, 1,330ms TBT, 7.0s FCP, and 28.4s Time to Interactive.
  • The sampled mobile PDP scores 34/100, with 12.8s LCP, 960ms TBT, 7.8s FCP, and 24.9s Time to Interactive.
  • CLS is 0 on both pages. The measured problem is load and render time, not layout instability.
  • No desktop PageSpeed data was collected.

Site Screenshots

  • The homepage shows no sitewide rating in its header or hero. The only review-related element in the captured first two folds is a floating review card.
  • The Equipment collection contains 641 products and defaults to Price, high to low, placing $4,095, $3,995, and $3,695 bundles first.
  • The representative PDP places Add To Cart below five included bundle items. Dealer badges, secure checkout, and 90-day returns appear in the next fold.
  • The captured checkout entry contains express-pay buttons and trust icons, but no upsell, cross-sell, or free-shipping-threshold message.

Live Site

  • The homepage reports 61 verified reviews at a 4.90-star average, but the captured hero does not surface that proof.
  • The store offers financing up to $100,000 on PDPs, while that financing message is absent from the captured homepage hero and collection page.

Competitor Analysis

  • Neither researched competitor visibly matched Concrete Tools Direct's 90-day return and 1-year limited warranty stack.
  • Neither researched competitor visibly offered financing up to $100,000.
  • Concrete Tool Supply features free shipping on its homepage, while Contractors Direct shows star ratings on select products.

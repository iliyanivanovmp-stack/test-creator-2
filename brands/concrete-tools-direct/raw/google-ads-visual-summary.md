# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png

**Ads visible:** Both screenshots show a Google Shopping / Product Listing Ad grid (adstransparency.google.com results view) rather than text search ads. google-ads-1.png shows roughly 26 product tiles; google-ads-2.png shows roughly 14 product tiles, with some products repeating across the two screenshots (e.g. an Imer Cement Mixer, "1,200 lbs Free Quikrete" bundle). Format is exclusively Shopping/product-grid — no search text ads, display ads, or video ads are visible in either capture.

**Headline themes:** Product titles are literal and spec-driven (brand + product type + key spec), e.g. "Tracker 1624 | 16 Cu. Ft. Swi...", "BravePro Hardened...", "Ironton Submersible...", "Klutch Cement Mixer | Electri...", "9 Cu Ft Cement Mixer | Brave |...", "Core Bore Drill 3"-6" -...", "6 Cu Ft Towable Mortar Mixer |...", "WerkMaster | Raider XL5 23...". No lifestyle or benefit-driven headline copy — titles read as catalog/spec listings, consistent with standard Shopping ad formatting pulled from product feed titles.

**Offers and CTAs:** One tile in google-ads-1.png is a text/site-link style ad rather than a product tile: "Concrete Tools Direct - Official Website ... Your Trusted Tool Partner" with sub-links (About Us, Contact Us, Financing Plan, Site Equipment, Best Sellers). A repeating bundle offer appears in both screenshots: "1,200 lbs Free Quikrete w/..." as its own product tile. No visible discount percentages, promo codes, or urgency CTAs in either screenshot; all shopping tiles show a "[Price]" placeholder rather than a rendered price (the actual price value was not visible/captured in these screenshots) and a "Concrete Tools Direct ..." merchant byline, with a few tiles labeled "By Google" instead.

**Visual approach:** Standard Shopping grid layout — each tile is a white card with a product photo on a plain/light background (mixers, saws, grinders, drills, hand tools, generators, wheelbarrows, diamond blades, gloves bundled in via a small "+" badge on some images), a blue product title link, a price line, and a merchant name line. Visual style matches the product photography used on-site (e.g. the same Imer mixer + free-gloves callout images seen on the PDP and Meta ad landing pages).

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Meta ads use lifestyle-adjacent ad copy with a consistent "Worried about the cost of high-quality tools? Our best price guarantee..." value-prop message and single-product creative (mixers, saws) with a "Shop Now" CTA. Google Shopping ads carry no equivalent value-prop copy — they are catalog-style listings driven by product feed data, spanning a much broader product range (mixers, saws, drills, grinders, generators, wheelbarrows, gloves, blades) rather than the two mixer SKUs and one saw SKU featured on Meta. The "Free Quikrete" and "Free gloves" bundle mechanics seen on-site and in Meta landing pages do surface as their own Shopping tile ("1,200 lbs Free Quikrete w/..."), suggesting some consistency in the bundle-as-offer approach across channels. No price values were visible in the captured screenshots, so price-message consistency between Google and Meta/site cannot be confirmed.

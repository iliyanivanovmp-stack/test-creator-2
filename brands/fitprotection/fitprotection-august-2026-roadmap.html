Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Paid-social creative leads with RV glass protection, rock-chip prevention, and hold-strength promises, while the homepage hero leads with cover retention.
  • RV-focused traffic reaches a homepage configurator that preselects UTV.
  • The 537+ review signal appears in fold 2, after LOXX credentials fill the first fold.

Google Ads

  • Video creative shows the line “THAT'S WHERE FIT PROTECTION Comes In” without a specific benefit, hold figure, or vehicle type.
  • A Yamaha Wolverine shopping listing displays “[Price]” instead of a rendered product price.

Page Speed Insights

  • The homepage scores 68/100, with a 4.4s LCP, 460ms Total Blocking Time, and 22.0s TTI.
  • The PDP scores 51/100, with an 8.7s LCP, 580ms Total Blocking Time, and 23.9s TTI.
  • CLS is 0 on both measured pages.

Reviews and UGC

  • 29 Judge.me verified reviews in the collected dataset average 4.17/5.
  • Customers repeatedly praise exact fit, highway hold in crosswinds, and easy installation and removal.
  • Three 1-star reviews cite customer support as the sole reason for their low rating.
  • One buyer reported $183.86 in tariffs on a $465 order with no advance notice.

Site Screenshots

  • The homepage fit finder is the primary CTA and no sticky desktop CTA appears across the captured folds.
  • The PDP places 537+ verified reviews above the price and uses a four-step configuration flow with a sticky “ADD PROTECTION · $364” bar.
  • The cart shows an All-LOXX upgrade and an already-unlocked free-shipping bar, but no accessory cross-sell.

Live Site Research

  • Support is limited to info@fitprotection.com with Mon-Fri 8a-5p MST hours. No phone number, chat widget, or named contact was visible.
  • Vinyl cleaner costs $25, removable magnets cost $39, and the extended warranty costs $39. None appeared in the captured PDP or cart experience.
  • A $20 off first-cover email capture exists but was not visible in the captured homepage folds.

Competitor Research

  • FIT Protection sits at the premium end of the reviewed market, with model-specific cuts, marine-grade materials, and LOXX fastening as differentiators.
  • DIY alternatives were found under $150, while universal-fit strap or velcro covers were found at $60 to $90.

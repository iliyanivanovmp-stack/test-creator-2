FIT Protection testing roadmap

August 2026

Paid traffic arrives with glass-protection intent but meets a UTV-first retention message and a slow product experience. The roadmap aligns the first click, puts customer proof earlier, and removes doubt at the point of purchase.

Testing ideas

Eight focused ways to connect the promise, the product fit, and the confidence needed to buy.

Slot 1A/B Test

Homepage Hero Headline

Homepage ↗

Rock-chip-focused paid traffic lands on a hero about losing a cover. The problem that earned the click disappears at the handoff. The variation keeps the same product image and layout, but leads with windshield protection.

* The hero keeps its familiar structure while the headline carries the windshield-damage promise into the landing experience. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2A/B Test

RV Configurator Default

Homepage ↗

RV-focused traffic currently enters a configurator pre-selected to UTV. That forces the shopper to correct the first field before choosing a brand or model. The variation opens with RV already selected while the rest of the three-tap flow stays familiar.

* RV replaces UTV as the opening category for RV-focused traffic, removing one correction before Brand and Model. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3A/B Test

Reviews in Fold One

Homepage ↗

The first fold relies on LOXX credentials while the 537+ review signal appears one fold later. A new visitor sees industrial proof before customer proof. The variation places the 4.17/5 rating beneath the hero message and leaves the LOXX credential row intact.

* The verified review strip moves into the hero message column, ahead of the existing LOXX credential tiles. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4Immediate Fix

PDP Page Speed

Product detail pages

The product page records an 8.7s LCP and 23.9s TTI on mobile, delaying the main image and the configuration flow. The page that hosts the purchase controls is the slowest measured surface. The fix prioritizes the hero product image and makes the buy box interactive sooner without changing the customer-facing layout.

* The same PDP appears with the main product image and purchase controls prioritized instead of waiting behind the rest of the page. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

Support Contact Visibility

Product detail pages

The PDP trust row promises shipping, warranty, and a free rebuild, but it does not show a route to a person. Three separate one-star reviews cite no phone number, no named contact, or unanswered email. The variation adds a visible team contact and support hours beside the existing assurances.

* A fourth reassurance item makes the existing support email and hours visible before purchase. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6A/B Test

International Duty Notice

Product detail pages

No import-duty notice appears on the PDP, cart, or checkout in the captured experience. One reviewer reported $183.86 in tariffs on a $465 order with no advance notice. The variation adds one clear international-order sentence beneath the shipping reassurance before the shopper commits.

* A concise duty notice appears directly below the shipping and warranty row for international buyers. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7A/B Test

Cart Accessories Cross-Sell

Cart ↗

The cart offers only the $409 All-LOXX swap, while free shipping is already unlocked by the $364 base product. Confirmed accessories do not appear in the cart or PDP. The variation places the $39 extended warranty beneath the LOXX upgrade with one direct add-to-order action.

* A single 2-Year Warranty offer appears below the existing All-LOXX upgrade, with its own add-to-order action. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8A/B Test

Desktop Sticky CTA

Homepage ↗

The homepage configurator is the only primary action and disappears once desktop visitors scroll into product and LOXX content. No persistent CTA appears in the captured desktop folds. The variation adds a compact Find My Cover button to the navigation after the configurator leaves view.

* A compact Find My Cover control joins the desktop navigation only after the original configurator scrolls away. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

What the sources show

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Paid-social creative uses RV glass protection, rock chip prevention, and LOXX hold claims, while every click lands on the same UTV-first homepage.
  • The homepage configurator pre-selects UTV even when the arriving promise is RV-focused.
  • The 3,000+ lb total RV hold claim does not reappear on the homepage, which instead shows 300+ lb per fastener and 850 lb mag.

Google Ads

  • Visible video creative says “THAT'S WHERE FIT PROTECTION Comes In” without naming a benefit, hold figure, or vehicle type.
  • A Yamaha Wolverine shopping listing displays “[Price]” as a literal placeholder.

Reviews and UGC

  • Reviewers repeatedly praise exact custom fit, highway hold in crosswinds, and easy installation and removal.
  • Three separate one-star reviews cite customer support, including no phone number, no named contact, and unanswered email.
  • One reviewer reported $183.86 in tariffs on a $465 order without advance notice.
  • Overnight privacy and blackout emerged as an unfeatured benefit in customer language.

PageSpeed Insights

  • The homepage scores 68/100, with 4.4s LCP, 460ms Total Blocking Time, and 22.0s TTI.
  • The PDP scores 51/100, with 8.7s LCP, 580ms Total Blocking Time, and 23.9s TTI.
  • CLS is 0 on both measured pages.

Site Screenshots

  • The homepage hero reads “NEVER LOSE ANOTHER COVER” and the primary configurator opens on UTV.
  • LOXX credential tiles appear in the first fold, while the 537+ verified review signal appears in the next fold.
  • The PDP has a four-step configuration flow and a sticky “ADD PROTECTION · $364” bar.
  • The cart shows an All-LOXX upgrade, unlocked free shipping, and no accessory offer.

Live Site

  • Support is shown as info@fitprotection.com with Mon-Fri 8a-5p MST hours.
  • Vinyl cleaner is $25, removable magnets are $39, and the extended warranty is $39. None appear as a PDP or cart cross-sell beyond the All-LOXX upgrade.
  • A $20 off first cover email capture exists but was not visible in the captured homepage folds.

Competitor Research

  • Universal strap and velcro covers were found at $60 to $90, and DIY shade-netting options cost under $150.
  • FIT Protection sits at the premium end with model-specific cuts, marine-grade materials, and LOXX industrial fastening.

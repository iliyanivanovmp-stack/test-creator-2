## Key Insights

Meta ads, homepage, PDP, collection, and cart all sell through "BUY 1 GET 1 FREE," free shipping, urgency, and lifetime guarantee. Cross Chain shows USD $59, $69 compare-at, and "SAVE $10.00"; cart shows two Cross Chain | Silver items, $59.00 total, and $138.00 struck through. The PDP buy box separates price, BOGO, dispatch, shipping, payment icons, and benefits, so two-item value is clearer after add-to-cart than before.

Trust proof is abundant but scattered. Meta ads say "Over 180.000 members" and "#iamsava"; homepage says "4.9/5 BY 180,000+ CUSTOMERS"; Cross Chain PDP shows 1321 reviews; Cross Bracelet PDP shows 1033 reviews; Google Ads emphasize lifetime warranty, 60-day returns, free shipping, and 180,000+ customers. Cart shows Trustpilot, but not lifetime guarantee or 60-day returns.

Product choice support is weaker than the catalog and offer. Collection/homepage cards show imagery, badges, ratings, names, compare-at prices, and red sale prices, but no filters, sorting, per-unit BOGO math, or second-item-free card treatment. Competitors dated August 11, 2026 show CRAFTD using a value ladder and JAXXON exposing detailed material/style filters.

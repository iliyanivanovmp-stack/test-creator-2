# Sava Amsterdam CRO Research Brief

**Data Sources:** manifest, Meta Ads, Google Ads, site visual summary, context, live fetches and competitor research, August 11, 2026

The biggest opportunity is offer clarity. Sava sells through "BUY 1 GET 1 FREE," free shipping, urgency, and lifetime guarantee across Meta ads, homepage, PDP, collection, and cart. But the PDP buy box asks shoppers to connect separate signals: USD $59 price, $69 compare-at, "SAVE $10.00," BOGO module, dispatch timer, shipping row, payment icons, and benefits. The cart makes the value clearer after the click, with two Cross Chain | Silver items for $59.00 total and $138.00 struck through. Source: roadmap seed and cart summary, August 11, 2026.

Trust proof is strong, but scattered. Meta ads collected August 11, 2026 say "Over 180.000 members" and "#iamsava." The homepage says "4.9/5 BY 180,000+ CUSTOMERS." Cross Chain PDP shows 1321 reviews, Cross Bracelet PDP shows 1033 reviews, and Google Ads emphasize lifetime warranty, 60-day returns, free shipping, and 180,000+ customers. The cart shows Trustpilot, but not lifetime guarantee or 60-day returns. Source: roadmap seed, Meta ads, Google Ads, homepage, PDP, and cart summaries.

Product-choice support is weaker than the offer. Collection and homepage cards show imagery, badges, ratings, names, compare-at prices, and red sale prices. They do not show filters, sorting, per-unit BOGO math, or a second-item-free card treatment in the collected August 11, 2026 screenshots. Source: roadmap seed and collection summary.

Paid traffic lands into different promise sets. Meta landings match the sale offer, while Google Ads lead with durability, warranty, returns, and 180,000+ trust. The PDP first fold leans heavily on sale mechanics, even though live PDP metadata from August 11, 2026 cites waterproof 316L steel. Source: roadmap seed, Google Ads summary, and live PDP metadata.

Revenue sizing is unavailable because monthly sessions and AOV are unknown in the seed. Once those are added, each slot can be sized as sessions per month x conservative lift x AOV.

## Slot 1: PDP BOGO Value Builder

**Type:** A/B test (1 variation vs. control)
**Page:** Product Detail Pages (`https://sava-amsterdam.com/en-us/products/cross-chain`, `https://sava-amsterdam.com/en-us/products/cross-bracelet`)
**Revenue potential:** unknown sessions/mo x 3% CR lift x unknown AOV = revenue sizing unavailable.

**Hypothesis:** If we make the two-item value visible inside the PDP buy box, more paid visitors will add to cart because the $59 price and the $138 cart value are currently connected only after add-to-cart.

**Data:** The August 11, 2026 PDP fold shows $59.00, $69.00 compare-at, "SAVE $10.00," material swatches, BOGO module, dispatch timer, CTA, shipping row, payment icons, and benefits as separate elements. The cart shows two Cross Chain | Silver items for $59.00 total with $138.00 struck through. Source: PDP screenshots, cart summary, Meta ads, and live PDP metadata.

**V1:** On mobile and desktop, keep the existing buy box structure but replace the separated BOGO module with a compact value builder directly under price: "2 items for $59.00 today" plus "You save $79.00 vs. $138.00." Keep material swatches, dispatch timer, CTA, shipping row, payment icons, benefit icons, and collapsibles in their current order.

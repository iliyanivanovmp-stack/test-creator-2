## Findings by Source

### TikTok
The dominant theme in the last 30 days is the launch of **Energy+**, a new paraxanthine-based energy product layered onto the brand's existing electrolyte line. Both the official account and multiple creators are pushing this launch hard:
- Official: "This is the next chapter... the moment Instant Hydration becomes more than a hydration brand" — @instanthydrationofficial, Aug 26 (190 views) and Aug 28 ("Energy, on your terms...")
- Creator @milesburristv (Sept 4, 8.4K views, 238 likes): "Been part of my routine for over a year... Welcome instant hydration energy plus. This is the real deal. Same hydration formula we've always loved, now with clean, crash free energy built right in."
- Creator @rrayyme (Sept 4, 10.5K views, 380 likes): positions Energy+ as a pre-workout replacement for coffee, name-drops "French gray sea salt, magnesium, and potassium" plus paraxanthine for "energy and focus... without the jitters and crash of traditional caffeine."
- Official account also ran a first-party post about the ICEE collab framed as removing caffeine anxiety ("Plot twist: @carl.witness.lee's pre-workout has ZERO caffeine").
Outside of Energy+, there is a steady stream of affiliate/gifted-product content (#instanthydrationpartner, #tiktokshopcreatorpicks) covering: the Luigi's Real Italian Ice collab (@sprinklesprinklecontent, three near-identical posts Aug 29-30), the ICEE collab (@destinyrosepov, Sept 9), Target retail availability (@domorewithlaur, Aug 27: "you can find @Instant Hydration on your next Target run at select Targets"), a travel/TSA hack tying an electrolyte packet to an ice-filled water bottle (@laurenwolfe, Aug 31, 18.6K views — the highest-view organic post in the set), and postpartum/breastfeeding-hydration framing (@gracebowesdeehan, @alli.stetson).

### Trustpilot
TrustScore 3.9 across 302 reviews (248 evaluated in the summary pulled). Positive: customers "truly love the wide variety of delicious flavors," describe the packets as dissolving easily and giving "a clean, refreshing boost for intense workouts or everyday routines." The summary also references some negative feedback beginning to mention subscription/support friction, consistent with the pattern already flagged in the site-collected data (locked "select a flavor to unlock free gifts" mechanic tied to subscription).

### YouTube
Category search surfaced several third-party review/unboxing videos of the brand's core electrolyte packets (titles referencing ingredients, flavor, price, and brand comparisons — see precheck results below for direct links), but the engine's date-windowed pull for the last 30 days did not return brand-relevant content — the one item in the dated evidence window was an unrelated podcast episode with no mention of the brand.

### Web (general)
- A LinkedIn brand post (Aug 27) confirms the Energy+ launch is being treated internally as a strategic pivot: "the moment Instant Hydration becomes more than a hydration brand."
- The brand's own "About Energy+" page states the $27.50/box subscription price and frames the product around paraxanthine as "the primary metabolite your body naturally produces when it breaks down caffeine."
- A "Shawn Johnson Energy+" page confirms an athlete/influencer partnership built around a "Sour Green Apple Energy+" flavor.
- A Social Nature listing shows the core electrolyte product being sampled via a cash-back/review program, with at least one 5/5 review calling it "light and refreshing," a contrast to the "salty or thick" texture some other electrolyte mixes are criticized for.
- LinkedIn profiles confirm active Co-Founder (Zachary Gordan, also Co-Founder of DIFF Eyewear) and Creative Director (Kari Evans) roles tied specifically to the Energy+ launch, and a supply chain/logistics hire, indicating the brand is actively staffing for growth.

### Amazon (precheck)
Multiple branded SKUs are listed and actively sold direct on Amazon (Raspberry, Citrus Orange, Mango Pineapple, Watermelon, Lemon Lime, Cotton Candy, and a 12-count Variety Pack), each carrying the same "Zero Sugar - Keto, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Non-GMO" claims used on-site.

## Cross-Source Signal

The Energy+ launch (paraxanthine-based, framed as a caffeine alternative with "no jitters/no crash") is the single dominant theme across TikTok (official account and multiple independent creators), Web/LinkedIn (internal framing as a strategic brand pivot), and the brand's own site — corroborating that Energy+ is genuinely the current marketing and business focus, not just a homepage design choice. Separately, Trustpilot and TikTok creator content both independently reference the core electrolyte product positively on taste/mixability, while the "flavor unlocks free gift" subscription mechanic seen on the live PDP surfaces as a point of friction in Trustpilot's negative-review theme.

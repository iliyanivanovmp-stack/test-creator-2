Sticky footer bar shows 4.5/5, "25k+ Reviews," "2M+ Orders," and a persistent "SELECT FROM 14 FLAVORS" CTA.
**LP CTA:** "SELECT FROM 14 FLAVORS" red button, present in the buy box and repeated in a sticky bottom bar. No separate "Add to Cart" — flavor selection gates the primary action.
**LP trust signals:** Star rating (4.5-4.7) and review count (25,000+ / 25k+) shown at the top of the buy box and again in the sticky footer bar. "50 Day Happiness Guarantee" appears near the CTA. No third-party badges (e.g., "as seen in") visible in the folds captured.
**Message Match:** The ad's headline and creative are entirely ICEE-branded (ICEE logo, "iconic flavor," ICEE product tube). The landing page hero also leads with ICEE branding and the same iconic-flavor messaging, but the buy box product title reads "Instant Hydration Electrolyte Powder" with no ICEE name in it, and the flavor-selection CTA doesn't name ICEE flavors specifically.

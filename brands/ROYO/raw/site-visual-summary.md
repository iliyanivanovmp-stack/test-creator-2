## Summary

All pages maintain consistent branding with yellow/orange accent colors and bold typography. Collection page shows extensive product range with strong review social proof (4,000-18,000+ reviews per SKU). PDP emphasizes subscription value with 15% discount and clear benefit communication. Cart drawer focuses on AOV through threshold-based savings and cross-sell carousel. No critical layout issues observed. Trust signals (reviews, guarantees, shipping promises) are present but could be strengthened in certain areas (e.g., reviews absent from homepage; shipping cost hidden until checkout).

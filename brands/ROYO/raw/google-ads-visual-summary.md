# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads.png

**Ads visible:** Multiple ad formats across search and display networks. Includes text search ads, product shopping ads, display ads featuring ROYO Starter Pack and product images.

**Headline themes:** 
- "Delicious and nutritious - Buy healthy and tasty bread" 
- "All - Royo Bread Co - High Protein Bread & Bagels" with sub-text about turning high carb foods into low calorie, low carb, keto-friendly goods
- "Low Carb Artisan Bread"
- "ROYO: Classic Baked Goods, Reimagined"
- "Variety Bagel Bundle - ROYO BREAD CO"
Multiple variations on the core message: healthy, low-carb, tasty alternative to regular bread

**Offers and CTAs:** 
- Rating displayed: 4.4 stars (10,786 reviews) on main search ad
- Specific ad copy references "Low Carb", "Keto-friendly", "High Protein"
- Product shopping ads show ROYO Starter Pack bundle
- "Retail Stores", "Products", "Low Carb Bread" sitelinks visible
- Pricing not prominent in ad copy (prices shown on product shopping cards)

**Visual approach:** 
- Search text ads dominate with clear headline/description structure
- Product shopping ads feature yellow-and-colored packaging for ROYO products
- Display ads show ROYO branding, product bundles, and prepared food photos
- Consistent brand color (yellow/orange) across creatives

**Gaps vs. Meta:**
- Google Ads heavily emphasize "Low Carb", "Keto-friendly", "High Protein" positioning
- No discount code or specific offer visible in Google Ads screenshots (Meta ads show 26% OFF NY26)
- Google Ads feature higher review volume call-out (10,786 reviews visible)
- Meta ads focus on the "26% discount" hook; Google ads focus more on product category and nutritional positioning
- Google Ads lack the emotional narrative that Meta ads provide ("Made by a Nutritionist, Crafted by a Baker")

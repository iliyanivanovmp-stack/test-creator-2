# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png (Google Ads Transparency Center)

**Ads visible:** Approximately 26 ad units across both screenshots — mix of text search ads, shopping/product image ads, video ads, and display/discovery-style image ads. All attributed to advertiser "Ashley Stewart, Inc."

**Headline themes:** Recurring themes across text ads:
- "Woman's Plus Size Clothing - New Trends for sizes 10-36"
- "Ashley Stewart® Dresses - Dresses That Love Your Curves" / "New Trends for sizes 10-36"
- "Ashley Stewart® Official - The Latest Plus Size Trends"
- "Plus size Clearance Final Sale Up to 75% off"
- "Ashley Stewart® Dresses - Shop The Season's Best - Styles In Sizes 10-36"
- "Ashley Stewart® Official - Calling All Plus Size Divas"
- "Ashley Stewart® Official - Fashion in sizes 10-36"
Consistent emphasis on "plus size," inclusive sizing (10-36), curve-focused language ("Love Your Curves," "Embrace Your Curves"), and being a category authority ("The first word in fashion for sizes 10-36").

**Offers and CTAs:** "-40%" badges on several shopping-style ads (Layered Gold Tone Chain Belt, Plus Size Pinstripe Strapless item, Plus Size Crisscross Denim). "Plus Size Clearance Final Sale Up to 75% off" text ad. Product carousel ad shows multiple SKUs (tops, dresses, bags, jewelry) with a "Shop now" style CTA bar. Interactive display ad: "Curvy babes love these bottoms — Stock up on Ashley Stewart's stylish collection of bottoms" with "Open >" CTA. One text ad displays a review-rating snippet: "4.4 ★★★★☆ (13,311)" and "4.2 ★★★★☆ (23,581)" on different listings, with "Email reply time: 1 hour."

**Visual approach:** Shopping/product ads use clean product-on-white or lifestyle photography (models in dresses, tops, denim). Video ad thumbnails show models in styled outfits against colored studio backgrounds (orange, poolside). One display carousel ad packs 9 product thumbnails into a single grid unit. Two ad slots render as "Sorry, we're not able to show you this ad at this time" placeholders (broken/unavailable creative in the Transparency Center).

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Google Ads messaging leans heavily on broad brand/category positioning ("plus size," "curves," "sizes 10-36") and sitewide discount messaging (40-75% off), whereas the Meta ads reviewed are single-SKU or single-collection focused (face gems, pantyhose, bridal). No bridal-specific or accessory-specific (face gems) messaging appears in the Google Ads screenshots — the two channels appear to run different creative concepts with no visible overlap in offer or product focus.

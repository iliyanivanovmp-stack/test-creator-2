Ashley Stewart's strongest purchase signals appear on product pages, while the homepage and paid journeys are crowded, inconsistent, or incomplete. This roadmap repairs the clearest funnel breaks, brings trust forward, and gives shoppers more direct paths from discovery to checkout.

Eight focused opportunities

Testing roadmap

Slot 1 Immediate Fix

Crystal Face Gems Landing Page

Crystal Face Gems product page

Offer-focused traffic for Crystal Face Gems reaches a product URL that returns a live 404. Shoppers cannot find the item they clicked to view. Restore the product at that URL or route the campaign to the correct live product page.

* The broken destination is replaced by a live, product-specific path. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2 Immediate Fix

PDP Load Performance

Basic Knit Cami Top PDP

The measured PDP scores 39/100 on mobile, with a 19.4-second Largest Contentful Paint and 61.3-second Time to Interactive. The page's strong buy box cannot work until shoppers can reach it. Resolve the render-blocking load so product imagery, choices, trust, and Add to Cart appear as one usable experience.

* The full PDP purchase surface loads as a usable experience instead of arriving in fragments. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3 A/B Test

Homepage Promo Stack

Homepage

Three full-width promotional banners appear before the Fall Edit hero, and the discount popup remains pinned over the page. This pushes Ashley Stewart's brand imagery lower in the first view. The variation combines the shipping, clearance, and new-arrivals messages into one compact rotating bar so the hero arrives sooner.

* Three stacked promotions become one compact bar, bringing the Fall Edit campaign into the first view. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4 A/B Test

Homepage Trust Signals

Homepage

The captured homepage folds show promotion after promotion, but no rating, review count, guarantee, or trust copy. The PDP already presents returns and installment options near purchase. The variation brings the existing "30-Day Returns & Exchanges" promise and Klarna, Afterpay, and Zip options directly beneath the homepage hero.

* Existing returns and installment signals move from the PDP into a clear row beneath the homepage hero. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5 A/B Test

Collection Tag Legend

New Arrivals collection

New Arrivals cards mix "New," "Online Exclusive," and percentage-off labels without explaining whether those states overlap. The variation keeps every product card unchanged and adds one plain-language key below the collection title: "New = just added | Online Exclusive = web only | % Off = discount applied."

* A single key explains how New, Online Exclusive, and percentage-off labels relate before shoppers scan the grid. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6 A/B Test

Cart Cross-Sell

Cart

The cart shows a free-shipping progress bar, one line item, and the order summary, but no product recommendation. The PDP already uses a "Ways to Wear It" carousel with "Shop the Look" actions. The variation adds a "Complete the Look" row between the cart item and order summary while leaving the shipping progress in place.

* The PDP's styling mechanic becomes a focused cart recommendation before the order summary. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7 A/B Test

Bridal Product-Led Path

Bridal landing page

Bridal traffic arrives with an emotional gown promise, then must continue browsing to reach an individual dress. The landing page has bridal imagery, but no product-level choice in the opening experience. The variation leads with "Say Yes To The Dress" and a curated row of gowns before the general promotional content.

* Bridal visitors meet a curated gown selection before general promotional content. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8 Immediate Fix

PDP Breadcrumb

All product detail pages

The Basic Knit Cami Top breadcrumb repeats the same category in sequence: "Home / CLOTHING / CLOTHING." The duplicate reads as a visible template defect near the top of the PDP. Correct the shared breadcrumb so each category level appears once.

* The repeated category is removed from the shared PDP breadcrumb. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Cross-source synthesis

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  • PDP speed blocks high-intent traffic

    The measured product page scores 39/100 on mobile, with a 19.4-second Largest Contentful Paint and 61.3-second Time to Interactive.

  • Trust changes across the journey

    The PDP shows ratings, returns, and installment options. The captured homepage shows none of them, while collected reviews describe month-plus refund delays and disputed return-shipping charges.

  • Paid journeys break or broaden

    One product campaign reaches a confirmed 404. Bridal traffic lands on a broad collection experience that requires shoppers to browse again before reaching a gown.

Source findings

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Crystal Face Gems traffic reaches a product URL that returns a confirmed HTTP 404.
  • Berkshire Ultra Sheer Pantyhose creative matches its PDP copy, image, selected color and size, 4.5-star rating, 44 reviews, and "Only 6 Left" message.
  • Bridal creative carries a specific emotional gown promise, while its destination requires shoppers to browse a collection before reaching a product.

Source findings

Google Ads

  • Visible messaging focuses on plus-size fashion, sizes 10-36, "Love Your Curves," and 40-75% discounts.
  • Google's broad category and discount themes do not visibly reinforce the bridal or face-gem promises found in paid-social creative.

Source findings

Reviews and UGC

  • 7+ of 20 collected reviews describe refund delays that exceed a month.
  • One reviewer reports both a $10 return-shipping deduction and a separate $9.95 shipping fee on the same return.
  • Collected complaints also describe disconnected calls, conflicting support answers, wrong items, damaged goods, and lost or stolen packages.
  • All 20 collected reviews are negative and rated 1-2 stars, so this sample may not represent the full customer base.

Source findings

Page Speed Insights

  • The homepage mobile performance score is 61/100. Largest Contentful Paint is 3.5 seconds, Total Blocking Time is 790 milliseconds, Speed Index is 8.0 seconds, and Time to Interactive is 27.6 seconds.
  • The PDP mobile performance score is 39/100. Largest Contentful Paint is 19.4 seconds, Total Blocking Time is 930 milliseconds, Speed Index is 22.8 seconds, and Time to Interactive is 61.3 seconds.
  • The PDP run was too slow to complete within Lighthouse's time limit, so the captured results may be incomplete.

Source findings

Site Screenshots

  • Three promotional banners appear before the homepage hero, and a persistent "Get 20% off!" popup overlaps content across the captured folds.
  • No rating, review count, guarantee badge, or trust copy appears in the three captured homepage folds.
  • The PDP shows a 4.5-star rating with 67 reviews, Klarna, Afterpay, Zip, and a "30-Day Returns & Exchanges" box near the buy box.
  • The cart's only visible order-value mechanic is the free-shipping progress bar. No cross-sell, upsell, or bundle appears in the captured cart.

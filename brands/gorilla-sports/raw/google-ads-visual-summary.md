# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png
**Ads visible:** Multiple display-style ad tiles in a two-column Google Ads Transparency layout. Most tiles are text-forward category ads; a few include product or lifestyle thumbnails.
**Headline themes:** Category and product terms including weight plates, functional training, wall-mounted pull-up bar, Gorilla Sports brand, gym mats, kettlebells, footballs, weight bench, adjustable weights, push-up bars, barbell sets, step boards, ankle straps, jump rope, and leg weights. Copy patterns repeatedly describe finding quality Gorilla Sports products for home or gym and mention a Finnish online store.
**Offers and CTAs:** Some visible ads mention discounts such as "jopa -40%" and "2026 - Alennukset jopa -40% katso." One image ad mentions more than 500 different products and saving money today. Explicit button CTAs are not visible in most tiles.
**Visual approach:** Most ads use simple white ad cards with logo/domain, blue headline text, and short description copy. Image variants use small product/lifestyle thumbnails inside the same grid.
**Gaps vs. Meta:** Google ads show broader category coverage and repeated store/category discovery copy. Meta ads shown are more product/offer-led with carousel or video formats and visible "Shop Now" CTAs.

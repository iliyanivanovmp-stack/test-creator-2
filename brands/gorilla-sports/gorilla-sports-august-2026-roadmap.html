CRO Testing Roadmap

August 2026

Gorilla Sports has strong offer and category match from ads to landing pages, but the first product decision carries too much of the work. The clearest opportunity is to bring goal guidance, trust proof, and add-on framing closer to the moments where shoppers choose, compare, and check out.

Testing Slots

Each card shows the customer-facing change we want to test and the exact shopping surface it affects.

Slot 1A/B Test

Gym Package Finder

Kuntosalipaketit collection page

Paid home-gym traffic reaches a dense package grid with filters, sorting, sale badges, ratings, and urgency strips, but no visible selector for goal, space, level, budget, or equipment need. That makes shoppers self-compare complete setups before they have a clear path. The variation adds a compact finder above the product grid while keeping the existing product cards and sale mechanics intact.

* The collection gains a goal, space, level, budget, and equipment finder before the dense grid. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2A/B Test

Parallel Bars Claim Tiles

Parallel Bars landing page

The Parallel Bars page matches the product category, but Fold 1 turns small-space suitability, durability, beginner-to-advanced use, and chest, shoulder, and core outcomes into a standard four-card grid. Those claims are purchase criteria, not just copy. The variation adds compact claim tiles above the cards so shoppers can compare the range through the promise that brought them in.

* Claim tiles turn small-space, durability, level, and muscle-group promises into visible comparison criteria. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3A/B Test

Collection Trust Strip

Collection landing pages

Collection decisions rely mostly on product-card ratings and sale mechanics, while stronger page-level proof appears on the homepage, PDP, and cart. Trust is available, but it is not consistently placed where shoppers make the first comparison. The variation adds a slim proof strip above the grid using existing signals: 4.3 rating, 7000+ reviews, 100,000+ customers, 30-day returns, Finnish strength claim, and AA credit rating.

* Existing proof moves above the grid before shoppers compare products. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4A/B Test

Product Card Decision Hierarchy

Collection product grids

Collection cards stack discount badges, sale prices, compare-at prices, savings labels, ratings, urgency strips, and savings-style buttons. The offer is loud, but product differences and the next action can become harder to scan. The variation keeps the same products, prices, ratings, discounts, and urgency message, but orders each card around image, title, rating, price, savings, urgency, and action.

* The same product-card ingredients are reordered so the product, rating, price, savings, urgency, and action scan cleanly. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

Homepage Goal Paths

Homepage

The homepage hero is visually strong and pushes "Taljalaitteet jopa -50%," while Google Ads demand spans weights, pull-up bars, mats, kettlebells, benches, adjustable weights, push-up bars, barbell sets, step boards, ankle straps, jump ropes, and leg weights. One category promotion does not reflect that spread of intent. The variation keeps the existing hero image and headline, but replaces the single red block with three goal paths: build a home gym, train strength, and add accessories.

* The hero keeps the same brand image and headline, but offers three shopper goals instead of one category block. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6A/B Test

Cart Drawer Add-On Module

Sitewide cart drawer

The cart drawer shows product details, quantity controls, a fixed "Siirry kassalle" button, and a trust panel, but no upsell, cross-sell, bundle reminder, or free-shipping threshold. The shopper has already shown purchase intent, yet the drawer does not offer a relevant next item. The variation adds one compact add-on recommendation between the cart item and checkout area while keeping checkout and trust proof unchanged.

* A single relevant add-on appears inside the cart drawer before checkout. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7A/B Test

PDP Bundle Explanation

Säädettävä käsipainosarja 30 kg PDP

The Fold 2 frequently-bought-together block shows three checked items, a 75.85 EUR total, a black add-to-cart button, and an option dropdown. It does not explain why the selected add-ons belong with the main product. The variation keeps the checked items, price, dropdown, and CTA, then adds a short benefit line under each item so the bundle feels easier to understand.

* The prechecked bundle gains benefit lines that explain why each add-on belongs with the dumbbell set. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8A/B Test

Buy Box Proof Compression

Säädettävä käsipainosarja 30 kg PDP

The PDP buy box stacks rating, 55 reviews, brand, title, bullets, discount, sale price, compare-at price, savings label, CTA, payment box, stock and delivery copy, limited-time offer, and trust bullets. The core purchase path is present, but the surrounding proof makes the area dense. The variation keeps the key rating, price, savings, and add-to-cart sequence visible, then compresses payment, delivery, offer, and trust details into two expandable rows below the CTA.

* Secondary proof collapses below the CTA so rating, price, savings, and add-to-cart stay dominant. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  • Guided category selection is the strongest recurring gap. Paid and search traffic reaches broad catalog pages, but the first product decision is still mostly a dense grid.
  • Trust and urgency exist, but they are not consistently placed near the first choice on collection landing pages.
  • Mobile speed is a real paid-entry constraint. The homepage mobile LCP is 5.4s and the collection mobile LCP is 4.3s.
  • The PDP and cart have strong purchase signals, but bundle value, proof, and add-on logic need cleaner framing around the action.

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Home-gym and bench equipment traffic lands on the Kuntosalipaketit page, where the category and discount match is strong.
  • The gym-package landing page shows intro copy, carousel, filters, sorting, five-column product grid, discount badges, red sale prices, compare-at prices, savings labels, star ratings, and black urgency strips.
  • The same grid mechanic is used for complete gym packages and parallel bars, even though those categories create different shopper questions.
  • The Parallel Bars page shows discounts from -47% to -72%, but Fold 1 does not turn small-space fit, durability, exercise outcomes, or beginner suitability into comparison criteria.

Google Ads

  • Google Ads cover weight plates, functional training, wall-mounted pull-up bars, gym mats, kettlebells, weight benches, adjustable weights, push-up bars, barbell sets, step boards, ankle straps, jump ropes, and leg weights.
  • Copy repeatedly positions Gorilla Sports as a Finnish online store with quality products for home or gym.
  • The shared opportunity is to make category landing pages translate broad demand into guided product selection.

PageSpeed Insights

  • Only mobile Lighthouse JSON files were collected. No desktop PageSpeed report was present.
  • Homepage mobile performance was 55/100, with FCP 2.2s, LCP 5.4s, TBT 630ms, and CLS 0.
  • Collection mobile performance was 61/100, with FCP 2.5s, LCP 4.3s, TBT 740ms, and CLS 0.
  • The JSON reports had no field Core Web Vitals, so the lab data is the usable performance signal.

Site Screenshots

  • The homepage trust strip shows 4.3 rating, 7000+ reviews, 100,000+ satisfied customers, 30-day return right, a Finnish strength-related claim, and AA credit rating.
  • The PDP for "Säädettävä käsipainosarja 30 kg" shows 55 reviews, 45.95 EUR sale price, 109.95 EUR compare-at price, and a dense buy box with payment, delivery, limited-time offer, and trust details.
  • The cart drawer shows secure payments, 30-day return right, and 2-year warranty, but no upsell, cross-sell, bundle reminder, or free-shipping threshold.

Reviews and UGC

  • No collected customer-review dataset exists for Gorilla Sports in this collection.
  • The skipped review source means product, operations, and support fixes should not be based on this audit alone.
  • The site itself displays review proof in screenshots, including the homepage 4.3 rating and the PDP's 55 product reviews.

Competitor Research

  • Fitnesstukku's observed home gym page showed seven homegym products, free shipping over 49 EUR, free returns, and 14-day cancellation.
  • Gymstick's observed home gyms page showed six home gym machine products and a clear product-line progression.
  • Treenikauppa's public Trustpilot profile showed 4.8 rating from 509 reviews and 417 reviews in the last 12 months.
  • Competitor evidence supports clearer package guidance and stronger trust reinforcement near product decisions.

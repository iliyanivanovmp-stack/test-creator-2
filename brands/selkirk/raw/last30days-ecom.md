## Cross-Source Signal

Durability and long-term performance retention of paddles (specifically the Boomstik) is corroborated across TikTok and Amazon reviews — a consistent positive theme distinct from the base-tier "value" framing in the client-supplied reviews. Warranty/returns friction also appears in two independent sources (Trustpilot claims-process complaints and the "Exchanges are unavailable" language on the official Boomstik product page), suggesting post-purchase support terms may be worth checking against on-site messaging.

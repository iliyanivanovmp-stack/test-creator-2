And How do You Play it?"), and discount/apparel merchandising (-37% shoes, -30% apparel). One search ad snippet surfaces on-site review content: "What customers are saying... Users note Selkirk Sport offers high-quality products, fast shipping, helpful customer support, and..."

**Offers and CTAs:** Visible discounts: -37% on Legacy Pro shoes, -30% (x2) on an apparel bundle. "Military Discount" and "Limited Edition" listed as sitelinks on one search ad. CTAs seen: "Open," and standard "Shop" framing implied by sitelink labels (Shop Selkirk Amped, Shop Pickleball Paddles). One search snippet shows a 4.6-star (25 reviews) rating for selkirk.com and a separate 4.7-star (1,291) rating referenced in another card's "Rating for selkirk.com."

**Visual approach:** Video ads dominate the grid and favor dark, high-contrast lifestyle/action footage (courts, players, product close-ups) with bold white/red text overlays. Shopping-style image ads use clean product-on-white or product-on-gradient photography consistent with site PDP imagery. Search ad cards follow standard Google text-ad formatting with blue headline links and sitelink rows.

**Gaps vs. Meta:** The Google Ads set is dominated by the Boomstik and the new OMNI paddle launch and general brand/education messaging, while the three Meta ads captured focus on markdowns/deals (AMPED Pro Air), a specific stat-led paddle promotion (Vanguard Power Air), and a review-testimonial for a $7 towel accessory. No direct overlap in featured products between the two channels' captured creative, and no discount codes/percentages appear in the Meta ad set despite two Google ads showing explicit percentage-off badges (-37%, -30%). Malaysia-region advertiser entities (SELKIRK SPORT MALAYSIA SDN. BHD.) also appear prominently in the Google Ads set with no equivalent regional variant observed in the Meta set.

Conversion testing roadmap

September 2026

Selkirk has strong product demand and deep proof, but key journeys do not always deliver the promise that earned the click. This roadmap brings the core paddle range forward, gives paid visitors a clearer next step, and removes friction around speed, stock, shipping, and add-ons.

Testing Lineup

Eight focused opportunities across the homepage, product pages, performance, and cart.

Slot 1A/B Test

Homepage Hero CTA Routing

Page: Homepage

The homepage hero promotes the Pro Line 2.0 Bag Series, and its only "Buy Now" button opens a three-product bags collection with no paddles. The first direct route to paddles appears after two full folds. The variation sends the hero CTA to the paddle collection so the main entry point opens Selkirk's core category.

The same hero opens the paddle collection instead of a bags-only destination. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2A/B Test

Color-Level Stock Recovery

Page: Vanguard Power Air - S2

The live $100.99 page still shows 847 reviews, but it is no longer a complete dead end: Red is available with Add to Cart while Black, Prestige, Selkirk Red, and Shadow Gray remain unavailable. The variation appears only after an unavailable color is selected, preserving Notify Me while adding a direct route to the buyable Red option and comparable in-stock paddles.

Red remains directly buyable; the recovery path appears only for the four unavailable colors. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3A/B Test

AMPED Deal Message

Page: AMPED Pro Air - Epic

Deal-focused traffic reaches a captured page showing $100 and a "Free Gift With Purchase" badge, but no visible discount or named gift. The offer exists, yet its value is left implicit. The variation names the included gift beside the price so the landing page immediately reflects the promise that earned the click.

The existing gift offer becomes explicit beside the price, with the exact gift named once confirmed. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4Immediate Fix

Mobile Load Time

Pages: Homepage and Project Boomstik PDP

Mobile Lighthouse shows a 12.0-second LCP on the homepage and 9.9 seconds on the Boomstik PDP, while both pages take more than 12 seconds to become interactive. Layout stability and blocking time already perform well, but the main content arrives late. The fix prioritizes the first visible content so the homepage hero and Boomstik buy box appear sooner.

Primary mobile media and purchase content become the first loading priority on both templates. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

Towel Review Order

Page: Tacky Grip Towel

Paid creative leads with a five-star testimonial about paddle quality, while the towel page's first visible reviews include a two-star "Not great" review and a three-star durability complaint. The first page impression contradicts the promise before shoppers see the positive feedback. The variation opens the section with the highest-rated towel reviews while keeping every review available.

The review section opens with five-star towel feedback while every rating remains accessible. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6Needs Revalidation

Delayed Cart Reassurance

Page: Cart drawer

The live Boomstik cart shows "Shipping: FREE" and a free-shipping confirmation, with no delay warning or 30-Day Guarantee in the drawer. The earlier warning may be conditional on a delayed SKU, so this test needs a fresh delayed-line-item capture before scoping. If that notice still appears, keep it intact and place the existing guarantee directly beneath it.

The live cart is shown first; the reassurance treatment remains conditional on recapturing a delayed SKU. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7Needs Revalidation

Delayed-Variant Shipping Context

Page: Project Boomstik

The live $333 Boomstik page still shows 2,664 reviews and an active Add to Cart button, but the checked variant no longer shows a 2-3-week shipping note near the buy box. This test should proceed only if another variant still carries a confirmed delay. For that delayed state, pair the lead time with a reason and estimated delivery date.

The live buy box removes the stale delay claim; the proposed message applies only to a confirmed delayed variant. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8A/B Test

Frequently Bought Together

Page: Project Boomstik PDP

The live Boomstik add-on row shows the Pro Line 2.0 Team Backpack at $248, CourtStrike shoe at $127.99, OMNI at $300 out of stock, and Pro S1 at $150 against $250. Available items still require separate Quick Add actions. The variation adds one "Add 3 available items" action while leaving OMNI visibly unavailable and preserving individual controls.

The available Boomstik add-ons gain one combined action while OMNI remains visibly unavailable. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  • The main homepage CTA opens a three-product bags collection with no paddles, while a direct paddle route does not appear until the third captured fold.
  • Paid promises and landing pages conflict on offer visibility, price, stock, and product-specific proof.
  • Mobile LCP reaches 12.0 seconds on the homepage and 9.9 seconds on the Boomstik PDP, with Time to Interactive above 12 seconds on both.
  • The live Boomstik add-on row still requires separate Quick Add actions and provides no combined action for the three available items.

What the Data Shows

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Deal-focused traffic reached a $100 AMPED Pro Air page with a "Free Gift With Purchase" badge but no visible markdown or discount cue in the captured folds.
  • The audit captured Vanguard Power Air at $100.99 in a fully unavailable state. The current checked page keeps the same price and 847 reviews, with Red buyable and Black, Prestige, Selkirk Red, and Shadow Gray unavailable.
  • The towel creative used a five-star paddle testimonial, while the towel page surfaced two-star and three-star product reviews within one scroll.

Google Ads

  • The visible mix emphasizes Boomstik, OMNI, pro positioning, product education, shoes, and apparel.
  • Shopping creative shows -37% on Legacy Pro shoes and -30% on an apparel bundle, while the captured paid-social set shows no percentage-off badge or discount code.

Reviews and UGC

  • SLK Neo reviewers repeatedly praise the large sweet spot, easy topspin, comfortable grip, entry-level value, and included carrying case.
  • The product is described mainly as a beginner, gift, or raffle-prize purchase.
  • One reviewer called the material "low par" and planned to upgrade after the set fell apart. The rest of the supplied reviews were four or five stars.

PageSpeed Insights

  • Homepage mobile performance is 64/100, with 12.0-second LCP and 12.6-second Time to Interactive.
  • Boomstik PDP mobile performance is 63/100, with 9.9-second LCP and 13.5-second Time to Interactive.
  • Both pages record CLS 0 and low blocking time, but no desktop performance data was collected.

Client Context and Site Screenshots

  • The client flagged the homepage's main CTA route to Pro Line 2.0 Bags. Screenshots confirm the destination contains Tour Backpack $298, Team Backpack $248, and Duffle $198, with no paddles.
  • The audit captured a Boomstik delay note, but the current checked $333 buy box with 2,664 reviews has an active Add to Cart button and no 2-3-week note. The live trust stack includes 30-Day Free Returns, Lifetime Warranty, Free Shipping $55+, and 30-Day Guarantee.
  • The current Boomstik cart shows $333, Shipping: FREE, and a free-shipping confirmation, with no delay warning or 30-Day Guarantee in the drawer. A delayed line item must be recaptured before the cart-reassurance test proceeds.
  • The live Boomstik add-on row shows Team Backpack $248, CourtStrike $127.99, OMNI $300 out of stock, and Pro S1 at $150 against $250; available products still use separate Quick Add actions with no combined Add All control.

Community Research

  • Boomstik durability and performance retention are described after 6-12+ months of use, supported directionally by 4.4-4.5-star Amazon ratings across three broader paddle lines.
  • Trustpilot reports warranty-claim friction, while the official Boomstik page states "Exchanges are unavailable" alongside "30-Day Free Returns."
  • Price skepticism appears in one YouTube discussion and is not corroborated elsewhere.

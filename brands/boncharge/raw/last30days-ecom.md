## Cross-Source Signal

Trustpilot data is split across two domains with conflicting scores: the current boncharge.com listing shows a very low TrustScore (2.4, only 6 reviews as of 2026-09-11), while the legacy blublox.com domain carries a much higher 4-star rating built on 1,400+ historical reviews. This split — old-domain reputation not yet transferred or consolidated onto the new domain — is worth flagging as a trust-signal risk if boncharge.com is what prospects see when checking reviews independently. Separately, third-party reviewers (TikTok, YouTube) consistently frame Bon Charge as competitively scored but not always the top pick in its category (e.g., "likely better as a brand-direct purchase," implying price/marketplace friction), which corroborates the Amazon findings showing active third-party marketplace listings competing with direct-to-consumer purchase.

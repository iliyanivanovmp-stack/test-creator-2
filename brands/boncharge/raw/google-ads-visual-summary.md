# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png (both from Google Ads Transparency Center)

**Ads visible:** Approximately 30+ ad units visible across both screenshots, all under advertiser "BLUBLOX PTY LTD" (verified), operating as "Bon Charge™." Formats include: text/search-style ads with headline + description, product image ads (glasses, face mask, toothbrush, sauna blanket), and video ad thumbnails (play-button overlay). Several units render as Google error pages ("500. That's an error. There was an error. Please try again later.") instead of ad content.

**Headline themes:** Recurring themes across ad units: blue light blocking glasses ("100% Blue Light Blocking - Blue Light Blocking Glasses - Bon Charge™," "Parker Blue Light Blocking Glasses | Best Sleep Glasses"), red light therapy ("Bon Charge Red Light Mask - Bon Charge Beauty, Skin & Hair," "Best Red Light Face Mask," "Pros & Cons: Red Light Therapy - Honest Red Light Pros and Cons"), red light toothbrush ("Red Light Toothbrush - Built-in Timer, Every..."), hair growth ("Red Light Therapy for Hair Growth - The Ultimate Guide - BON"), sleep/night products ("Night Light | Motion Sensor Light | BON CHARGE - Bon Charge," "Better Nights Start at Dusk - Night Mode Skips the Key Waves"), and general brand awareness ("BON CHARGE - Bon Charge," "Science backed wellness products for our modern world...").

**Offers and CTAs:** "25% off" appears repeatedly (e.g., "Red Light Therapy | At Home Premium Products - Bon Charge... 25% off sitewide"), "Labor Day Sale" with "Extended 48 More Hours" and "25% off, auto-applied at checkout" messaging, and a "Red Light Cap - 25% off Labor Day Sale" product-specific promo. CTAs embedded in descriptions include "Shop Now," "Shop Collection," "View Collections," "Learn More."

**Visual approach:** Product image ads show single-item product shots on plain/white backgrounds (orange-framed glasses, black face mask, toothbrush) consistent with the Meta ad creative style. Video ad thumbnails show lifestyle imagery (a person's face, a red-lit figure in motion) with a play icon overlay, suggesting native video ad units alongside static ones. Article/guide-style ads ("Pros & Cons: Red Light Therapy," "The Ultimate Guide") use a more editorial framing than the direct product ads.

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Meta ad creatives are narrow and product-specific (glasses x2, face mask x1) with consistent "Enhance your wellbeing" / "Don't wait for better skin" messaging. Google Ads coverage is broader, spanning many more product lines (toothbrush, night light, hair growth, sauna blanket) and includes educational/guide-style ad copy not present in the Meta set. Several Google ad slots resolve to server error pages rather than live ad creative or landing content — this is a technical/delivery observation from the screenshots, not a messaging comparison.

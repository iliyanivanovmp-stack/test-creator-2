Below the CTAs, small text: "Over 500,000 orders from happy customers with [5-star icon] reviews." Below the hero, a "Featured Products" section begins showing hoodie product cards.
**Fold 2:** Continuation of "Featured Products" hoodie grid — 5 colorways of "OVERSIZED LUX HOODIE 'LOVE SEEING YOU HAPPY'" ($80.00 USD each) with per-card size selector, "ADD TO CART" button, and purple "Buy with Shop" button. Then a "Shop by Category" section heading begins.
**Fold 3:** "Shop by Category" tile grid — 4 lifestyle-photo category tiles: "Unisex Shorts," "Simple Collection," "Womens," "Unisex Lux Tees," each with a black "SHOP" pill button. Below that, a "Shop Hoodies" section heading with carousel arrows, showing the start of another hoodie product row (same "I Love Seeing You Happy" hoodie design in black, black, black/pink, blue, yellow colorways).
**CTA behavior:** No sticky header or sticky CTA bar visible in any of the three captured folds — the main nav bar scrolls out of view between fold 1 and fold 2 (not present at the top of fold 2 or fold 3). Primary hero CTAs ("SHOP MENS" / "SHOP WOMENS") are above the fold.
**Trust signals:** "Over 500,000 orders from happy customers" with a 5-star icon graphic, positioned in the hero (fold 1). A 4-icon trust bar (Ships directly from U.S.A, Ships within 24 Hours, Hassle Free Returns & Free Exchanges, Support Suicide Prevention) positioned below the featured-products grid (fold 2). No specific review count or star rating (e.g., "4.8 stars, 2,437 reviews") visible on the homepage in these three folds — the homepage trust claim is a round order count, not a review score.
**Layout notes:** Homepage leads with brand/mission messaging (anti-negativity, mental health framing) paired with lifestyle photography before showing any product-specific claims. Product grids use a consistent card format across the page: product image, name, price, size dropdown, "ADD TO CART," and "Buy with Shop" button stacked vertically per card.

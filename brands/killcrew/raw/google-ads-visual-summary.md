# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png (Google Ads Transparency Center grid view, killcrew.co)

**Ads visible:** 10 ad units visible in the grid, all attributed to "KILL CREW INC." (Verified). Mix of formats: an image/display ad (colorful shorts flat-lay, "Shop The Simple Collection"), a video ad thumbnail (Kill Crew Inc., no caption text visible), several responsive search ads (text with headline + description + sitelinks), one Shopping/product ad (Muay Thai Flame Shorts, $50, "Free" shipping label, "Shop now" CTA), and a large pink display ad ("Shop Kill Crew Apparel").

**Headline themes:** Several distinct messaging angles: (1) self-care/mental health framing — "Selfcare 101 – take care of yourself. Buy Kill Crew," "Our quest for inner victory over anxiety, fear, depression, stress, insomnia, ocd, ptsd, phobias, eating disorders makes us forever hungry. We are wolves among sheep. We are the Kill Crew"; (2) product/performance framing — "Shop Kill Crew Apparel - Squat Proof Shorts," "Squat Proof Gym Shorts," "Shorts That Don't Ride Up – Heavyweight fabric that doesn't go thin, fade or lose its shape. Over 10,000+ 5 Stars"; (3) brand/identity framing — "A brand for the misfits, the rebels, the square pegs that don't fit in round holes. Selfcare 101: Buy Kill Crew. Look good, feel good."; (4) service/logistics framing — "Kill Crew Official Site – Hassle Free Returns," "The official Kill Crew store. Unisex gym and active wear apparel, shipped from us. Proceeds support suicide prevention. Everyone's got something to kill."

**Offers and CTAs:** "Open" (display ad), "Shop now" (Shopping ad, $50 Muay Thai Flame Shorts, free shipping badge), text-ad sitelinks include "Gym Shorts," "Shop Womens," "Shop Mens," "Unisex Tees & Shorts," "Womens Matching Sets," "Customer Login," "Ships From The U.S.A.," "Womens Matching Sets," "Shop Mens," "Shop Womens." One text ad cites "Rating for killcrew.co 4.9 ★★★★★ (10)" and "10,000+ verified reviews at 5 stars. True to size, XS to 5XL, free shipping over $75." Another cites "10,000+ 5 Stars."

**Visual approach:** Display/image ads use flat-lay product photography (colorful shorts arranged in a fan) and a plain black-and-pink brand-forward layout with bold white/dark text on a pink background for the "Shop Kill Crew Apparel" unit. The Shopping ad uses a single product photo (black shorts with pink floral/wave print) on a plain background. Video ad thumbnail shows a similar colorful shorts flat-lay in a vertical/mobile video frame. Text ads follow standard Google search ad formatting (blue headline link, green/gray URL, black description, blue sitelink pills).

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Meta ads captured (Ads 1-3) all center on a single specific product story (reptile-print shorts set for Ads 1-2, floral mid-thigh shorts for Ad 3) with a "Fit and Comfort 100/10" tagline and Shop Now CTA. Google Ads show a broader, more varied set of messaging angles not seen in the Meta creative — notably the mental-health/self-care brand positioning ("Selfcare 101," "inner victory over anxiety, fear, depression...") and specific review-count/rating claims ("4.9 ★★★★★ (10)," "10,000+ verified reviews at 5 stars," "10,000+ 5 Stars") that do not appear in the three Meta ad units collected. The Meta ads also don't reference "squat proof" or "doesn't ride up" as headline claims the way several Google text ads do, though Ad 3's landing page description does use similar squat-proof/chafe-proof language.

# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png
**Ads visible:** Mix of shopping ads, display/image ads, search text ads, and video thumbnail ads.

**Headline themes:**
- "Sharland England by Louise Roe | Homewares & Accessories"
- "Timeless Design - Save 10% On Your First Order"
- "Rattan Or Wicker? What's the Difference? | Sharland England"
- Shopping product titles: Splatter Verona Vase, Juliette Coffee Table, Juliette Loveseat, Pimlico Bamboo Side Table, Splatter Large Bowl, Rattan Placemat Set of 4, Fraises Des Bois Tablecloth, Splatter Verona Vase (large)

**Offers and CTAs:**
- "Save 10% On Your First Order" — 10% off appears in the search ad headline and body ("10% Off Your First Order · Rustic Rattan Interiors")
- Shopping ads show GBP prices directly in the ad unit [Price] label
- Display ads use "Open" as the CTA
- "Rattan Or Wicker?" ad leads with educational content with no visible discount

**Ad copy details (search ads):**
- Brand search: "We believe in meaningful design to create beautiful items that last. Enjoy long lunches in the garden with our beautiful garden furniture collection. Welcome to Sharland England, a collection of handmade wicker and antique homewares, by Louise Roe. More coming soon"
- Timeless Design: "A collection of timeless design. Every piece is carefully hand-painted or hand-embroidered. 10% Off Your First Order · Rustic Rattan Interiors"
- Educational: "Create A Cosy Country Interior With Luxurious Decor & Indoor Rattan Furniture. Modern Rustic Furniture & Decor From British Homeware Brand Sharland England. Rattan and wicker are often used interchangeably, but they are actually not the same..."

**Visual approach:**
- Display ads: Branded lifestyle photography (consistent with site). "Sharland England By Louise Roe / Luxurious British Home Decor & Rattan" copy. Dark/warm toned imagery.
- Shopping ads: Mix of clean product-on-white and lifestyle shots. Consistent with site product imagery.
- A competing display ad visible ("Mugs, Plates, Vases & More - Handmade By Italian Artisans") appearing alongside Sharland England ads in the same SERP — potential competitive ad showing in brand search results.

**Gaps vs. Meta:**
- Google leads more explicitly with "10% off" in search ad headlines; Meta ad 1 mentions it but buries it in ad copy. Meta ads 2 and 3 don't use the 10% offer at all.
- Google includes educational content targeting ("Rattan Or Wicker?") not present in Meta.
- Google shopping shows prices in GBP; US visitors clicking from Meta's "We're Now in the US" ad would still land on GBP-priced pages.
- "Ships Globally" and US-specific messaging absent from Google ads visible — Ad 3's US shipping angle appears Meta-only.

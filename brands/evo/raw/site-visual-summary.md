## Homepage
**Fold 1:** Sitewide top banner: "LABOR DAY SALE - UP TO 50% OFF!" (red bar, links to sale). Sticky nav below: logo, search bar, utility links ("Book a Trip," "Visit our Hotels," "Reserve a Rental," "Service Your Gear," "Join Membership," "Explore evo"), main category nav (Ski, Snowboard, Bike, Trail, Wake & Surf, Skate, Travel, Mens, Womens, Kids, Only at evo, Brands, Sale), and right-side utility icons (Ask an Expert, Find a Store, Sign In, cart). Hero is a full-width lifestyle photo (two people with mountain bikes on a grassy hillside) overlaid with large sale graphic: "UP TO 50% OFF — SUMMER & SNOW GEAR."
**Fold 2:** "Shop Labor Day Sale" section heading with a 5-tile category carousel (Bike Sale, Ski Sale, Snowboard Sale, Wake Sale, Clothing Sale), each tile a lifestyle action photo with a text label overlay.
**Fold 3:** "Labor Day Sale Top Picks" product carousel (6 products shown: wake vest, ski bindings, wakesurf board, helmet, ski bindings, snowboard boots) each with product image, color/size swatches, title, sale price with strikethrough original, and star rating with review count. Below that, a "Brands We Love" logo carousel (Arc'teryx, Smith, Santa Cruz, Patagonia, K2, and more) shown as branded lifestyle image tiles.
**CTA behavior:** No sticky CTA bar or button visible on the homepage itself; the sticky element present is the top nav/search bar, not a purchase CTA. "Shop Labor Day Sale" and "Shop New Arrivals" are inline pill-shaped buttons, not fixed/sticky.
**Trust signals:** Star ratings with numeric review counts appear on individual product tiles in the carousels (e.g., 4.5★ (485), 5★ (3), 4.5★ (59)). No sitewide guarantee, shipping threshold, or badge banner visible in folds 1-3 outside of the Labor Day discount messaging.
**Layout notes:** Heavy use of horizontal product/category carousels with left/right arrow navigation controls. A live chat widget bubble is fixed in the bottom-right corner across all folds.

**AOV elements:** "You May Also Like" product recommendation carousel inside the drawer, showing 2+ alternate jackets with image, title, and price — no bundle, cross-sell discount, or free-shipping-threshold progress bar visible.

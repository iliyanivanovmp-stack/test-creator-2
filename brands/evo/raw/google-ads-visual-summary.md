# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png (Google Ads Transparency Center results for evo / Evolucion Innovations Inc., all listed "Verified")

**Ads visible:** 20+ ad units captured across both screenshots, spanning multiple formats: text search ads, Shopping-style product ads (with rating/price/image), Local/Maps ads (store listings with Call/Directions buttons), Discovery/Display image and video ads, and placeholder units showing the generic `<Rating (Reviews)> <Category> <Open Hours> <Distance>` template (ad content not fully loaded/rendered in the transparency tool).

**Headline themes:** Recurring themes: (1) category/product-specific search ads — "Round Fit Ski Helmets," "Arc'teryx Jackets for Women," "How to Wash a Sleeping Bag," "Salomon Genesis Shoes," "Women's Five Ten Sleuth Shoes," "Atomic Hawx Ultra XTD 130 BOA GW Ski Boots Review"; (2) broad sale/offer search ads — "Up To 50% Off 2025 Gear - evo snow Pant Sale," "Authentic reviews - Shop at evo today"; (3) store/local ads for evo Portland and evo Denver rental/retail locations; (4) video/display creative with lifestyle imagery and short taglines — "GET TRAIL READY," "Find Your Fit," "NOMAD 7 BIKE REVIEW," "One Day & Multi-Day Rentals."

**Offers and CTAs:** Explicit offers seen: "Up To 50% Off 2025 Gear," "Enjoy Fast FREE SHIPPING on qualifying orders," "Lowest Price Guarantee," "We Beat Prices by 5%," "Free Shipping, Low Price Guarantee" (sleeping bag ad), "366 Day No Hassle Returns" (Salomon Genesis Shoes ad), "Best selection and authentic reviews" (Arc'teryx ad). Local ads use Call / Directions CTAs; search ads rely on the headline link as the CTA (no explicit "Shop Now" button visible in text ads, unlike Meta).

**Visual approach:** Local/store ads show interior photos of evo Portland and evo Denver retail locations with star ratings (4.5–4.8) and review counts. Display/video ads use moody outdoor lifestyle photography (skier in goggles, mountain bike trail, textured hat close-up) with minimal white sans-serif text overlay and the evo logo badge in a top corner. Shopping-style ad shows a single product image (Santa Cruz 5010 bike) with price and rating pulled from feed data.

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Google ads lead with price/guarantee-driven copy (50% off, lowest price guarantee, free shipping, beat prices by 5%) and explicit review/return-policy claims in the ad text itself. Meta ads carry generic seasonal lifestyle copy ("Looking for adventure?", "Make your powder dreams come true...") without stating price, discount percentage, or guarantee terms in the ad copy — those details only appear once the user reaches the landing page. Google search ads name specific brands and products in headlines (Arc'teryx, Salomon, Atomic) more consistently than the three Meta ads reviewed, which reference specific products only in the link title/body, not the headline.

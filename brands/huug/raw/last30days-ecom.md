## Cross-Source Signal

The "no removable foam pads / sewn-in pads" and "machine washable / dryer safe" claims appear consistently across TikTok, Instagram, and the Meta ad creatives already collected — this is the brand's core repeated differentiator across every channel, not just paid social. Separately, the nurse/scrubs/shift-work angle used in Meta Ads #1 and #2 does not appear in any of the organic TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube content found here — it looks like a paid-only messaging test rather than an established organic brand angle.

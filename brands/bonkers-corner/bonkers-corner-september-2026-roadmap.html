The brand is visually distinct, but the buying journey tells different offer stories and withholds trust until late. This roadmap makes the promise consistent, brings reassurance into decision moments, and removes costly friction from mobile shopping.

Eight focused moves

Each card shows the customer-facing change and the storefront moment it improves.

Slot 1A/B Test

One Discount Offer

Paid-traffic landing pages and PDP offer box

Offer-focused traffic arrives with one code or percentage, while the landing page rotates SHARKTANK10, EMI, and UPI savings. The promise disappears at the exact moment the shopper evaluates price. The variation carries one fixed SHARKTANK10 offer from arrival through the PDP.

* One SHARKTANK10 promise remains visible from arrival to product decision. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2Immediate Fix

Mobile Load Speed

Homepage and Product Detail Page

Mobile LCP is 39.0 seconds on the homepage and 49.2 seconds on the PDP, and both Lighthouse runs timed out before completing. Slow rendering undercuts every paid visit and every test that follows. The fix prioritizes the first campaign image and buy box so the shopping surface appears promptly.

* The campaign surface and shopping controls load before secondary scripts and below-fold content. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3A/B Test

90-Day Guarantee

Rev It Up Oversized T-shirt PDP

The collected Rev It Up PDP has no guarantee badge, while other observed PDPs place the existing 90-day guarantee directly under price. That makes reassurance depend on which product a shopper opens. The variation brings the same guarantee badge to the standard PDP buy box.

* The existing 90-day guarantee moves directly under price on the standard PDP. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4A/B Test

Homepage Trust Strip

Homepage

The first three homepage folds show campaign photography and Shop Now buttons, while existing shipping and Made in India copy sits much farther down. The opening experience asks imagery to carry all credibility. The variation moves those two existing trust statements directly beneath the first hero.

* Existing Made in India and 48-hour shipping copy moves into the first homepage decision window. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

Cart Offer Clarity

Cart drawer

The cart drawer stacks SHARKTANK10, a GOBONKERS15 tier bar, and a prepaid discount beneath Checkout, with no trust statement. Three savings mechanics compete at the last decision point. The variation keeps the tiered cart-value offer and replaces the other messages with the existing 90-day guarantee.

* Competing cart discounts are reduced to the tiered offer, while the 90-day guarantee takes the freed space. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6A/B Test

PDP Color Swatches

Rev It Up Oversized T-shirt PDP

The collected standard PDP offers sizes only, while other observed product pages show three to five color swatches beside size. Standard products hide a browsing pattern that already exists elsewhere in the store. The variation adds a clear color row above the size selector and updates the product image when a swatch is chosen.

* A color row joins the existing size selector and changes the product view when selected. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7A/B Test

Sticky Add to Cart

All Product Detail Pages

Across every observed PDP, Add to Cart stays inside the initial buy box while fabric and style content continues below. The purchase action leaves view as shoppers explore product details. The variation keeps product, price, and Add to Cart fixed at the bottom after the primary buy box scrolls away.

* Product, price, and Add to Cart remain available while the shopper reads style details. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8A/B Test

Category Price Anchor

Homepage category tiles

Homepage category tiles show lifestyle imagery and Shop Now with no price context, while paid search already uses starting-price language. The on-site effect is not yet proven, so this is the roadmap’s lowest-confidence test. The variation adds a consistent “Starting at ₹999” anchor without changing the campaign imagery or CTA.

* A starting-price anchor adds context to the existing category imagery and Shop Now action. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  • 01At least five discount framings appear across acquisition, PDP, and cart, with no single offer staying consistent through the journey.

  • 03The first three homepage folds and the cart drawer show no reviews, guarantee, or shipping reassurance. Guarantee copy also changes between PDPs.

What the Data Shows

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Offer-focused creative promises NEW15, 10% prepaid, or an extra 5% prepaid saving. The destination pages instead rotate SHARKTANK10, EMI, and UPI discounts.
  • Guarantee treatment varies by product. Two observed landing pages show the 90-day guarantee below price, while another does not.
  • Observed review counts range from 1 to 12, and every observed Add to Cart remains static inside the buy box.

Google Ads

  • Search and display messaging introduces “Starting At ₹999 Only,” “5% off on Prepaid Orders,” and product prices from ₹299.00.
  • “As Seen on Shark Tank India” appears repeatedly in search messaging, while paid-social creative uses other offer angles.

Page Speed Insights

  • Homepage mobile performance is 33/100, with 39.0-second LCP, 1,020 ms Total Blocking Time, and 48.8-second Time to Interactive.
  • PDP mobile performance is 37/100, with 49.2-second LCP, 750 ms Total Blocking Time, and 53.2-second Time to Interactive.
  • Both runs were too slow for Lighthouse to complete. CLS is 0 on the homepage and 0.001 on the PDP.

Site Screenshots

  • The first three homepage folds are dominated by campaign photography and Shop Now buttons. Trust content appears farther down the live page.
  • The standard Rev It Up PDP shows ₹999, a ₹1,199 compare-at price, 17% savings, 2 reviews, and sizes from XS to XXL, but no guarantee badge or color swatches.
  • The cart drawer combines SHARKTANK10, GOBONKERS15 spend tiers, and a prepaid savings line beneath Checkout. No guarantee, returns, or trust badge is visible.
  • No actual product-listing grid was captured, so collection-page findings cannot be evaluated from this data set.

Competitor Research

  • Bonkers Corner sits in the observed ₹999 to ₹2,999 mid-premium D2C streetwear bracket.
  • The Souled Store competes through licensed-IP catalog depth, while Snitch is the closer mid-premium comparison.

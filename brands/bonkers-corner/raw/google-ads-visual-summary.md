# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png (Google Ads Transparency Center grid, advertiser "Bad Brains Streetstyle Private Limited" / Bonkers Corner, Verified)

**Ads visible:** Roughly 25+ ad units across both screenshots — a mix of Search text ads, Shopping/product ads, Display banner ads, and short video/carousel ads. Several tiles show "Sorry, we're not able to show you this ad at this time" or "500. That's an error" placeholders (ad creative not renderable in the transparency tool).

**Headline themes:**
- Value/price anchoring: "Starting At ₹999 Only – Comfort meets crazy," "Starting At ₹999 Only – Namma Fit. Namma Style," "5% off on Prepaid Orders – Comfort meets crazy"
- Shark Tank social proof: "As Seen on Shark Tank India – Bonkers Corner Is Trending," "As Seen on Shark Tank India" (repeated across multiple ad units)
- Category/product intent: "Buy Joggers for Mens Online in India – Bonkers Corner," "Shop Bonkers - Trendy Styles - Joggers for Every Mood," "Buy Official Spiderman Merch Online | Bonkers Corner India"
- Brand attitude/positioning: "Unapologetic, By Bonkers - Built To Be Noticed" with body copy "Graphic tees that talk back. Oversized fits, loud prints, zero filter."
- Local/regional targeting: "Namma Fit. Namma Style" (Bengaluru/Kannada-market framing), "Clothing store - Bengaluru - Brigade Road" listing.
- Licensed merch angle: Spiderman/Marvel collection callouts ("Buy Official Spiderman Merch," "Marvel" nav link).

**Offers and CTAs:** "Extra 5% Off Prepaid" (Drift Collection display banner), "SHARKTANK10 at checkout... Save 10%," "5% off on Prepaid Orders," starting price call-outs (₹999, ₹299.00). CTAs seen: "Open," "Visit site," "Shop Now," "View 3 prices from ₹299.00" / "View 3 prices from ₹999.00."

**Visual approach:** Display/video ads use dark, moody product photography (models in streetwear, close crops) similar to the Meta ad creative style. One banner ("DRIFT" collection) uses a stylized night-driving visual with bold "Extra 5% Off Prepaid" overlay text. Video tiles show short lifestyle clips (group of models "LIVE NOW," a solo model walking). Shopping-style ad shows a single product card (Black Nirvana Oversized Tank) with price/rating placeholder fields.

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Google ads use several distinct discount figures (5% prepaid, 10% via SHARKTANK10, starting-price anchors of ₹999/₹299) that don't consistently match any single Meta ad's stated offer (15% off, 10% off prepaid, extra 5% prepaid). The Shark Tank angle is far more prominent and explicit in Google Search ad copy than in the Meta ad captions reviewed, though it does appear as a persistent site-wide announcement bar on the landing pages.

# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** `google-ads-1.png`, `google-ads-2.png` (Google Ads Transparency Center grid for "Swanson Health Products, Incorporated")

**Ads visible:** Roughly 24 ad units shown across both screenshots. Mix of formats: text/search ads with sitelinks, product/shopping listing ads (image + price + rating placeholder + "By Google"), video ads (product demo and testimonial-style talking-head clips), and at least one display banner. One tile shows "Sorry, we're not able to show you this ad at this time."

**Headline themes:** Recurring angles include: sleep/stress/mood support ("Support Better Sleep," "Total Well Being Support," "Feeling Stressed Or Sleepless?"), digestive/gut health ("Order Gut Health Supplements," "Looking For Help With Digestion?"), general vitamins/supplements breadth ("Vitamins & Supplements - Shop Swanson Vitamins," "Since 1969... quality vitamins & supplements"), recovery/hydration and electrolytes ("Support Recovery Hydration - Hydrate After Exercise Fast"), energy/metabolism ("Support Energy & Metabolism - Berberine Metabolic Support"), and brand-trust/heritage framing ("Since 1969," "Behind the Scenes at Swanson Vitamins," "Real wellness starts here"). Video ads use founder/expert and customer-testimonial formats (e.g., "I stay consistent and I can tell..." with a supplement bottle; "3 types of trending magnesium in 30 seconds").

**Offers and CTAs:** Visible offers include "35% off SHP + 2X Rewards," "Save up to 40% on your first order," and free/return-policy callouts ("Return policy: Most items 100+ days"). Text ads carry sitelink CTAs such as "Shop Swanson Bestsellers," "Shop Our Best Sellers," "Subscribe & Save," "Shop Official Site." Shopping-style tiles show "[Price]," "[Rating (Reviews)]," and "By Google" placeholders (actual values not rendered in the transparency-center capture).

**Visual approach:** Shopping ads use plain product-on-white bottle/jar photography (e.g., Ceylon cinnamon, B-Complex, Astaxanthin, Lithium Orotate, Psyllium Husk, CuraMed turmeric) similar to marketplace listing style. Text ads follow the standard Google search-ad layout (blue headline, green URL, description, star rating line, sitelinks in a list). Video ads are shot in informal, handheld/selfie or in-office documentary style (talking directly to camera, product in hand, or behind-the-scenes lab/facility footage) rather than polished studio production.

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Meta ad creatives (probiotics, k2o hydration, Mellow Magnesium) are polished lifestyle/product photography with a single clear CTA ("Shop now") and consistent "100% Money Back Guarantee" messaging on 2 of 3 ads. The Google Ads set is broader and more heterogeneous — covering sleep, digestion, energy, recovery, and general brand/heritage messaging rather than the specific SKUs seen in the Meta set — and leans more heavily on video/testimonial formats and discount-code offers (35% off, 40% off) that don't appear in the Meta creatives reviewed.

Conversion testing roadmap

September 2026

Swanson has strong product proof and compelling value, but several buying journeys hide that value behind slow loads, blocked collection pages, or information placed away from the decision. This roadmap brings products, proof, and useful next steps into view when shoppers need them.

Testing Roadmap

Eight focused opportunities across paid landing pages, product detail, performance, homepage trust, and cart.

Slot 1Immediate Fix

Collection Age Gate

Pages: k2o Hydration and Mellow Magnesium collections

The k2o Hydration and Mellow Magnesium collection pages open behind an 18+ verification gate before shoppers can see products or the money-back promise. That blocks the experience paid traffic came to explore. Remove the gate on these non-restricted collections so products, prices, and reassurance appear immediately.

The collection opens on real products and the promised guarantee instead of an age gate. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Subscription Price Clarity

Page: Triple Magnesium Complex

The audit capture showed both purchase options at $23.19 despite the 40% subscription claim. The live page now shows $13.91 for Subscribe to Save and $23.19 for one-time purchase, resolving the core contradiction. Preserve this visible price difference and full benefit stack throughout the purchase flow.

The live purchase options now turn the 40% claim into a visible price difference with the full subscriber benefit stack intact. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3Performance Test

PDP Load Time

Page: Triple Magnesium Complex

The same PDP appears with its primary product media and purchase controls prioritized for first paint. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Page: Triple Magnesium Complex

The combined 400mg total gains a clear three-form breakdown after the exact formulation values are confirmed. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  • Two paid-traffic collection pages hide the product grid and money-back promise behind an 18+ verification gate.
  • The Triple Magnesium PDP recorded a 12.2-second LCP, while the homepage recorded 7.2 seconds. Both pages also exceeded 1.6 seconds of Total Blocking Time.
  • Ingredient-ratio transparency is the most repeated complaint in the Triple Magnesium review sample, with at least 3 of 22 reviews asking for or criticizing the missing split.

What the Data Shows

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Probiotic traffic reaches a complete collection with science-backed badges, a hero product, and a seven-product grid.
  • k2o Hydration and Mellow Magnesium traffic reaches an 18+ gate before products or the guarantee claim appear.

Google Ads

  • The reviewed creative spans sleep, digestion, energy, recovery, hydration, and brand heritage.
  • Discount-led messaging varies across the set, while the money-back guarantee used in paid-social creative does not carry through consistently.

Reviews and UGC

  • At least 3 of 22 Triple Magnesium reviews ask for or criticize the missing oxide, citrate, and glycinate ratio.
  • Customers value the multi-source formula, long-term repeat use, and US-based manufacturing.
  • Some reviewers describe a mismatch between the blended formula and the single-purpose effect they expected.

Page Speed Insights

  • The homepage recorded a 0.33 performance score, 7.2-second LCP, and 1,750ms TBT.
  • The Triple Magnesium PDP recorded a 0.37 performance score, 12.2-second LCP, and 1,630ms TBT.
  • CLS was zero on both pages, so the reports show late loading and main-thread blocking rather than layout movement.

Competitor Analysis

  • Swanson's stated 40% first subscription discount is larger than the compared 20% and 10% programs.
  • The audit capture did not reflect that advantage in the purchase-option prices, while competitor discounts were visibly differentiated.

Social and Community Research

  • On-site and Amazon ratings found in the research run consistently ranged from 4.3 to 4.8 stars.
  • No genuine on-topic Reddit discussion was found during the research window.

Site Screenshots

  • The homepage trust badges sit below the experts hero instead of in the first fold.
  • The Triple Magnesium certification row sits below the image gallery, away from price and purchase options.
  • The cart drawer shows a $25.81 free-shipping gap with no product suggestion to help close it.

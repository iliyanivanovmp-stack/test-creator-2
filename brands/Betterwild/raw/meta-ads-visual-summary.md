# Meta Ads Visual Summary

Note: All three ads share the same landing page (betterwild.com/pages/lf). Landing page folds are described once and referenced for all three ads.

## Ad 1
**Creative:** Influencer video ad. Creator "The Downtown Aly" with BetterWild (influencer partnership). Copy opens with dog symptom list (itchy paws, redness, eating grass, head shaking, behavior change) and "You need to fix this one problem." Video still shows subtitle: "In a week and a half, I noticed that she stopped itching." Headline: "Public Service Announcement From A Terrified Dog." CTA: Learn More. Running on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Threads since Jun 9, 2026.

**Landing Page Fold 1:** Announcement bar: "Subscribe today and get free US shipping on every subscription order!" Nav: SHOP, FAQ, QUALITY, LOGIN. Star rating + "100,000+ Happy Dogs" above headline. Headline: "ALLERGY RELIEF THAT FIXES THE ROOT CAUSE, NOT JUST THE SYMPTOMS." Subhead: "Support your dog's immunity and ease allergies—naturally. A blend of clinically validated ingredients!" CTA: "SHOP NOW" (pink button). Hero image: two dogs outdoors. Section header partially visible below: "SIGNS YOUR DOG NEEDS ALLERGY CHEWS."

**Landing Page Fold 2:** Section: "SIGNS YOUR DOG NEEDS ALLERGY CHEWS" with intro: "Dogs can't say they're uncomfortable—but their actions and skin can. Watch for allergy signs and act early to restore comfort." Sticky/in-section "SHOP NOW" button visible top right. Five symptom image cards: ITCHING, SCRATCHING, PAW LICKING, SKIN BITING, RED SKIN. Next section begins: "WHAT IF YOUR DOG'S ALLERGIES WERE ITS BODY CRYING OUT FOR HELP?"

**Landing Page Fold 3:** Left: product dog image with gut bacteria diagram overlaid. Right: three content blocks — "Your Dog's Body Still Speaks 'Wolf'" (wolf-sourced probiotics), "Rebuilding the Gut Nature Intended," "Relief That Starts at the Root." CTA: "LEARN MORE" (pink button). Below CTA: star rating + "100,000+ Happy Dogs."

**LP CTA:** "SHOP NOW" in fold 1 and fold 2 (sticky bar visible top right on fold 2). "LEARN MORE" in fold 3. No sticky CTA bar on mobile confirmed — the SHOP NOW in fold 2 appears to be a section-level button, not a persistent sticky bar.

**LP trust signals:** "100,000+ Happy Dogs" with star rating visible in fold 1 and fold 3. No review count, no price, no guarantee copy visible in fold 1. No guarantee visible above the first CTA. Guarantee and review count not visible until deeper in the page (not collected in these 3 folds).

**Message Match:** Ad 1 names specific symptoms (itchy paws, redness, head shaking, behavior change). LP headline pivots to "root cause, not just symptoms" — directionally aligned but does not echo the specific symptom language from the ad copy. The LP does show a symptom grid in fold 2 (itching, scratching, paw licking, skin biting, red skin) which partially reinforces ad messaging. No continuation of the influencer narrative or "PSA" framing on the LP.

Product jar (red BetterWild container) held in front of French Bulldog. Headline: "This Will Change Your Dog's Life." CTA: Learn More. Running on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger since Jun 9, 2026.

**Landing Page Fold 1–3:** Same as Ad 1 above.

**LP CTA:** Same as Ad 1.

**LP trust signals:** Same as Ad 1.

**Message Match:** Ad 2 leads with a rescue/foster dog story and vet care + allergy prevention framing. LP does not continue rescue or foster narrative. "This Will Change Your Dog's Life" headline is more outcome-focused than the LP's "Root Cause" positioning. Broadly aligned on allergy topic; no narrative continuity.

## Ad 3
**Creative:** Branded video ad. Sender: BetterWild brand page (not influencer). Copy: "I'm on a mission to make a real difference in the lives of our dogs. And after years of seeing how animals thrive in nature, and seeing what most modern pet products are missing…" Video thumbnail shows founder (Cody) speaking to camera. Video caption: "BetterWild Founder Exposes Big Secret" and "Hey, I'm Cody." Headline: "BetterWild Founder Exposes Big Secret." CTA: Learn more. Running on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Threads since Jun 10, 2026.

**Landing Page Fold 1–3:** Same as Ad 1 above.

**LP CTA:** Same as Ad 1.

**LP trust signals:** Same as Ad 1.

**Message Match:** Ad 3 builds a "founder exposes secret" narrative with a personal story hook. The LP has no founder reference, no "secret" reveal, and no continuation of the Cody story. This is the largest message match gap across the three ads — the LP is a generic allergy product page that does not deliver on the intrigue promised in the ad.

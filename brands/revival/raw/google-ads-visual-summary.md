# Google Ads Visual Summary

**Screenshots reviewed:** google-ads-1.png, google-ads-2.png
**Ads visible:** Approximately 20+ ad units across search results, shopping/product listings, an Amazon listing, a display banner, and a video thumbnail. Formats: text search ads (majority), shopping-style cards with product images, one Amazon marketplace sponsored listing, one image/display ad, one video ad thumbnail ("RAPID ACTION REHYDRATION").
**Headline themes:** Three recurring angles: (1) Category authority — "UK's No 1 Rehydration Powder," "UK's #1 Hydration Brand," "No 1 Best Seller In The UK," "UK's Most Reviewed Hydration Sachets"; (2) Hangover/morning-after recovery — "Wake Up Fresh - No Foggy Head," "Hangover Cure | How To Wake Up Fresh," "Hangover Recovery Drink - No 1 Best Seller," "Why Do I Fatigue? stop the tired feeling and wake up fresh"; (3) Informational/listicle style — "Which Drinks Contain The Most Electrolytes," "7 Reasons You Still Feel Off After a Late Night" (implied by ad3 creative, consistent with this set).
**Offers and CTAs:** "15% Off For New Customers" appears repeatedly. "100% Money Back Guarantee" / "Try risk free" appears on multiple listings. CTA buttons visible on the Amazon-style unit: "Get 15% Off," "Shop Now," "Best Way To Stop..." One listing shows a promo code reference ("15% off New Customers, Code WELCO...").
**Visual approach:** Shopping/product cards use packshots of the stick pack boxes with fruit garnish (cherries, orange, apple) consistent with Meta ad and site imagery. One display ad uses a lifestyle photo (product + drink glass) with "Awarded Best Hydration Powder" badge overlay. Video thumbnail uses bold white-on-black text "RAPID ACTION REHYDRATION."
**Gaps vs. Meta:** Google Ads lean heavily into "UK's No.1 / Best Seller" authority claims and informational/listicle framing (hangover cures, electrolyte comparisons) that don't appear as prominently in the Meta creative set. The hangover/late-night recovery angle is consistent with Meta Ad 1 and Ad 3. The travel angle from Meta Ad 2 has no counterpart in the Google Ads screenshots.

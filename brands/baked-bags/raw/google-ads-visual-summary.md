**Offers and CTAs:** No specific discount/price called out in any of the three ads; all use generic "shop now" framing.

**Visual approach:** All three are text-based search ads (no display/shopping imagery). Two show the bakedbags.com domain with a Baked Bags logo icon; format is standard Google search ad card with headline, description, and sitelinks.

**Verified advertiser names shown:** The three cards list different verified advertiser names below each ad — "Asaduk haswe," "HANH PHUC COMMUNICATI...," and "RICHFIELD ELECTRONICS LIMITED" — none of which match "Baked Bags" as the verified entity name.

**Gaps vs. Meta:** Meta ads lead with specific pricing and percentage-off offers ($39.99, 33% off, 50% off). Google search ad copy collected here has no pricing or discount callouts, relying on generic "shop now" and lifestyle language instead.

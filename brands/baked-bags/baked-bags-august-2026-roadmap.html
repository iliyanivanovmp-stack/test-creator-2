Baked Bags CRO Testing Roadmap

August 2026

Paid traffic reaches pages that load slowly and sometimes lose the promise that earned the click. This roadmap fixes the performance foundation first, then makes the CONED purchase path clearer, easier to act on, and closer to the language customers already use.

Testing Roadmap

Eight focused ideas, sequenced from urgent performance repairs to clearer offers, safer product guidance, persistent purchase access, and sharper paid-search messaging.

Slot 1Immediate Fix

PDP Core Web Vitals

CONED Variety Pack PDP

The CONED PDP records an 18.5-second LCP and 53.3-second Time to Interactive on mobile. A shopper can reach the buy box before the page responds to taps. The fixed experience prioritizes the product, price, selection controls, and Add to Cart action so the buying surface becomes usable first.

* The essential buy box becomes usable before secondary page content. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 2Immediate Fix

Homepage Core Web Vitals

Homepage

The homepage records an 8.1-second LCP and 49.2-second Time to Interactive on mobile. The main product promise and Shop Legal THC action can appear before the page is ready to respond. The fixed experience prioritizes the hero product image, headline, and CTA before the product grids and secondary modules.

* The homepage hero and primary CTA load before secondary merchandising. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 3A/B Test

CONED Subscription Option

CONED Variety Pack PDP

Subscription-focused paid traffic reaches a landing page and CONED buy box with no matching subscription purchase option in the collected screenshots. The promise disappears at the decision point. The variation adds a clear subscribe-and-save choice with free shipping and broader subscriber-benefit language beside the existing one-time and Bundle & Save options.

* A subscription choice carries the paid-traffic promise into the CONED buy box. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 4A/B Test

Serving-Size Callout

CONED Variety Pack PDP

The buy box lists potency and pack totals but does not state serving size in plain language. One verified customer ate an entire cone after reading the serving cue as “1.” The variation adds “1 Cone = 1 Serving (25MG)” in the existing benefit list at the point of purchase.

* Plain-language serving guidance joins the existing potency bullets. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 5A/B Test

Homepage Countdown Position

Homepage

The federal-ban countdown currently appears before the hero headline and primary offer. It asks for urgency before the shopper sees the product value. The variation keeps the exact banner copy but moves it beneath the headline, subhead, and Shop Legal THC button.

* Product value and the primary CTA lead; the existing countdown follows. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 6A/B Test

Sticky Add to Cart

CONED Variety Pack PDP

The primary Add to Cart button sits inline below the bundle selector, and no persistent CTA appears in the three collected PDP folds. Shoppers who continue into descriptions and reviews lose direct access to purchase. The variation keeps a compact product thumbnail, $39.99 price, and Add to Cart action visible after the buy box scrolls away.

* A compact purchase bar stays visible through product details and reviews. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 7A/B Test

Ice-Cream Pairing

CONED Variety Pack PDP

At least three of 20 collected CONED reviews independently mention pairing the cones with real ice cream, but that use case does not appear in the collected ads, landing pages, or PDP. The variation brings that customer-led idea into the buy box with the line “Customer favorite: pair with real ice cream.”

* Customer language turns an organic ice-cream pairing into a visible product use case. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Slot 8Paid Media A/B Test

Google Search Offer Copy

Google Search Ads

The reviewed search ads use generic “shop now” framing and show no price or discount. Offer-focused paid creative uses specific numbers such as $39.99 and 33% off. The variation tests that same pricing clarity in search with “4 Flavors for $39.99” and “Save 33%.”

* Generic search copy becomes a specific CONED price-and-savings offer. Concept illustration only. Final design will differ.

Biggest Killers of Conversion Rate

  • The homepage takes 49.2 seconds and the CONED PDP takes 53.3 seconds to become interactive on mobile. This performance problem sits beneath every other funnel stage.
  • Subscription-focused paid traffic reaches landing pages and a CONED buy box with no visible subscription or rewards purchase option.
  • A verified customer over-consumed after the serving-size cue was unclear, while the PDP buy box states potency and pack totals without saying “1 cone = 1 serving.”

What the Data Shows

Meta Ads and Landing Pages

  • Subscription-focused creative promises “10+ tiers of free gifts, surprise rewards and exclusive perks,” but the destination page has no subscription or rewards mention in the collected folds.
  • Offer-focused traffic promising “Save 33% on Cones Variety Packs. 4 flavors for $39.99” reaches a landing page with matching “SAVE 33%” and $39.99 copy.
  • The collected landing pages use the same press bar and customer quote block, and neither shows a sticky CTA in the captured folds.

Google Ads

  • The reviewed search creative uses generic “shop now” framing without a price or discount, while paid-social creative leads with $39.99, 33% off, and 50% off.
  • The Transparency Center screenshot shows verified advertiser names including “Asaduk haswe,” “HANH PHUC COMMUNICATI...,” and “RICHFIELD ELECTRONICS LIMITED.” None matches Baked Bags.

Reviews and UGC

  • Potency is the dominant theme across the 20 verified CONED reviews.
  • At least three customers independently describe pairing the cones with real ice cream.
  • A verified customer reported eating an entire cone after reading the serving size as “1,” then being “zonked for the rest of the day.”
  • Customers repeatedly call out the variety-pack format as a purchase driver.

PageSpeed Insights

  • The homepage scores 0.34 for performance, with 8.1s LCP, 790ms TBT, 0.153 CLS, and 49.2s Time to Interactive.
  • The CONED PDP scores 0.39 for performance, with 18.5s LCP, 690ms TBT, 0.002 CLS, and 53.3s Time to Interactive.
  • Google’s “good” LCP threshold is 2.5 seconds. The PDP is roughly seven times above it.

Site Screenshots

  • The homepage places the federal-ban countdown above “THE LEADING CAUSE OF RED EYES” and the Shop Legal THC CTA.
  • The CONED buy box shows one-time purchase and quantity-based Bundle & Save, with no visible subscription option.
  • The main Add to Cart button is inline, and no persistent CTA appears in the collected PDP folds.
  • The cart drawer already includes a free-shipping progress bar, an inline quantity prompt, product cross-sells, Rush Order, a discount field, and Sezzle installments.

Competitor Analysis

  • Delta Munchies shows a live subscribe-and-save offer with 15% off, free shipping, and pause or edit controls.
  • Diamond CBD offers subscriptions across product lines and free shipping over $99.
  • Kanha did not show a direct-to-consumer subscription and primarily uses dispensary or delivery distribution.
